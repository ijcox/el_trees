English Walnut Tree
The English Walnut Tree (Juglans regia), also called Persian Walnut, is a large deciduous tree native to southeastern Europe and western Asia. It is cultivated worldwide for its edible nuts and high-quality wood.

English Walnut trees grow 20–30 meters tall with a broad, rounded crown. They thrive in full sun and well-drained soils. The tree produces edible walnuts encased in a thin husk and pinnately compound leaves. English Walnut wood is valued for furniture, cabinetry, and veneer.

Characteristics of English Walnut Trees

  • Deciduous tree with pinnately compound leaves
  • Produces edible walnuts in thin husks
  • Broad, rounded crown and straight trunk
  • Moderate growth rate, long-lived
  • Native to southeastern Europe and western Asia

Common Uses

  • Edible nuts for culinary use
  • Timber for furniture, cabinetry, and veneer
  • Ornamental planting in parks and large gardens
  • Wildlife habitat and food source for birds and mammals
  • Shade tree in orchards and landscapes
