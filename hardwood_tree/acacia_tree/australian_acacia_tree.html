Australian Acacia Tree
The Australian Acacia Tree (Acacia spp.) is a large genus of shrubs and trees native to Australia. It is valued for its durable wood, fragrant flowers, and adaptability to various soils.

Australian Acacia trees grow 5–20 meters tall depending on the species. They have pinnately or bipinnately compound leaves and produce yellow, fragrant, globular flowers. The tree thrives in well-drained soils with full sun and is drought-tolerant.

Characteristics of Australian Acacia Trees

  • Pinnately or bipinnately compound leaves
  • Yellow, fragrant, globular flowers
  • Adaptable to various soils and drought-tolerant
  • Fast-growing, medium to large size
  • Valued for durable wood and ornamental flowers
  • Native to Australia

Common Uses

  • Timber for furniture and construction
  • Ornamental planting in gardens and parks
  • Soil stabilization and erosion control
  • Source of gum and nectar for bees
  • Wildlife habitat for birds and insects
