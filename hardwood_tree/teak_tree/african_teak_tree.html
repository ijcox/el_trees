African Teak Tree
The African Teak Tree (Milicia excelsa) is a tropical hardwood tree native to West and Central Africa. It is highly valued for its durable, golden-brown timber and resistance to termites and decay.

African Teak trees grow 25–35 meters tall with a straight trunk and broad, spreading crown. They thrive in tropical, well-drained soils and full sunlight. The leaves are pinnately compound, and the tree produces small seeds.

Characteristics of African Teak Trees

  • Pinnately compound leaves
  • Straight trunk with broad, spreading crown
  • Produces small seeds
  • Durable, golden-brown wood resistant to termites and decay
  • Fast-growing and long-lived
  • Native to West and Central Africa

Common Uses

  • High-quality timber for furniture, flooring, and boat building
  • Cabinetry, veneers, and outdoor structures
  • Ornamental planting in tropical landscapes
  • Shade tree in plantations and parks
  • Wildlife habitat in tropical forests
