East Indian Rosewood Tree
The East Indian Rosewood Tree (Dalbergia latifolia) is a highly valued tropical hardwood native to India and Southeast Asia, known for its rich coloration and durability.

East Indian Rosewood trees grow up to 20–25 meters tall with a straight trunk and dense canopy. They thrive in tropical climates with well-drained soils and seasonal rainfall. This species is one of the most widely used rosewoods in modern woodworking and instrument making.

Characteristics of East Indian Rosewood Trees

  • Dark brown wood with purple or black streaks
  • Dense, strong, and durable hardwood
  • Fine to medium grain with natural luster
  • Native to India and Southeast Asia
  • Moderately fast-growing compared to other rosewoods
  • Resistant to decay and insect damage

Common Uses

  • Musical instruments (guitars, fingerboards)
  • Furniture and cabinetry
  • Flooring and veneers
  • Turned objects and carvings
  • Boat building and specialty woodwork
