Ceiba Pentandra Tree
The Ceiba Pentandra Tree is the species encompassing all Kapok varieties, native to tropical regions of the Americas, Africa, and Asia. It is renowned for its massive trunk, buttress roots, and cotton-like seed fibers.

Ceiba Pentandra trees can grow 50–70 meters tall with wide buttress roots for stability. They thrive in tropical rainforest climates with ample sunlight and well-drained soils. This species produces soft, cotton-like fibers around its seeds, widely used for stuffing, insulation, and other applications, and supports diverse tropical wildlife.

Characteristics of Ceiba Pentandra Trees

  • Extremely tall trunk with broad buttress roots
  • Thrives in tropical rainforest climates
  • Produces soft, cotton-like seed fibers
  • Supports birds, mammals, and insects
  • Fast-growing and long-lived
  • Widespread across the Americas, Africa, and Asia

Common Uses

  • Filling material for mattresses, pillows, and cushions
  • Light timber for crafts and construction
  • Ornamental planting in tropical areas
  • Wildlife habitat support
  • Traditional medicinal uses of bark and leaves
