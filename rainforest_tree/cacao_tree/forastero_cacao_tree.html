Forastero Cacao Tree
The Forastero Cacao Tree is the most widely cultivated cacao variety, known for its hardiness and high-yield cocoa production.

Forastero cacao trees (Theobroma cacao) originate from the Amazon basin and are now grown extensively across West Africa, South America, and parts of Asia. They are considered the backbone of global chocolate production due to their resilience and productivity.

Unlike fine-flavor varieties, Forastero cacao tends to have a stronger, more bitter profile but is highly valued for large-scale cocoa supply chains.

Characteristics of Forastero Cacao Trees

  • High-yield and fast-growing cacao variety
  • Hardy and disease-resistant compared to Criollo
  • Stronger, more bitter cocoa flavor profile
  • Widely cultivated in commercial plantations
  • Originates from the Amazon rainforest
  • Forms the majority of global cocoa production

Common Uses

  • Mass chocolate production
  • Cocoa powder and cocoa butter extraction
  • Industrial confectionery products
  • Blending base for commercial chocolate
  • Global cocoa supply chains
