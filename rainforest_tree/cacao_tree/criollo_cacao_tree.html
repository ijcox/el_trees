Criollo Cacao Tree
The Criollo Cacao Tree is a rare, fine-flavor cacao variety (Theobroma cacao) known for producing some of the highest quality chocolate beans in the world.

Criollo cacao trees are traditionally cultivated in shaded tropical environments across Central and South America. They are highly valued for their delicate flavor profile, low bitterness, and complex aromatic qualities.

However, Criollo trees are more sensitive to disease and environmental stress compared to other cacao varieties, making them less commonly grown.

Characteristics of Criollo Cacao Trees

  • Produces premium fine-flavor cocoa beans
  • Low bitterness and highly aromatic profile
  • More delicate and disease-sensitive than other cacao types
  • Requires shaded tropical rainforest conditions
  • Lower yield but higher quality production
  • One of the oldest cultivated cacao varieties

Common Uses

  • Luxury and artisan chocolate production
  • High-end confectionery products
  • Specialty cocoa blends
  • Gourmet chocolate markets
  • Premium cocoa-based beverages
