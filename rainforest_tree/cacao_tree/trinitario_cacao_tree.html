Trinitario Cacao Tree
The Trinitario Cacao Tree is a hybrid cacao variety combining the flavor quality of Criollo with the hardiness of Forastero.

Trinitario cacao trees (Theobroma cacao) are a naturally occurring hybrid originally developed in Trinidad after disease outbreaks affected Criollo plantations. They are now widely cultivated for their balanced flavor and improved resilience.

This variety is highly valued in premium chocolate production due to its mix of aromatic complexity and stronger disease resistance.

Characteristics of Trinitario Cacao Trees

  • Hybrid of Criollo and Forastero cacao
  • Balanced flavor profile with good aroma
  • More disease-resistant than Criollo
  • Moderate to high yield production
  • Grown in tropical plantation systems
  • Widely used in fine chocolate markets

Common Uses

  • Premium chocolate production
  • Specialty cocoa blends
  • Gourmet confectionery
  • Bean-to-bar chocolate making
  • High-quality cocoa exports
