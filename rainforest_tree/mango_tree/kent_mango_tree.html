Kent Mango Tree
The Kent Mango Tree (Mangifera indica 'Kent') is a tropical fruit tree originating from Florida, USA, renowned for its large, sweet, juicy fruit with minimal fiber.

Kent Mango trees grow up to 10–12 meters tall with a dense, spreading canopy. They thrive in tropical and subtropical climates with well-drained soils and full sunlight. This variety is popular commercially and for home gardens due to its reliable fruit production and quality.

Characteristics of Kent Mango Trees

  • Medium to large tree with dense canopy
  • Produces large, sweet, juicy fruit with minimal fiber
  • Thrives in tropical and subtropical climates
  • Moderate growth rate
  • Originated in Florida, USA
  • Reliable fruit producer for commercial and home use

Common Uses

  • Fresh consumption
  • Juices, smoothies, and desserts
  • Pickles and preserves
  • Flavoring for ice creams and confectionery
  • Home gardens and commercial orchards
