Ataulfo Mango Tree
The Ataulfo Mango Tree (Mangifera indica 'Ataulfo') is a small to medium tropical fruit tree native to Mexico, prized for its creamy, sweet, golden-yellow fruit.

Ataulfo Mango trees grow up to 5–8 meters tall with a compact, spreading canopy. They thrive in tropical and subtropical climates with well-drained soil and full sunlight. This variety is favored for its smooth, fiberless flesh and intense sweetness.
