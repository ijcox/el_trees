Tommy Atkins Mango Tree
The Tommy Atkins Mango Tree (Mangifera indica 'Tommy Atkins') is a tropical fruit tree widely cultivated in the Americas, known for its bright red and green fruit and long shelf life.

Tommy Atkins Mango trees grow up to 10–12 meters tall with a spreading canopy. They thrive in tropical and subtropical climates with full sunlight and well-drained soils. This variety is especially valued commercially for its durability during transport and extended storage.

Characteristics of Tommy Atkins Mango Trees

  • Medium to large-sized tree with spreading canopy
  • Produces red and green, sweet fruit
  • Thrives in tropical and subtropical climates
  • Long shelf life and transport durability
  • Moderate growth rate
  • Widely cultivated commercially

Common Uses

  • Fresh consumption
  • Juices, smoothies, and desserts
  • Pickles and preserves
  • Flavoring for ice creams and confectionery
  • Commercial export due to long shelf life
