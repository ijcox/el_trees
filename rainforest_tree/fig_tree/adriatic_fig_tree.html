Adriatic Fig Tree
The Adriatic Fig Tree (Ficus carica 'Adriatic') is a premium fig variety known for its light green skin and sweet, berry-like pink flesh.

Adriatic Fig trees grow 3–10 meters tall with broad, lobed leaves and a spreading canopy. They thrive in warm, Mediterranean-like climates with full sun and well-drained soil. The fruit is especially prized for fig bars, desserts, and high-quality dried figs.

Characteristics of Adriatic Fig Trees

  • Deciduous fruit tree with lobed leaves
  • Produces light green figs with pink flesh
  • Very sweet, dessert-quality fruit
  • Thrives in warm Mediterranean climates
  • Highly valued for drying and baking
  • Reliable seasonal producer

Common Uses

  • Fig bars and baked goods
  • Dried fruit production
  • Fresh eating dessert fig
  • Jams and preserves
  • Gourmet culinary use
