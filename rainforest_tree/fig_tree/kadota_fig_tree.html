Kadota Fig Tree
The Kadota Fig Tree (Ficus carica 'Kadota') is a popular fig variety known for its light green fruit and mild, honey-like sweetness.

Kadota Fig trees grow 3–10 meters tall with broad, lobed leaves and a spreading canopy. They thrive in warm climates with full sun and well-drained soil. The fruit is typically greenish-yellow with amber flesh and is commonly used for drying and preserves.

Characteristics of Kadota Fig Trees

  • Mild, sweet flavor profile
  • Thrives in warm, sunny climates
  • Common commercial drying fig
  • Hardy and productive cultivar
