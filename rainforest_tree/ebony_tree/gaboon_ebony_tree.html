Gaboon Ebony Tree
The Gaboon Ebony Tree (Diospyros crassiflora) is a highly valued tropical hardwood native to Central Africa, especially Gabon and surrounding regions, known for its deep black wood and exceptional density.

Gaboon Ebony trees typically grow 15–25 meters tall with straight trunks and dense foliage. They thrive in humid tropical rainforests with rich, well-drained soils. This species is one of the most commercially important ebonies due to its uniform color and superior hardness.

Characteristics of Gaboon Ebony Trees

  • Deep black, uniform heartwood
  • Extremely dense and heavy hardwood
  • Fine, smooth grain
  • Slow-growing and long-lived
  • Native to Central Africa
  • Highly resistant to wear and decay

Common Uses

  • Musical instruments (fingerboards, keys)
  • Fine furniture and luxury cabinetry
  • Carvings and decorative art pieces
  • High-end veneers and inlays
  • Precision tools and specialty items
