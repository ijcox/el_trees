African Ebony Tree
The African Ebony Tree (Diospyros crassiflora) is a dense hardwood species native to Central and West Africa, renowned for its extremely dark, almost black wood.

African Ebony trees typically grow to heights of 15–25 meters with a straight trunk and dense canopy. They thrive in tropical rainforests and prefer warm, humid climates with well-drained soils. The wood is exceptionally hard and heavy, making it one of the most valuable timbers in the world.

Characteristics of African Ebony Trees

  • Extremely dense, dark black hardwood
  • Straight trunk with compact crown
  • Slow-growing and long-lived
  • Small leaves with glossy appearance
  • Native to Central and West Africa
  • Highly resistant to decay and pests

Common Uses

  • Fine furniture and luxury woodwork
  • Musical instruments (piano keys, fingerboards)
  • Carvings and ornamental objects
  • High-end flooring and veneers
  • Tool handles and specialty items
