Commercial Balsa Tree
The Commercial Balsa Tree (Ochroma pyramidale var. commercialis) refers to Balsa trees cultivated specifically for timber production and industrial use.

Commercial Balsa trees grow 25–30 meters tall with straight trunks and broad leaves. They are cultivated in plantations under controlled tropical conditions to produce lightweight wood efficiently. This wood is widely used in crafts, model-making, flotation devices, and other commercial applications.

Characteristics of Commercial Balsa Trees

  • Fast-growing tree reaching 25–30 meters tall
  • Thrives in tropical plantation conditions
  • Produces lightweight, buoyant wood
  • Straight trunk with broad leaves
  • Short-lived but renewable through plantation cycles
  • Supports controlled ecosystem management in plantations

Common Uses

  • Lightweight wood for models, crafts, and industrial products
  • Floats, rafts, and boats
  • Construction of lightweight structures
  • Ornamental planting and timber research
  • Supports sustainable timber production
