Central American Balsa Tree
The Central American Balsa Tree (Ochroma pyramidale var. centralis) is a fast-growing tropical tree native to Central America, prized for its lightweight wood.

Central American Balsa trees grow 25–30 meters tall with straight trunks and broad leaves. They thrive in tropical climates with full sunlight and well-drained soils. The wood is exceptionally light and buoyant, making it ideal for model building, crafts, and lightweight construction.

Characteristics of Central American Balsa Trees

  • Fast-growing tree reaching 25–30 meters tall
  • Thrives in Central American tropical climates
  • Lightweight, buoyant wood
  • Straight trunk with large leaves
  • Supports biodiversity in tropical forests
  • Short-lived but quickly renewable

Common Uses

  • Lightweight wood for model building and crafts
  • Floats and rafts
  • Ornamental planting in tropical areas
  • Experimental timber for construction
  • Supports wildlife habitat in forests
