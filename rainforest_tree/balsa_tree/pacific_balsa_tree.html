Pacific Balsa Tree
The Pacific Balsa Tree (Ochroma pyramidale var. pacifica) is a tropical tree native to Pacific islands, valued for its extremely light and buoyant wood, ideal for traditional and modern applications.

Pacific Balsa trees grow 25–30 meters tall with straight trunks and broad leaves. They thrive in tropical island climates with full sun and well-drained soils. The wood is prized for model-making, lightweight structures, and flotation devices due to its low density and excellent buoyancy.

Characteristics of Pacific Balsa Trees

  • Fast-growing tree reaching 25–30 meters tall
  • Thrives in Pacific island tropical climates
  • Lightweight, buoyant wood
  • Straight trunk with broad leaves
  • Supports island forest biodiversity
  • Short-lived but quickly renewable

Common Uses

  • Lightweight wood for models, crafts, and construction
  • Floats, rafts, and small boats
  • Ornamental planting in tropical areas
  • Experimental timber for design and engineering
  • Habitat support for forest wildlife
