Castilla Elastica Tree
The Castilla Elastica Tree (Castilla elastica) is a tropical tree native to Central America, historically important for its latex, used by ancient Mesoamerican civilizations.

Castilla Elastica trees grow 20–30 meters tall with straight trunks and smooth bark. They thrive in humid tropical forests with well-drained soils. The latex harvested from this tree was used for making balls, footwear, and other items in ancient cultures, and today it contributes to natural rubber research.
