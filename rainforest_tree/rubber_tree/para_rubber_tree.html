Para Rubber Tree
The Para Rubber Tree (Hevea brasiliensis var. para) is a tropical tree native to the Amazon rainforest, known for its latex-producing bark and economic importance in natural rubber production.

Para Rubber trees grow 30–45 meters tall with straight trunks and smooth bark. They thrive in tropical climates with high humidity and well-drained soils. The latex extracted from the bark is processed to produce natural rubber used in countless industrial and consumer products.

Characteristics of Para Rubber Trees

  • Straight, tall trunk with smooth bark
  • Thrives in tropical, humid climates
  • Produces latex for rubber extraction
  • Fast-growing and long-lived in suitable conditions
  • Supports biodiversity in tropical forests
  • Native to the Amazon rainforest

Common Uses

  • Natural rubber production for tires, industrial goods, and consumer products
  • Ornamental planting in tropical regions
  • Latex harvesting for adhesives and medical equipment
  • Timber for light construction and furniture
  • Habitat for forest wildlife
