Evergreen Tree
Evergreen trees are trees that retain their leaves or needles throughout the year. Instead of shedding all their foliage seasonally, they gradually replace leaves over time, allowing them to remain green year-round.

Evergreen trees are found in many climates, from cold northern forests to tropical regions. Many conifer trees are evergreen, though some broadleaf species are evergreen as well.

Common Types of Evergreen Trees

Characteristics of Evergreen Trees

  • Keep foliage year-round instead of shedding seasonally
  • Often have needle-like or waxy leaves
  • Many species are adapted to cold or dry environments
  • Continue photosynthesis whenever conditions allow
  • Often grow in forests where nutrient conservation is important

Common Uses

  • Timber and construction lumber
  • Paper and pulp production
  • Landscaping and ornamental planting
  • Windbreaks and privacy screens
  • Christmas trees
  • Essential oils and natural resins
