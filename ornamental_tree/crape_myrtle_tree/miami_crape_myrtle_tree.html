Miami Crape Myrtle Tree
The Miami Crape Myrtle is a compact ornamental variety known for its bright pink-purple blooms and excellent heat tolerance.

Miami crape myrtles grow 3–5 meters tall, making them smaller than many other varieties. They are ideal for tight garden spaces and urban landscapes where a smaller flowering tree is needed. Their blooms are abundant and appear throughout the summer.
