Natchez Crape Myrtle Tree
The Natchez Crape Myrtle is a fast-growing ornamental tree known for its white summer blooms and smooth, cinnamon-colored bark.

Natchez crape myrtles grow 6–9 meters tall with an upright, vase-shaped canopy. They are highly valued for their long blooming season and attractive exfoliating bark that provides winter interest. They thrive in warm climates and are widely used in landscaping.
