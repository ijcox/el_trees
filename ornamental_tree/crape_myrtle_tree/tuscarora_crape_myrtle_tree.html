Tuscarora Crape Myrtle Tree
The Tuscarora Crape Myrtle is a popular ornamental tree known for its vibrant coral-pink flowers and strong resistance to heat and drought.

Tuscarora crape myrtles grow 6–8 meters tall with a rounded, upright form. They are valued for their vivid summer blooms and reliable performance in hot climates. Their flowers are slightly darker and more saturated than many other crape myrtle varieties.
