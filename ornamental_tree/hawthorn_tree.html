Hawthorn Tree
Hawthorn trees (Crataegus species) are small to medium deciduous trees native to temperate regions of the Northern Hemisphere. They are valued for their attractive spring blossoms, bright red fruits, and use as ornamental or hedge plants.

Hawthorn trees thrive in temperate climates with well-drained soil and full to partial sunlight. They are commonly planted in gardens, parks, and as hedgerows for their beauty, wildlife value, and protective thorns.

Characteristics of Hawthorn Trees

  • Deciduous trees with thorny branches and rounded crowns
  • Small to medium height, typically 15–30 feet
  • Flowers are small, white to pink, and bloom in spring
  • Fruit is a small pome, often red, attractive to birds
  • Leaves may turn red or orange in autumn

Common Uses

  • Ornamental planting for gardens, parks, and streets
  • Hedgerows and natural fencing due to thorns
  • Attracts pollinators and wildlife
  • Shade tree in smaller spaces
  • Traditional medicinal uses from berries and bark
