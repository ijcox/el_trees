Shaina Japanese Maple Tree
The Shaina Japanese Maple (Acer palmatum 'Shaina') is a compact, dwarf variety known for its dense growth and vibrant red foliage.

Shaina Japanese maples typically grow 1.5–3 meters tall with a dense, rounded form. They thrive in partial shade with well-drained soil and are ideal for small gardens, containers, and tight spaces. Their bright red leaves hold color well through the growing season.
