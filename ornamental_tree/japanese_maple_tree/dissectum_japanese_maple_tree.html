Dissectum Japanese Maple Tree
The Dissectum Japanese Maple (Acer palmatum var. dissectum) is a delicate ornamental tree known for its finely cut, lace-like leaves and graceful weeping form.

Dissectum Japanese maples typically grow 1.5–3 meters tall with wide, cascading branches. They thrive in partial shade with well-drained soil and are highly valued in landscaping for their elegant structure and seasonal color changes.
