Emperor I Japanese Maple Tree
The Emperor I Japanese Maple (Acer palmatum 'Emperor I') is a hardy red-leaf variety known for its late leaf-out and improved resistance to spring frost.

Emperor I Japanese maples grow 5–8 meters tall with a broad, rounded canopy. They are similar to Bloodgood but leaf out later in spring, helping avoid frost damage. They thrive in partial sun and well-drained soils, maintaining deep red foliage throughout the growing season.
