Bloodgood Japanese Maple Tree
The Bloodgood Japanese Maple (Acer palmatum 'Bloodgood') is a highly popular ornamental tree known for its rich, deep red foliage and strong growth habit.

Bloodgood Japanese maples grow 5–8 meters tall with a rounded canopy and upright branching. They are more sun-tolerant than many Japanese maples and maintain their deep red color throughout the growing season. They thrive in well-drained soil and are widely used in residential and garden landscapes.
