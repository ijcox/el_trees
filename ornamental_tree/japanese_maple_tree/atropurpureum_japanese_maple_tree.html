Atropurpureum Japanese Maple Tree
The Atropurpureum Japanese Maple (Acer palmatum 'Atropurpureum') is a popular ornamental tree known for its deep red to purple foliage throughout the growing season.

Atropurpureum Japanese maples grow 4–8 meters tall with a rounded, spreading canopy. They thrive in partial shade with well-drained soil and are valued for their consistent color and elegant form in landscapes.
