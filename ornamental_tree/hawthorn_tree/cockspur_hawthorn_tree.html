Cockspur Hawthorn Tree
The Cockspur Hawthorn (Crataegus crus-galli) is a hardy ornamental tree known for its long thorns, white spring flowers, and red fall fruit.

Cockspur hawthorns grow 5–9 meters tall with a wide, spreading canopy. They are extremely hardy and tolerant of urban conditions, drought, and poor soils. Their long, sharp thorns make them useful as natural barrier trees.
