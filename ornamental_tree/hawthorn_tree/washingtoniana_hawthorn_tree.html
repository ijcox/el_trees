Washingtoniana Hawthorn Tree
The Washingtoniana Hawthorn is an ornamental hawthorn variety known for its dense flowering, red berries, and adaptability to urban environments.

Washingtoniana hawthorns grow 6–10 meters tall with a compact, rounded canopy. They are valued for their heavy spring bloom and persistent fruit that attracts birds into winter. They are often used in landscape plantings where a hardy ornamental tree is needed.
