Washington Hawthorn Tree
The Washington Hawthorn (Crataegus phaenopyrum) is a small ornamental tree known for its white spring flowers and bright red fall berries.

Washington hawthorns grow 6–10 meters tall with a dense, rounded canopy and thorny branches. They are commonly used in urban landscapes due to their tolerance of pollution and poor soils. Their berries persist into winter, providing food for birds.
