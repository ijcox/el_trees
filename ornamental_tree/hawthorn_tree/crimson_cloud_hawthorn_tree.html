Crimson Cloud Hawthorn Tree
The Crimson Cloud Hawthorn is a flowering ornamental tree known for its bright crimson-pink blossoms and compact growth habit.

Crimson Cloud hawthorns grow 5–8 meters tall with a neat, rounded canopy. They are prized for their dense clusters of vivid pink-red flowers in late spring. They are hardy, adaptable, and widely used in decorative landscaping.
