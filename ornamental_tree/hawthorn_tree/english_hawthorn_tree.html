English Hawthorn Tree
The English Hawthorn (Crataegus monogyna) is a small, hardy ornamental tree known for its white spring blossoms and red berry-like fruits.

English hawthorns grow 5–10 meters tall with a dense, thorny branching structure. They are widely used in hedgerows across Europe and are valued for their spring flowers and wildlife-supporting berries. They are also extremely hardy and adaptable to many soil conditions.

Characteristics of English Hawthorn Trees

  • Small deciduous ornamental tree
  • White spring flower clusters
  • Red berry-like fruit (haws)
  • Thorny branches
  • Very hardy and adaptable
  • Common hedgerow species

Common Uses

  • Hedgerows and fencing
  • Wildlife habitat
  • Ornamental landscaping
  • Windbreak planting
  • Naturalized areas
