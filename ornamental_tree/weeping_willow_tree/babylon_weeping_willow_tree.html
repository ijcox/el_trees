Babylon Weeping Willow Tree
The Babylon Weeping Willow Tree (Salix babylonica) is a deciduous tree known for its long, drooping branches and elegant weeping form. It is widely cultivated as an ornamental tree around water features and gardens.

Babylon Weeping Willow trees grow 10–25 meters tall with a broad, drooping crown. They thrive in moist soils and prefer full sunlight. The leaves are narrow, lance-shaped, and green, while the branches gracefully sweep towards the ground.

Characteristics of Babylon Weeping Willow Trees

  • Narrow, lance-shaped green leaves
  • Broad, drooping crown with elegant weeping branches
  • Fast-growing and medium-lived
  • Thrives in moist soils
  • Ornamental form ideal for landscapes
  • Originated from China

Common Uses

  • Ornamental planting near ponds, lakes, and rivers
  • Shade and visual interest in gardens and parks
  • Windbreaks in wet areas
  • Wildlife habitat
  • Cut branches used in crafts and floral arrangements
