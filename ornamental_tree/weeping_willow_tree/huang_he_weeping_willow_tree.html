Huang He Weeping Willow Tree
The Huang He Weeping Willow Tree (Salix babylonica 'Huang He') is a cultivated variety of weeping willow known for its graceful, cascading branches and tolerance to a variety of soils. It is often used in ornamental landscaping along waterways.

Huang He Weeping Willow trees grow 8–20 meters tall with a broad, drooping crown. They thrive in moist soils and full sunlight. The leaves are narrow and lance-shaped, with branches that elegantly sweep toward the ground.

Characteristics of Huang He Weeping Willow Trees

  • Narrow, lance-shaped leaves
  • Broad, drooping crown with cascading branches
  • Fast-growing and medium-lived
  • Tolerates a variety of soil conditions
  • Ornamental form for landscaping
  • Originated as a cultivated variety from China

Common Uses

  • Ornamental planting along rivers, lakes, and gardens
  • Shade and visual interest in parks
  • Soil stabilization near water bodies
  • Wildlife habitat
  • Cut branches for decorative uses
