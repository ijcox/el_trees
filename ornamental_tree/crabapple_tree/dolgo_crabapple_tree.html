Dolgo Crabapple Tree
The Dolgo Crabapple is a hardy, cold-tolerant variety known for its early white blossoms and large, tart red fruit.

Dolgo crabapples grow 5–8 meters tall with a spreading canopy. They are especially valued for their edible fruit, which is larger and more useful for preserves than many ornamental crabapples. They also provide strong spring bloom and wildlife value in fall and winter.
