Royal Raindrops Crabapple Tree
The Royal Raindrops Crabapple is a modern ornamental variety known for its deep pink flowers, purple foliage, and excellent disease resistance.

Royal Raindrops crabapples grow 5–7 meters tall with an upright, spreading canopy. They are especially valued for their striking purple-red leaves and long-lasting ornamental appeal. Their bright pink blooms attract pollinators in spring, followed by small red fruits in fall.
