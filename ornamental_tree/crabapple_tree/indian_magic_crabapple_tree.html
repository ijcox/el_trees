Indian Magic Crabapple Tree
The Indian Magic Crabapple is an ornamental crabapple variety known for its vibrant pink-red spring blossoms and persistent red fruit.

Indian Magic crabapples grow 4–8 meters tall with a rounded, spreading canopy. They are valued for their long-lasting seasonal interest, including spring flowers, summer foliage, and colorful autumn fruit display. They are widely used as ornamental landscape trees.
