Centennial Crabapple Tree
The Centennial Crabapple is an ornamental tree known for its pink spring blossoms and bright red fruit that persists into winter.

Centennial crabapples grow 5–8 meters tall with a rounded, spreading canopy. They are valued for their strong seasonal interest and adaptability to a wide range of soils and climates. Their fruit is often retained through winter, providing food for wildlife.
