Sugar Tyme Crabapple Tree
The Sugar Tyme Crabapple is a highly disease-resistant ornamental tree known for its abundant white spring blossoms and bright red persistent fruit.

Sugar Tyme crabapples grow 5–7 meters tall with a compact, rounded canopy. They are widely planted in urban and residential landscapes due to their strong disease resistance and attractive seasonal features. Their spring bloom is dense and fragrant, followed by decorative fruit in fall and winter.
