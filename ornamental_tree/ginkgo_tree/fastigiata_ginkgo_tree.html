Fastigiata Ginkgo Tree
The Fastigiata Ginkgo is a narrow, upright cultivar valued for its formal shape and brilliant golden autumn foliage.

Fastigiata ginkgo trees grow 15–25 meters tall with a very narrow, column-like form. They are commonly used in urban landscapes where space is limited but height is desired. They maintain the classic ginkgo resilience and stunning fall color.

Characteristics of Fastigiata Ginkgo Trees

  • Deciduous ornamental tree
  • Very narrow upright growth
  • Bright golden fall foliage
  • Highly urban tolerant
  • Low maintenance tree
  • Formal landscape appearance

Common Uses

  • Street tree planting

  • Formal gardens
  • Driveway accents
  • Fall color landscaping
