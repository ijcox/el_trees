Autumn Gold Ginkgo Tree
The Autumn Gold Ginkgo is a popular cultivar known for its uniform golden fall color and symmetrical branching habit.

Autumn Gold ginkgo trees grow 15–25 meters tall with a broad, upright canopy. They are prized for their reliable, vibrant yellow fall foliage and strong urban tolerance. They are often planted as street trees and in parks for seasonal color.
