Pink Oleander Tree
The Pink Oleander is a flowering evergreen shrub known for its abundant soft pink blooms and strong tolerance to heat and drought.

Pink oleanders grow 2–6 meters tall with dense, upright branching. They are widely used in warm climates as ornamental plants due to their continuous flowering and low maintenance needs. Their soft pink blossoms appear in clusters throughout the warmer seasons.
