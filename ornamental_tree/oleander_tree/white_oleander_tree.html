White Oleander Tree
The White Oleander is an evergreen ornamental shrub known for its crisp white flower clusters and strong drought resistance.

White oleanders grow 2–6 meters tall and form dense, upright shrubs. They are widely used in warm, dry climates due to their ability to tolerate heat, salt, and poor soils. Their white blossoms provide a clean, bright appearance in landscape designs.

Characteristics of White Oleander Trees

  • Evergreen ornamental shrub/tree
  • White clustered flowers
  • Drought and heat tolerant
  • Long blooming season
  • Dense upright growth
  • Common hedge plant

Common Uses

  • Ornamental landscaping
  • Hedges and screens
  • Roadside planting
  • Coastal gardens
  • Low-water gardens
