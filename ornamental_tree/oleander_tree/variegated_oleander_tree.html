Variegated Oleander Tree
The Variegated Oleander is an ornamental evergreen shrub known for its striking green-and-cream foliage combined with colorful flower clusters.

Variegated oleanders grow 2–6 meters tall and are especially valued for their decorative foliage as well as their blooms. They are commonly used in warm climates for hedges, borders, and accent plantings. Their striped leaves provide year-round visual interest even when not in bloom.

Characteristics of Variegated Oleander Trees

  • Evergreen ornamental shrub/tree
  • Green-and-cream variegated leaves
  • Pink, white, or red flower clusters
  • Heat and drought tolerant
  • Long blooming season
  • Highly decorative foliage plant

Common Uses

  • Ornamental landscaping
  • Hedges and screens
  • Accent garden plant
  • Coastal gardens
  • Warm climate landscapes
