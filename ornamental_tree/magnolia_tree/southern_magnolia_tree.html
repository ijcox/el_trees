Southern Magnolia Tree
The Southern Magnolia Tree (Magnolia grandiflora) is native to the southeastern United States. It is an evergreen tree famous for its large, fragrant white flowers and glossy dark green leaves.

Southern Magnolia trees grow 15–30 meters tall with a pyramidal to rounded crown. They prefer well-drained, acidic soils and full to partial sunlight. Leaves are broad, glossy, and evergreen, and flowers are large, white, and highly fragrant.

Characteristics of Southern Magnolia Trees

  • Evergreen, broad, glossy leaves
  • Pyramidal to rounded crown
  • Large, fragrant white flowers
  • Long lifespan and slow to medium growth
  • Thrives in well-drained, acidic soils
  • Native to southeastern United States

Common Uses

  • Ornamental planting and shade tree
  • Fragrant flowers for gardens and landscaping
  • Timber for furniture and decorative woodwork
  • Wildlife habitat, especially for birds and insects
  • Street tree and public park planting
