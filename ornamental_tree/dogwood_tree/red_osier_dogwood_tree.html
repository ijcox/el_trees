Red Osier Dogwood Tree
The Red Osier Dogwood (Cornus sericea) is a hardy shrub-like dogwood known for its bright red stems, especially visible in winter.

Red osier dogwoods grow 1.5–3 meters tall and often spread into dense thickets. They thrive in wet soils such as riverbanks, wetlands, and lowland areas. Their vivid red stems provide strong winter visual interest in landscapes.

Characteristics of Red Osier Dogwood Trees

  • Multi-stemmed shrub or small tree
  • Bright red stems in winter
  • Thrives in wet soil conditions
  • Fast-growing and hardy
  • Excellent for erosion control
  • Wildlife-friendly habitat plant

Common Uses

  • Wetland restoration
  • Erosion control planting
  • Ornamental winter color
  • Wildlife habitat
  • Natural hedgerows
