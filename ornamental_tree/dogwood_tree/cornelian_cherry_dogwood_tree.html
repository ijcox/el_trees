Cornelian Cherry Dogwood Tree
The Cornelian Cherry Dogwood (Cornus mas) is a hardy, early-flowering tree known for its yellow blossoms and edible red fruit.

Cornelian cherry dogwoods grow 4–8 meters tall with a rounded, multi-stemmed structure. They bloom very early in spring with bright yellow flowers before most other trees leaf out. In late summer, they produce red berry-like fruits used in jams and syrups.
