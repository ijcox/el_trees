Pacific Dogwood Tree
The Pacific Dogwood (Cornus nuttallii) is a flowering tree native to western North America, known for its large white blooms.

Pacific dogwoods grow 8–20 meters tall with a graceful branching structure. They thrive in cool, moist forest environments and are especially common in the Pacific Northwest. Their large white bracts make them a striking ornamental and native woodland tree.
