Kousa Dogwood Tree
The Kousa Dogwood (Cornus kousa) is an ornamental tree known for its star-shaped spring flowers and excellent disease resistance.

Kousa dogwoods grow 4–8 meters tall with a layered, vase-like structure. They bloom later than flowering dogwood and produce decorative berry-like fruits in summer. They are highly valued for their resistance to dogwood anthracnose disease.
