Flowering Dogwood Tree
The Flowering Dogwood (Cornus florida) is a popular ornamental tree known for its showy spring blooms and vibrant fall color.

Flowering dogwoods grow 5–10 meters tall with a graceful, layered branching structure. They thrive in partial shade and well-drained, slightly acidic soils. Their spring display of white or pink “flowers” (bracts) makes them a favorite landscape tree.

Characteristics of Flowering Dogwood Trees

  • Small ornamental deciduous tree
  • Showy spring bracts (white/pink)
  • Attractive red fall foliage
  • Layered branching habit
  • Prefers partial shade
  • Wildlife-friendly fruit

Common Uses

  • Ornamental landscaping
  • Garden focal points
  • Woodland gardens
  • Wildlife habitat planting
  • Residential yards
