Forest Pansy Redbud Tree
The Forest Pansy Redbud (Cercis canadensis 'Forest Pansy') is a striking ornamental variety known for its deep purple foliage and vibrant spring flowers.

Forest Pansy redbuds grow 4–8 meters tall with a rounded, spreading canopy. They are especially valued for their rich purple-red leaves that emerge in spring and hold color through summer in cooler climates. Their pink-purple flowers appear early in spring before the leaves fully develop.
