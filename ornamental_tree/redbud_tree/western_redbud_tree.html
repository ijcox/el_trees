Western Redbud Tree
The Western Redbud (Cercis occidentalis) is a drought-tolerant ornamental tree native to the western United States, known for its vivid magenta spring blooms.

Western redbuds grow 3–6 meters tall and often have a multi-stemmed, shrub-like form. They are well adapted to dry, rocky environments and are commonly found in chaparral and foothill ecosystems. Their intense magenta flowers create a striking display in early spring.
