Sweet Cherry Tree
The Sweet Cherry Tree (Prunus avium) is a large deciduous tree native to Europe and western Asia. It is cultivated widely for its sweet, edible cherries and attractive spring blossoms.

Sweet Cherry trees grow 15–25 meters tall with a rounded crown. They thrive in well-drained, fertile soils and full sun. The tree produces edible cherries and beautiful white to pink flowers in spring. The wood is used for fine furniture, cabinetry, and decorative purposes.

Characteristics of Sweet Cherry Trees

  • Deciduous tree with ovate green leaves and glossy foliage
  • Produces clusters of sweet, edible cherries
  • Beautiful white to pink blossoms in spring
  • Moderate to fast growth rate and long-lived
  • Native to Europe and western Asia, widely cultivated worldwide

Common Uses

  • Fruit production for fresh consumption and culinary uses
  • Ornamental planting for spring blossoms
  • Timber for fine furniture and cabinetry
  • Wildlife habitat and food source for birds and mammals
  • Shade tree in large gardens and orchards
