Silver Maple Tree
The Silver Maple Tree (Acer saccharinum) is a fast-growing deciduous tree native to eastern North America. It is recognized for its silvery underside of leaves and its adaptability to wet soils.

Silver Maple trees grow 15–25 meters tall with a rounded to spreading crown. They thrive in full sun and tolerate wet, poorly drained soils, making them common along streams, rivers, and urban landscapes. Acorns are less prominent than other maples, and the wood is used for furniture and general construction.

Characteristics of Silver Maple Trees

  • Deciduous tree with deeply lobed green leaves turning yellow in fall
  • Silvery underside of leaves
  • Rounded to spreading crown
  • Fast-growing and moderately long-lived
  • Native to eastern North America and tolerant of wet soils

Common Uses

  • Ornamental planting along streets and parks
  • Timber for furniture and general construction
  • Wildlife habitat
  • Shade tree in open landscapes
  • Riparian and erosion control planting
