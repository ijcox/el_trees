Red Maple Tree
The Red Maple Tree (Acer rubrum) is a medium to large deciduous tree native to eastern North America. It is celebrated for its bright red fall foliage and adaptability to a wide range of soils.

Red Maple trees typically grow 15–25 meters tall with a rounded to oval crown. They thrive in full sun to partial shade and can tolerate wet or poorly drained soils. Acorns provide food for wildlife, and the wood is used for furniture, flooring, and general construction.

Characteristics of Red Maple Trees

  • Deciduous tree with lobed green leaves turning bright red in fall
  • Rounded to oval crown
  • Adaptable to a wide range of soils, including wet areas
  • Medium growth rate and moderately long-lived
  • Native to eastern North America

Common Uses

  • Ornamental planting for fall color in parks and landscapes
  • Timber for furniture, flooring, and construction
  • Wildlife habitat and seed food source
  • Shade tree in urban and suburban areas
  • Street and park planting due to adaptability
