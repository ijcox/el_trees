Honduran Mahogany Tree
The Honduran Mahogany Tree (Swietenia macrophylla) is a large, fast-growing tropical hardwood tree native to Central and South America. It is prized for its rich, reddish-brown wood and fine grain.

Honduran Mahogany trees grow 30–45 meters tall with a straight trunk and rounded crown. They thrive in well-drained tropical soils with full sunlight. The leaves are pinnately compound, and the tree produces small, winged seeds in capsules.

Characteristics of Honduran Mahogany Trees

  • Pinnately compound leaves
  • Straight, tall trunk with rounded crown
  • Produces small, winged seeds in capsules
  • Rich, reddish-brown wood with fine grain
  • Fast-growing and long-lived
  • Native to Central and South America

Common Uses

  • High-quality timber for furniture, cabinetry, and musical instruments
  • Boat building and flooring
  • Ornamental planting in tropical landscapes
  • Shade tree in plantations and gardens
  • Wildlife habitat for tropical species
