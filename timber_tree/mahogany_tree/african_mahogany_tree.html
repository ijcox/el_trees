African Mahogany Tree
The African Mahogany Tree (Khaya spp.) is a large tropical hardwood tree native to West and Central Africa. It is valued for its reddish-brown wood and durability, widely used in furniture and construction.

African Mahogany trees grow 30–45 meters tall with a straight trunk and broad, rounded crown. They thrive in tropical, well-drained soils with full sunlight. The leaves are pinnately compound, and the tree produces small winged seeds.

Characteristics of African Mahogany Trees

  • Pinnately compound leaves
  • Straight trunk with broad, rounded crown
  • Produces small winged seeds
  • Durable, reddish-brown wood
  • Fast-growing and long-lived
  • Native to West and Central Africa

Common Uses

  • High-quality timber for furniture, cabinetry, and musical instruments
  • Boat building and flooring
  • Ornamental planting in tropical landscapes
  • Shade tree in plantations and parks
  • Wildlife habitat in tropical forests
