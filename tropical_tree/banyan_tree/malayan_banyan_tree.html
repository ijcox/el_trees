Malayan Banyan Tree
The Malayan Banyan Tree (Ficus microcarpa var. malayana) is a tropical fig tree native to Malaysia, appreciated for its thick canopy, aerial roots, and ornamental appeal.

Malayan Banyan trees grow 15–25 meters tall with a wide, spreading canopy supported by multiple aerial prop roots. They thrive in tropical climates with full sunlight and well-drained soils. This variety is popular for ornamental landscaping and provides shade and habitat for local wildlife.

Characteristics of Malayan Banyan Trees

  • Medium to large tree with broad canopy
  • Multiple aerial prop roots forming secondary trunks
  • Thrives in tropical climates
  • Dense foliage providing shade
  • Ornamental and visually striking
  • Native to Malaysia

Common Uses

  • Ornamental planting in parks and gardens
  • Shade tree in urban and rural landscapes
  • Habitat for birds and small animals
  • Cultural and aesthetic purposes
  • Soil stabilization and windbreaks
