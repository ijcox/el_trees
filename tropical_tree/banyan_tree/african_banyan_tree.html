African Banyan Tree
The African Banyan Tree (Ficus sycomorus) is a tropical fig tree native to Africa, renowned for its broad canopy, massive trunk, and cultural significance in local communities.

African Banyan trees grow 20–25 meters tall with a wide, spreading canopy and thick trunk. They thrive in tropical climates with well-drained soils and full sunlight. This tree provides shade, habitat for wildlife, and has long-standing cultural and historical importance in Africa.

Characteristics of African Banyan Trees

  • Medium to large tree with broad canopy
  • Massive trunk and spreading branches
  • Thrives in tropical climates
  • Provides shade and habitat for wildlife
  • Long-lived and culturally significant
  • Native to Africa

Common Uses

  • Shade and ornamental planting
  • Wildlife habitat
  • Cultural and ceremonial uses
  • Soil stabilization and erosion control
  • Urban landscaping and community spaces
