Indian Banyan Tree
The Indian Banyan Tree (Ficus benghalensis) is a majestic tropical fig tree native to India, famous for its extensive aerial roots and wide canopy coverage.

Indian Banyan trees can grow over 30 meters tall with a canopy spreading widely via aerial prop roots. They thrive in tropical and subtropical climates with full sunlight and well-drained soils. This tree is culturally significant in India and provides extensive shade and habitat for wildlife.

Characteristics of Indian Banyan Trees

  • Massive tree with wide canopy
  • Aerial prop roots that form secondary trunks
  • Thrives in tropical and subtropical climates
  • Provides extensive shade
  • Long-lived and culturally significant
  • Native to India

Common Uses

  • Shade and ornamental planting
  • Wildlife habitat for birds and small mammals
  • Cultural and religious significance
  • Medicinal uses of leaves, bark, and fruits
  • Soil stabilization and windbreaks
