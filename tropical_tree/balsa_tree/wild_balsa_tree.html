Wild Balsa Tree
The Wild Balsa Tree (Ochroma pyramidale var. silvestre) refers to naturally occurring Balsa trees found in tropical forests, providing lightweight timber and ecological value.

Wild Balsa trees grow 20–30 meters tall with straight trunks and wide leaves. They thrive in humid tropical forests with full sun and well-drained soils. The lightweight wood supports local construction and craft needs, while the trees contribute to habitat diversity in their native forests.

Characteristics of Wild Balsa Trees

  • Medium to tall tree, 20–30 meters in height
  • Thrives in natural tropical forest environments
  • Lightweight, soft wood
  • Straight trunk with broad leaves
  • Supports tropical forest biodiversity
  • Short-lived but naturally regenerative

Common Uses

  • Lightweight wood for local construction, crafts, and models
  • Floats and rafts
  • Supports habitat for forest wildlife
  • Ornamental planting in tropical areas
  • Ecological restoration projects
