Philippine Mahogany Tree
The Philippine Mahogany Tree (Shorea spp.) is a tropical hardwood tree native to the Philippines and Southeast Asia. It is valued for its reddish-brown timber and is widely used in furniture and construction.

Philippine Mahogany trees grow 20–40 meters tall with a straight trunk and broad, rounded crown. They thrive in tropical, well-drained soils with full sunlight. The leaves are simple to pinnately compound depending on species, and the tree produces small winged seeds.

Characteristics of Philippine Mahogany Trees

  • Simple or pinnately compound leaves depending on species
  • Straight trunk with broad, rounded crown
  • Produces small winged seeds
  • Reddish-brown, durable wood
  • Fast-growing and long-lived
  • Native to the Philippines and Southeast Asia

Common Uses

  • Timber for furniture, cabinetry, and construction
  • Boat building and flooring
  • Ornamental planting in tropical landscapes
  • Shade tree in plantations and parks
  • Wildlife habitat in tropical forests
