Teak Tree
Teak trees (Tectona grandis) are large tropical hardwood trees native to South and Southeast Asia. They are highly prized for their durable, water-resistant timber and rich golden-brown color.

Teak thrives in tropical climates with well-drained soils and abundant sunlight. It is widely cultivated for premium furniture, boat building, flooring, and outdoor structures due to its natural oils and resistance to decay.

Characteristics of Teak Trees

  • Tropical hardwood with dense, golden-brown wood
  • Deciduous leaves with large, ovate shape
  • Can grow up to 100 feet tall
  • Natural oils make wood resistant to water, pests, and decay
  • Valued for shade, timber, and ecological habitat in tropical forests

Common Uses

  • Premium furniture and cabinetry
  • Boat building, decking, and outdoor structures
  • Flooring and interior woodwork
  • Ornamental planting and shade trees in tropical regions
  • Ecological habitat for tropical forest wildlife
