Haden Mango Tree
The Haden Mango Tree (Mangifera indica 'Haden') is a popular tropical fruit tree originating from Florida, USA, known for its bright red and yellow fruit with a sweet, aromatic flavor.

Haden Mango trees grow up to 10–12 meters tall with a dense, spreading canopy. They thrive in tropical and subtropical climates with full sunlight and well-drained soils. The tree is widely cultivated for its colorful, flavorful fruit and is an important commercial variety.

Characteristics of Haden Mango Trees

  • Medium to large-sized tree with dense canopy
  • Produces red and yellow, sweet, aromatic fruit
  • Thrives in tropical and subtropical climates
  • Moderate growth rate
  • Originated in Florida, USA
  • Widely cultivated commercially

Common Uses

  • Fresh consumption
  • Juices, smoothies, and desserts
  • Pickles and preserves
  • Flavoring for ice creams and confectionery
  • Ornamental planting in tropical gardens
