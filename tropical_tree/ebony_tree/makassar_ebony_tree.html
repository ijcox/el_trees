Makassar Ebony Tree
The Makassar Ebony Tree (Diospyros celebica) is a tropical hardwood native to Indonesia, especially Sulawesi, known for its striking dark wood with brown or golden streaks.

Makassar Ebony trees grow up to 20–30 meters tall with a straight trunk and dense canopy. They thrive in tropical forests with warm temperatures and well-drained soils. Unlike other ebony species, its wood features dramatic striping, making it highly decorative and unique.

Characteristics of Makassar Ebony Trees

  • Dark wood with brown or golden streaks
  • Dense and heavy hardwood
  • Straight trunk with a full canopy
  • Moderately slow-growing
  • Native to Indonesia (Sulawesi)
  • Highly decorative grain patterns

Common Uses

  • Luxury furniture and cabinetry
  • Decorative veneers and paneling
  • Musical instruments and specialty woodwork
  • Fine carvings and artistic pieces
  • High-end interior design applications
