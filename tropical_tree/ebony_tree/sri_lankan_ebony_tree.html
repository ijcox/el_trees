Sri Lankan Ebony Tree
The Sri Lankan Ebony Tree (Diospyros ebenum) is a premium tropical hardwood native to Sri Lanka, known for its deep black heartwood and exceptional density.

Sri Lankan Ebony trees grow up to 20–25 meters tall and develop a dense, rounded canopy. They thrive in tropical climates with rich, well-drained soils and consistent rainfall. This species is one of the most sought-after ebonies due to its uniform color and fine grain.

Characteristics of Sri Lankan Ebony Trees

  • Uniform jet-black heartwood
  • Extremely dense and heavy wood
  • Fine, smooth grain ideal for polishing
  • Slow-growing and long-lived
  • Native to Sri Lanka
  • Highly resistant to decay and insects

Common Uses

  • High-end furniture and cabinetry
  • Musical instruments and piano components
  • Luxury carvings and decorative art
  • Fine veneers and inlays
  • Traditional and ornamental woodworking
