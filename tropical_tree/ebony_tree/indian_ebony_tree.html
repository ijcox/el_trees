Indian Ebony Tree
The Indian Ebony Tree (Diospyros ebenum) is a tropical hardwood species native to India and Sri Lanka, valued for its deep black wood and fine texture.

Indian Ebony trees grow to heights of 20–25 meters and feature a dense, rounded canopy. They thrive in tropical climates with well-drained soils and moderate rainfall. This species is highly prized for its durability and smooth finish, making it ideal for precision craftsmanship.

Characteristics of Indian Ebony Trees

  • Jet-black heartwood with fine grain
  • Dense and heavy hardwood
  • Slow-growing and highly durable
  • Small, leathery green leaves
  • Native to India and Sri Lanka
  • Resistant to decay and insect damage

Common Uses

  • Luxury furniture and cabinetry
  • Musical instruments and decorative inlays
  • Carved ornaments and sculptures
  • High-end veneers and paneling
  • Traditional woodworking and tools
