Pacific Breadfruit Tree
The Pacific Breadfruit Tree (Artocarpus altilis var. pacifica) is a tropical tree native to Pacific islands, known for its starchy fruit and cultural importance.

Pacific Breadfruit trees grow 10–20 meters tall with spreading crowns and large leaves. They thrive in tropical island climates with well-drained soils and abundant sunlight. The fruit is rich in starch and serves as a staple food in many Pacific cultures.

Characteristics of Pacific Breadfruit Trees

  • Medium to large tree, 10–20 meters tall
  • Thrives in tropical Pacific climates
  • Produces starchy, edible fruit
  • Spreading crown with large leaves
  • Supports island biodiversity
  • Fast-growing and long-lived in suitable conditions

Common Uses

  • Staple food: roasted, boiled, or baked fruit
  • Animal fodder
  • Ornamental planting and shade trees
  • Wood for lightweight construction and crafts
  • Supports local ecosystems and cultural practices
