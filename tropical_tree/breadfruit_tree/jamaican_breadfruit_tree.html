Jamaican Breadfruit Tree
The Jamaican Breadfruit Tree (Artocarpus altilis var. jamaicana) is a tropical tree widely cultivated in Jamaica for its starchy fruit and traditional culinary uses.

Jamaican Breadfruit trees grow 10–20 meters tall with spreading crowns and large, glossy leaves. They thrive in humid tropical climates with fertile, well-drained soils. The fruit serves as a staple food and is prepared in many ways, including roasting, baking, and frying.

Characteristics of Jamaican Breadfruit Trees

  • Medium to large tree, 10–20 meters tall
  • Thrives in Jamaican tropical climates
  • Produces starchy, edible fruit
  • Spreading crown with large leaves
  • Supports local biodiversity
  • Fast-growing and long-lived

Common Uses

  • Staple food: roasted, boiled, or baked fruit
  • Animal fodder
  • Ornamental planting and shade tree
  • Wood for lightweight construction and crafts
  • Supports cultural and culinary traditions
