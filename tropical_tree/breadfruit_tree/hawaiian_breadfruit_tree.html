Hawaiian Breadfruit Tree
The Hawaiian Breadfruit Tree (Artocarpus altilis var. hawaiiensis) is a tropical tree cultivated in Hawaii for its starchy fruit and its importance in local food traditions.

Hawaiian Breadfruit trees grow 10–20 meters tall with broad, spreading crowns and large leaves. They thrive in tropical Hawaiian climates with fertile, well-drained soils. The fruit is highly nutritious and prepared in various ways, such as roasting, boiling, or baking.

Characteristics of Hawaiian Breadfruit Trees

  • Medium to large tree, 10–20 meters tall
  • Thrives in Hawaiian tropical climates
  • Produces starchy, edible fruit
  • Broad, spreading crown with large leaves
  • Supports local biodiversity
  • Fast-growing and long-lived

Common Uses

  • Staple food: roasted, boiled, or baked fruit
  • Animal fodder
  • Ornamental planting and shade tree
  • Wood for light construction and crafts
  • Supports cultural and culinary traditions
