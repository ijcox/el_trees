Mango Tree
Mango trees (Mangifera indica) are tropical fruit trees native to South Asia. They are widely cultivated in tropical and subtropical regions for their sweet, edible fruit and dense, durable wood.

Mango trees thrive in well-drained soils with full sun exposure and are tolerant of dry conditions once established. They are popular in orchards, home gardens, and landscapes for fruit production and shade.

Characteristics of Mango Trees

  • Evergreen tropical trees with dense foliage
  • Produce large, sweet, fleshy fruits with a single seed
  • Moderate to fast growth, reaching 35–100 feet tall
  • Adapted to tropical and subtropical climates
  • Provide shade, ornamental value, and food

Common Uses

  • Fruit production for fresh consumption, juice, and processed foods
  • Ornamental planting and shade trees in gardens and parks
  • Wood for furniture, tools, and small construction projects
  • Ecological support for pollinators and wildlife
  • Traditional and medicinal uses in some cultures
