Myanmar Teak Tree
The Myanmar Teak Tree (Tectona grandis) is a tropical hardwood tree native to Myanmar and parts of Southeast Asia. It is prized for its hard, durable, golden-brown timber and natural resistance to rot and insects.

Myanmar Teak trees grow 30–40 meters tall with a straight trunk and broad, spreading crown. They thrive in tropical, well-drained soils and full sunlight. Leaves are large, deciduous, and ovate, while the tree produces small, hard seeds.

Characteristics of Myanmar Teak Trees

  • Large, ovate, deciduous leaves
  • Straight trunk with broad, spreading crown
  • Produces small, hard seeds
  • Durable, golden-brown wood resistant to rot and pests
  • Fast-growing and long-lived
  • Native to Myanmar and parts of Southeast Asia

Common Uses

  • High-quality timber for furniture, flooring, and boat building
  • Cabinetry, veneers, and outdoor structures
  • Ornamental planting in tropical landscapes
  • Shade tree in plantations and parks
  • Wildlife habitat in tropical forests
