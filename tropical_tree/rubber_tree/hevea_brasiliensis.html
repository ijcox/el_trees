Hevea Brasiliensis
Hevea Brasiliensis, commonly called the Rubber Tree, is a tropical tree native to the Amazon region, and it is the primary source of natural rubber worldwide.

Hevea Brasiliensis grows 30–45 meters tall with straight trunks and latex-rich bark. It thrives in humid tropical climates with well-drained soils and abundant rainfall. The latex harvested from the tree’s bark is processed into natural rubber used in tires, industrial products, and consumer goods.
