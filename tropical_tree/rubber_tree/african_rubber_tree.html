African Rubber Tree
The African Rubber Tree (Funtumia elastica) is a tropical tree native to West and Central Africa, valued for its latex and contribution to local rubber production.

African Rubber trees grow 20–30 meters tall with straight trunks and latex-bearing bark. They thrive in tropical rainforest regions with high humidity and well-drained soils. The latex collected from this tree is used locally and commercially to produce natural rubber.

Characteristics of African Rubber Trees

  • Straight trunk with latex-producing bark
  • Thrives in West and Central African tropical climates
  • Produces latex for rubber
  • Fast-growing and supports forest biodiversity
  • Medium to large tree, long-lived
  • Native to African tropical rainforests

Common Uses

  • Latex for rubber production
  • Local construction timber
  • Ornamental and shade planting in tropical regions
  • Traditional uses of latex and wood
  • Habitat for forest wildlife
