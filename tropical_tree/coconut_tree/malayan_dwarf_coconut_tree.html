Malayan Dwarf Coconut Tree
The Malayan Dwarf Coconut Tree (Cocos nucifera 'Malayan Dwarf') is a small-sized coconut variety from Malaysia, valued for its early fruiting, disease resistance, and suitability for small-scale cultivation.

Malayan Dwarf Coconut trees grow 6–12 meters tall with a compact canopy. They thrive in tropical climates with well-drained soils and full sunlight. This variety is particularly favored in plantations and home gardens for its manageable size and reliable production.

Characteristics of Malayan Dwarf Coconut Trees

  • Short, compact tree
  • Early and reliable fruiting
  • Thrives in tropical climates
  • Disease-resistant and hardy
  • Suitable for small gardens and plantations
  • Manageable size for harvesting and maintenance

Common Uses

  • Fresh coconut consumption
  • Coconut oil and milk production
  • Small-scale plantations and home gardens
  • Ornamental planting
  • Food products such as beverages, desserts, and preserves
