Tall Coconut Tree
The Tall Coconut Tree (Cocos nucifera 'Tall') is a traditional coconut variety known for its height and high fruit yield, commonly found in tropical coastal regions.

Tall Coconut trees can grow up to 30 meters with slender, straight trunks and large canopies. They thrive in tropical coastal climates with well-drained sandy soils. This variety produces abundant coconuts suitable for fresh consumption, oil, and copra production.

Characteristics of Tall Coconut Trees

  • Very tall, straight trunk
  • Large, spreading canopy
  • High fruit yield
  • Thrives in tropical coastal climates
  • Deep root system for stability
  • Traditional variety widely cultivated

Common Uses

  • Fresh coconuts for drinking and cooking
  • Coconut oil and copra production
  • Construction materials (leaves and trunks)
  • Ornamental planting in coastal areas
  • Food products such as desiccated coconut and milk
