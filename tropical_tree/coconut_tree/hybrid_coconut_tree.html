Hybrid Coconut Tree
The Hybrid Coconut Tree (Cocos nucifera 'Hybrid') is a cultivated variety resulting from the cross between Tall and Dwarf coconut types, combining high yield with early fruiting.

Hybrid Coconut trees grow 12–20 meters tall with a robust trunk and dense canopy. They thrive in tropical climates with well-drained soils and full sunlight. This variety is preferred commercially for its balance of height, early production, and abundant fruit yield.

Characteristics of Hybrid Coconut Trees

  • Medium to tall tree height
  • Combines traits of Tall and Dwarf varieties
  • Early and high fruit yield
  • Thrives in tropical climates
  • Moderate to fast growth rate
  • Popular for commercial cultivation

Common Uses

  • Fresh coconut consumption
  • Oil and milk production
  • Commercial plantations and export
  • Ornamental and landscaping use
  • Food products such as desserts, beverages, and preserves
