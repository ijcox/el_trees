West African Coconut Tree
The West African Coconut Tree (Cocos nucifera 'West African') is a tropical coconut variety adapted to the coastal regions of West Africa, valued for its resilience and reliable fruit production.

West African Coconut trees grow 15–25 meters tall with a sturdy trunk and wide canopy. They thrive in tropical coastal climates with sandy, well-drained soils. This variety is cultivated both for local consumption and commercial purposes due to its adaptability and consistent fruiting.

Characteristics of West African Coconut Trees

  • Tall tree with strong trunk and wide canopy
  • Adapted to West African coastal climates
  • Reliable and abundant fruit production
  • Thrives in sandy, well-drained soils
  • Moderate to fast growth rate
  • Resilient to local environmental conditions

Common Uses

  • Fresh coconut consumption
  • Coconut oil and milk production
  • Construction and palm-based products
  • Commercial cultivation for local and export markets
  • Ornamental planting in coastal areas
