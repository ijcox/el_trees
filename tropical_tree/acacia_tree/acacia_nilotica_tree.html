Acacia Nilotica Tree
The Acacia Nilotica Tree (Acacia nilotica), commonly called Gum Arabic Tree or Babul, is a thorny hardwood tree native to Africa and the Indian subcontinent. It is valued for its hard wood, gum production, and ornamental yellow flowers.

Acacia Nilotica trees grow 5–20 meters tall with a spreading crown and thorny branches. They have bipinnately compound leaves and produce small, bright yellow, fragrant flowers in dense clusters. The tree thrives in well-drained soils with full sun and is drought-tolerant.

Characteristics of Acacia Nilotica Trees

  • Bipinnately compound leaves
  • Small, bright yellow, fragrant flowers in dense clusters
  • Spreading crown with thorny branches
  • Drought-tolerant and adaptable to dry soils
  • Medium-sized, fast-growing tree
  • Native to Africa and the Indian subcontinent

Common Uses

  • Production of gum arabic
  • Timber for furniture and construction
  • Ornamental planting in gardens and parks
  • Soil stabilization and erosion control
  • Wildlife habitat and fodder for livestock
