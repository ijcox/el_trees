Indian Rosewood Tree
The Indian Rosewood Tree (Dalbergia latifolia) is a tropical hardwood species native to the Indian subcontinent, known for its strength, durability, and rich brown coloration.

Indian Rosewood trees grow up to 20–25 meters tall with a straight trunk and broad canopy. They thrive in tropical and subtropical climates with well-drained soils. This species is widely used as a more sustainable alternative to Brazilian rosewood.

Characteristics of Indian Rosewood Trees

  • Dark brown wood with purple or black streaks
  • Strong, dense, and durable hardwood
  • Moderately coarse but attractive grain
  • Native to India and surrounding regions
  • More sustainable than some other rosewoods
  • Resistant to decay and pests

Common Uses

  • Furniture and cabinetry
  • Musical instruments (guitars, fingerboards)
  • Flooring and decorative veneers
  • Carvings and turned objects
  • Construction and specialty woodwork
