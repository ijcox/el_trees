Madagascar Rosewood Tree
The Madagascar Rosewood Tree (Dalbergia maritima) is a rare tropical hardwood native to Madagascar, prized for its deep red coloration and fine grain.

Madagascar Rosewood trees typically grow to 15–25 meters tall with a straight trunk and dense canopy. They thrive in tropical environments with well-drained soils and seasonal rainfall. Due to illegal logging and habitat loss, this species is highly protected and considered endangered.

Characteristics of Madagascar Rosewood Trees

  • Deep red to reddish-brown heartwood
  • Fine, smooth grain with high polish potential
  • Dense and durable hardwood
  • Native to Madagascar
  • Slow-growing and rare
  • Highly valued and protected species

Common Uses

  • Luxury furniture and fine cabinetry
  • Decorative veneers and paneling
  • High-end musical instruments
  • Carvings and artistic woodwork
  • Collector-grade materials
