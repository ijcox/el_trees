European Olive Tree
The European Olive Tree (Olea europaea) is native to the Mediterranean region and is widely cultivated for its olives and olive oil.

European Olive trees grow 8–15 meters tall with a gnarled, twisted trunk and a broad, rounded crown. They thrive in well-drained, alkaline soils and full sunlight. Leaves are evergreen, narrow, and silvery-green, and the tree produces small green to black olives.

Characteristics of European Olive Trees

  • Evergreen, narrow, silvery-green leaves
  • Gnarled, twisted trunk with a broad, rounded crown
  • Produces green to black olives
  • Slow-growing, long-lived tree
  • Thrives in well-drained, alkaline soils and full sun
  • Native to the Mediterranean region

Common Uses

  • Olives for table consumption
  • Olive oil production
  • Ornamental planting in gardens and landscapes
  • Wildlife habitat for birds and insects
  • Traditional Mediterranean landscaping and cultural significance
