Peepal Tree
The Peepal Tree is a sacred fig species known for its cultural, spiritual, and ecological importance across South Asia.

The Peepal Tree is widely regarded as one of the most sacred trees in Hinduism, Buddhism, and Jainism. It is valued for its heart-shaped leaves, long lifespan, and ability to provide continuous oxygen production through photosynthesis.

It is commonly planted near temples and is often associated with meditation and spiritual practice.

Characteristics of Peepal Tree

  • Large deciduous fig tree
  • Heart-shaped leaves with long tapering tips
  • Strong aerial root system in mature trees
  • Long lifespan and fast canopy spread
  • Thrives in tropical and subtropical climates
  • Highly oxygen-producing tree

Common Uses

  • Religious and spiritual worship
  • Temple and roadside planting
  • Shade provision
  • Traditional medicine
  • Environmental and ecological benefits
