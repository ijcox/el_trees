Sacred Fig Hybrid
Sacred Fig Hybrid is a cultivated form of Ficus religiosa and related figs designed for improved resilience and adaptability.

Sacred Fig Hybrids are developed through selective propagation techniques combining traits from different Ficus religiosa lineages. They aim to preserve the spiritual and cultural significance of the sacred fig while improving growth performance and environmental tolerance.

These hybrids are commonly used in landscaping, conservation projects, and temple planting initiatives where durability is important.

Characteristics of Sacred Fig Hybrid

  • Enhanced environmental adaptability
  • Faster growth than wild varieties
  • Maintains sacred fig leaf structure
  • Improved disease resistance
  • Suitable for urban environments
  • Mixed genetic lineage

Common Uses

  • Urban landscaping
  • Temple and sacred grove planting
  • Ecological restoration
  • Ornamental planting
  • Conservation programs
