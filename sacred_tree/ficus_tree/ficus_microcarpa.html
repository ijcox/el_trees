Ficus Microcarpa
Ficus Microcarpa is a versatile evergreen fig tree commonly used in landscaping and bonsai cultivation.

Ficus microcarpa is native to Southeast Asia and is widely planted in tropical and subtropical regions around the world. It is especially popular for its dense canopy, strong root system, and adaptability to pruning, making it ideal for bonsai.

It is also used in urban landscaping due to its resilience and ability to tolerate pollution and varied soil conditions.

Characteristics of Ficus Microcarpa

  • Evergreen fig species
  • Dense, rounded canopy
  • Strong aerial root formation
  • Highly adaptable to pruning
  • Tolerates urban environments
  • Common bonsai species

Common Uses

  • Bonsai cultivation
  • Urban landscaping
  • Street and park planting
  • Ornamental shade tree
  • Indoor decorative plant (young specimens)
