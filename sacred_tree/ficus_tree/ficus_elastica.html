Ficus Elastica
Ficus Elastica, commonly known as the Rubber Tree, is a popular ornamental plant valued for its large glossy leaves.

Ficus elastica is native to Southeast Asia and is widely grown as both an indoor and outdoor ornamental plant. It is known for its thick, glossy leaves and its ability to grow into a large tree in suitable tropical conditions.

Historically, it was also used as a source of natural rubber, though this has largely been replaced by Hevea brasiliensis.

Characteristics of Ficus Elastica

  • Large evergreen fig species
  • Thick, glossy, dark green leaves
  • Produces aerial roots in mature trees
  • Fast-growing in tropical climates
  • Can be grown indoors as a houseplant
  • Strong structural trunk development

Common Uses

  • Indoor ornamental plant
  • Landscape tree in tropical regions
  • Air purification (houseplant use)
  • Decorative foliage plant
  • Historical rubber production
