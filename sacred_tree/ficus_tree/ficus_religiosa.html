Ficus Religiosa (Sacred Fig)
The Sacred Fig is a culturally and spiritually significant tree revered across South and Southeast Asia.

Ficus religiosa, commonly known as the Sacred Fig or Peepal tree, is deeply associated with religious traditions including Buddhism, Hinduism, and Jainism. It is especially known as the tree under which Siddhartha Gautama attained enlightenment.

This species is widely planted near temples and sacred sites and is valued for its shade and longevity.

Characteristics of Sacred Fig

  • Large deciduous fig tree
  • Heart-shaped leaves with long drip tips
  • Fast-growing and long-lived
  • Strong aerial root formation in maturity
  • Thrives in tropical and subtropical climates
  • Highly sacred and culturally significant

Common Uses

  • Religious worship and ceremonies
  • Temple and monastery planting
  • Shade tree in public spaces
  • Traditional medicine
  • Cultural heritage conservation
