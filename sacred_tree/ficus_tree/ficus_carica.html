Ficus Carica (Common Fig)
The Common Fig is a fruit-bearing fig tree widely cultivated for its edible fruit and historical importance.

Ficus carica is one of the oldest cultivated fruit trees in the world. Native to the Middle East and western Asia, it is now grown globally for its sweet, nutrient-rich figs.

It has played an important role in agriculture and human diets for thousands of years, appearing in many ancient cultures.

Characteristics of Common Fig

  • Deciduous fruit-bearing tree
  • Produces edible figs
  • Broad lobed leaves
  • Thrives in warm, dry climates
  • Moderate height and spreading canopy
  • Long history of cultivation

Common Uses

  • Fruit production
  • Dry fruit and food processing
  • Ornamental planting
  • Traditional medicine
  • Agricultural cultivation
