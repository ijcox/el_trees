Fony Baobab Tree
The Fony Baobab is a smaller baobab species native to Madagascar, adapted to dry, rocky environments.

Fony baobabs are among the smaller baobab species, typically found in the dry regions of Madagascar. They are well adapted to harsh, arid conditions and often grow in rocky or sandy soils. Despite their smaller size, they are an important part of the local ecosystem.
