African Baobab Tree
The African Baobab is an iconic, long-lived tree known for its massive trunk and ability to store water in arid climates.

Baobab trees are among the most recognizable trees in the world, native primarily to Africa. They can live for thousands of years and develop enormous trunks that store water during dry seasons. They play an important ecological and cultural role across African landscapes.

Characteristics of Baobab Trees

  • Massive water-storing trunk
  • Long-lived species (hundreds–thousands of years)
  • Deciduous in dry seasons
  • Large white flowers
  • Drought-resistant adaptation
  • Iconic “upside-down tree” appearance

Common Uses

  • Traditional food and medicine
  • Water storage in dry regions
  • Ecotourism landmarks
  • Wildlife habitat
  • Cultural and spiritual significance
