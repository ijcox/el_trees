Grandidier's Baobab Tree
Grandidier’s Baobab is one of the most iconic baobab species of Madagascar, known for its tall cylindrical trunk and striking silhouette.

Grandidier’s baobab is native to Madagascar and is one of the most famous and visually striking baobab species. It can grow to enormous heights with smooth, column-like trunks and a flat crown of branches. It plays an important role in dry forest ecosystems.
