Desert Neem Tree
The Desert Neem is a drought-resistant variety adapted to hot, arid environments with very low rainfall.

Desert neem trees are specially adapted to survive in extreme dry conditions where most trees struggle to grow. They have deep root systems and hardy leaves that reduce water loss. They are often planted for desert greening and soil stabilization.

Characteristics of Desert Neem Trees

  • Drought-tolerant evergreen tree
  • Adapted to arid climates
  • Deep root system
  • Hardy leaf structure
  • Slow to moderate growth
  • Soil stabilization ability

Common Uses

  • Desert afforestation
  • Soil erosion control
  • Windbreak planting
  • Dryland farming systems
  • Environmental restoration
