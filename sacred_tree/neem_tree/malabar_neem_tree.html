Malabar Neem Tree
The Malabar Neem is a fast-growing variety of neem known for its rapid timber production and strong adaptability.

Malabar neem trees are widely cultivated for timber, shade, and reforestation projects due to their extremely fast growth rate. They resemble the traditional neem but are often taller and more uniform in plantation settings. They are commonly used in commercial forestry.

Characteristics of Malabar Neem Trees

  • Fast-growing evergreen tree
  • Commercial timber species
  • Strong drought tolerance
  • Tall uniform growth habit
  • Light shade canopy
  • Adapted for plantations

Common Uses

  • Timber plantations
  • Reforestation projects
  • Shade tree farming
  • Agroforestry systems
  • Commercial wood production
