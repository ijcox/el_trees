Indian Neem Tree
The Indian Neem is a fast-growing evergreen tree known for its medicinal properties and strong drought tolerance.

Neem trees are widely valued across tropical and subtropical regions for their resilience and traditional medicinal uses. They produce small white flowers and bitter leaves that are commonly used in herbal practices. The Indian Neem is the most well-known and widely distributed variety.

Characteristics of Neem Trees

  • Evergreen tropical tree
  • Highly drought tolerant
  • Medicinally valued leaves and bark
  • Small white flowers
  • Fast-growing species
  • Long-lived shade tree

Common Uses

  • Traditional medicine
  • Natural pest control
  • Shade tree planting
  • Reforestation projects
  • Rural landscaping
