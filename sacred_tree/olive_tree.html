Olive Tree
Olive trees (Olea europaea) are evergreen trees native to the Mediterranean region. They are highly valued for their fruit (olives) and oil, and for their hardy, drought-tolerant nature.

Olive trees thrive in dry, rocky soils with full sun exposure. They are cultivated worldwide in Mediterranean climates for fruit production, ornamental use, and landscaping.

Characteristics of Olive Trees

  • Evergreen trees with narrow, gray-green leaves
  • Small, oval fruit (olives) harvested for food and oil
  • Slow to moderate growth rate with long lifespan
  • Drought-tolerant and adapted to Mediterranean climates
  • Provide shade, fruit, and ornamental value

Common Uses

  • Fruit production for eating and olive oil extraction
  • Ornamental planting and landscaping in warm climates
  • Timber for furniture and small woodworking projects
  • Shade and aesthetic enhancement in gardens and streets
  • Ecological support for pollinators and wildlife
