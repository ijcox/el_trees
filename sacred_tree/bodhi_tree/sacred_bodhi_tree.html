Sacred Bodhi Tree
The Sacred Bodhi Tree is a revered fig tree species associated with enlightenment and spiritual tradition.

The Bodhi tree is traditionally identified as a variety of Ficus religiosa under which Siddhartha Gautama attained enlightenment. It holds deep religious significance in Buddhism, Hinduism, and Jainism, symbolizing wisdom and awakening.

These trees are often cultivated and protected near temples and sacred sites across Asia.

Characteristics of Sacred Bodhi Tree

  • Large deciduous fig tree
  • Heart-shaped leaves with elongated tips
  • Aerial root system in mature trees
  • Long lifespan and spiritual symbolism
  • Thrives in tropical and subtropical climates
  • Often planted near temples

Common Uses

  • Religious and spiritual worship
  • Temple and monastery planting
  • Shade and ornamental use
  • Traditional cultural ceremonies
  • Environmental conservation planting
