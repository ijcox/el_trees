Ficus Religiosa Hybrid
Ficus Religiosa Hybrid is a cultivated cross-form designed to combine resilience, adaptability, and traditional sacred characteristics.

Ficus religiosa hybrid varieties are developed through selective breeding or controlled propagation techniques to enhance growth performance while preserving the sacred traits of the Bodhi tree. These hybrids are often used in ecological, ornamental, and conservation applications.

They maintain the spiritual symbolism of the original species while offering improved adaptability to different environments.

Characteristics of Ficus Religiosa Hybrid

  • Improved environmental adaptability
  • Faster growth rate than wild types
  • Retains sacred Bodhi leaf structure
  • Enhanced disease resistance
  • Suitable for urban planting
  • Genetically mixed lineage

Common Uses

  • Urban landscaping
  • Temple and sacred grove planting
  • Ecological restoration projects
  • Botanical gardens
  • Conservation and research programs
