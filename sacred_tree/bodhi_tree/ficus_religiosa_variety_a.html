Ficus Religiosa Variety A
Ficus Religiosa Variety A is a cultivated form of the sacred Bodhi tree known for its stable growth and traditional form.

This variety of Ficus religiosa is selected for its consistent leaf shape, strong root system, and adaptability to cultivation in temple environments and landscaped gardens. It maintains the spiritual significance of the species while offering improved uniformity for planting programs.

It is commonly grown in nurseries and used for religious planting projects across South and Southeast Asia.

Characteristics of Variety A

  • Stable and uniform growth pattern
  • Heart-shaped leaves with long drip tips
  • Strong root establishment
  • Adapted for cultivated environments
  • Medium to large canopy development
  • Common in temple plantations

Common Uses

  • Religious and temple planting
  • Ornamental landscaping
  • Cultural heritage conservation
  • Shade provision in sacred spaces
  • Botanical garden collections
