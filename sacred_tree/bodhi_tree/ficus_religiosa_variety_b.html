Ficus Religiosa Variety B
Ficus Religiosa Variety B is a cultivated variation of the sacred Bodhi tree selected for resilience and environmental adaptability.

This variety of Ficus religiosa is developed for improved tolerance to different soil types and climatic conditions while maintaining the traditional characteristics of the sacred Bodhi tree. It is often used in reforestation and religious landscaping projects.

It retains the cultural and spiritual importance of the species while offering enhanced survivability in diverse environments.

Characteristics of Variety B

  • High environmental adaptability
  • Moderate to fast growth rate
  • Strong drought tolerance
  • Heart-shaped leaves with long tips
  • Flexible soil tolerance
  • Suitable for restoration projects

Common Uses

  • Reforestation projects
  • Temple and sacred site planting
  • Urban greening
  • Ornamental landscaping
  • Ecological restoration
