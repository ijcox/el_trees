Bo Tree Clones
Bo Tree Clones are genetically identical propagations of the sacred Bodhi tree used for preservation and cultural continuity.

Bo tree clones are cultivated from cuttings or tissue samples of revered Ficus religiosa specimens. They are used to preserve historically significant trees and maintain genetic consistency across sacred plantings.

These clones are especially important in temple conservation efforts and botanical preservation programs.

Characteristics of Bo Tree Clones

  • Genetically identical to parent trees
  • Uniform growth patterns
  • Preserves sacred lineage
  • Developed through cutting or tissue culture
  • Used in conservation programs
  • Stable morphological traits

Common Uses

  • Temple and monastery planting
  • Religious heritage preservation
  • Botanical conservation
  • Landscape restoration
  • Cultural replication of sacred trees
