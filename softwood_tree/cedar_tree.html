Cedar Tree
Cedar trees are large, aromatic evergreen conifers belonging to the genus Cedrus. They are valued for their fragrant, durable wood and ornamental appeal in landscapes and gardens.

Cedar trees are native to the mountains of the Mediterranean region and the Himalayas. They are often used in construction, furniture, and as decorative trees in parks and gardens due to their straight trunks and beautiful foliage.

Characteristics of Cedar Trees

  • Evergreen conifers with needle-like leaves
  • Aromatic and durable wood
  • Adapted to mountainous regions and temperate climates
  • Can grow very tall with straight trunks and conical shapes
  • Provide habitat for wildlife and are used in ornamental landscaping

Common Uses

  • Construction and furniture making
  • Closets, chests, and aromatic wood products
  • Ornamental planting in parks and gardens
  • Timber for outdoor and indoor applications
  • Windbreaks and soil stabilization in mountainous regions
