Tamai Larch Tree
The Tamai Larch (Larix × marschlinsii) is a hybrid larch species developed by crossing the European Larch and the Japanese Larch. It combines the fast growth of Japanese Larch with the durability of European Larch.

Tamai Larch trees grow quickly and develop a straight trunk with a tall, conical crown. Like other larch species, they produce soft needles that grow in clusters and turn golden before falling in autumn. Because of its vigor and adaptability, the Tamai Larch is widely planted in commercial forestry plantations.

Characteristics of Tamai Larch Trees

  • Hybrid between European Larch and Japanese Larch
  • Fast-growing deciduous conifer
  • Soft green needles growing in clusters
  • Needles turn golden yellow in autumn
  • Strong, straight trunk suitable for timber production

Common Uses

  • Commercial forestry and timber production
  • Construction lumber and outdoor structures
  • Reforestation and plantation forests
  • Windbreaks and soil stabilization
  • Wildlife habitat in managed forests
