Leyland Cypress Tree
The Leyland Cypress Tree (×Cupressocyparis leylandii) is a fast-growing evergreen conifer hybrid commonly used in landscaping. It is a cross between the Monterey Cypress and the Nootka Cypress.

Leyland Cypress trees can grow 20–30 meters tall and develop a dense, pyramidal crown. They are highly valued for their rapid growth, making them ideal for privacy screens, windbreaks, and large hedges. The foliage is soft, bright green, and retains its color throughout the year.

Characteristics of Leyland Cypress Trees

  • Evergreen conifer with soft, bright green foliage
  • Fast-growing and dense growth habit
  • Pyramidal crown that can be pruned into hedges
  • Hybrid of Monterey Cypress and Nootka Cypress
  • Widely used in temperate climates for landscaping

Common Uses

  • Privacy screens and hedges
  • Windbreaks for gardens and farms
  • Specimen trees in parks and large landscapes
  • Ornamental planting along driveways and property borders
  • Timber for limited outdoor use
