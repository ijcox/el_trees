Italian Cypress Tree
The Italian Cypress Tree (Cupressus sempervirens) is an evergreen conifer native to the Mediterranean region. It is renowned for its tall, narrow, columnar shape and is often used in formal landscapes and gardens.

Italian Cypress trees typically grow 15–25 meters tall and can live for hundreds of years. They thrive in well-drained soils and full sun and are highly drought-tolerant. This species is commonly planted along walkways, property borders, and in Mediterranean-style gardens.

Characteristics of Italian Cypress Trees

  • Evergreen conifer with dark green foliage
  • Tall, slender, and columnar growth habit
  • Needles scale-like and densely packed
  • Highly drought-tolerant and hardy in warm climates
  • Native to the Mediterranean region

Common Uses

  • Formal landscaping and garden borders
  • Ornamental planting along pathways and entrances
  • Windbreaks in Mediterranean climates
  • Symbolic plantings in cemeteries and historical sites
  • Container planting for terraces and patios
