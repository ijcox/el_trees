Deodar Cedar Tree
The Deodar Cedar (Cedrus deodara) is a tall, graceful evergreen native to the Himalayas. It is known for its pendulous branches, soft bluish-green needles, and aromatic wood.

Deodar Cedars can reach up to 60 meters tall in the wild, with a pyramidal shape and drooping branches. They produce barrel-shaped cones and thrive in mountainous regions with well-drained soils and cool climates. These trees prefer full sunlight and can tolerate cold winters.

Characteristics of Deodar Cedar Trees

  • Evergreen conifers with soft, bluish-green needles
  • Pendulous, graceful branches forming a pyramidal crown
  • Produce barrel-shaped cones
  • Native to Himalayan mountainous regions
  • Long-lived and highly aromatic wood

Common Uses

  • Timber for construction, furniture, and carving
  • Ornamental planting in parks and gardens
  • Resin and oils for traditional medicine and incense
  • Windbreaks and erosion control in mountainous areas
  • Cultural and spiritual significance in Himalayan regions
