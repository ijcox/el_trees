Juniper Virginiana Tree
The Juniper Virginiana Tree (Juniperus virginiana) is an evergreen conifer native to eastern North America. It is commonly known as Eastern Red Cedar and valued for its aromatic wood, dense foliage, and wildlife benefits.

Juniper Virginiana trees typically grow 10–20 meters tall, forming a narrow, pyramidal crown. They are highly adaptable to a wide range of soils, drought-tolerant, and often planted for windbreaks or privacy screens. The small bluish cones provide food for birds and other wildlife.

Characteristics of Juniper Virginiana Trees

  • Evergreen conifer with dense, aromatic foliage
  • Narrow, pyramidal growth habit
  • Produces small, bluish berry-like cones
  • Drought- and wind-tolerant
  • Native to eastern North America

Common Uses

  • Ornamental planting, hedges, and windbreaks
  • Timber for fence posts, furniture, and cedar chests
  • Wildlife habitat for birds and small mammals
  • Screening and privacy plantings
  • Land reclamation and erosion control
