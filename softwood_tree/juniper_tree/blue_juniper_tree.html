Blue Juniper Tree
The Blue Juniper Tree (Juniperus squamata 'Blue Star') is an ornamental evergreen conifer known for its striking blue-grey foliage. It is commonly used in landscaping, rock gardens, and as a low-maintenance decorative shrub.

Blue Juniper trees typically grow 1–3 meters tall and have a compact, spreading form. They thrive in well-drained soils, full sun, and are highly drought-tolerant. The dense, needle-like foliage maintains its blue color year-round, providing a unique accent in gardens and landscapes.

Characteristics of Blue Juniper Trees

  • Evergreen conifer with striking blue-grey foliage
  • Compact, spreading growth habit
  • Needle-like leaves remain colorful year-round
  • Drought-tolerant and low-maintenance
  • Popular ornamental shrub for gardens and rockeries

Common Uses

  • Ornamental planting in gardens and parks
  • Rock gardens and borders
  • Groundcover or accent plantings
  • Low-maintenance landscaping in urban and residential areas
  • Container planting and bonsai-style cultivation
