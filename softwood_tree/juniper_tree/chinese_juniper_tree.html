Chinese Juniper Tree
The Chinese Juniper Tree (Juniperus chinensis) is an evergreen conifer native to China. It is prized for its adaptability, ornamental forms, and use in bonsai and landscaping.

Chinese Juniper trees typically grow 3–15 meters tall, with forms ranging from upright and pyramidal to spreading and shrub-like. They tolerate a wide range of soils and climates, including urban conditions, and produce small, blue berry-like cones. The species is widely used in gardens, parks, and bonsai collections.

Characteristics of Chinese Juniper Trees

  • Evergreen conifer with needle- or scale-like foliage
  • Varied growth habit: upright, spreading, or shrub-like
  • Produces small, blue berry-like cones
  • Highly adaptable to a wide range of soils and climates
  • Native to China

Common Uses

  • Ornamental planting in gardens and parks
  • Bonsai cultivation
  • Hedges and privacy screens
  • Soil stabilization and landscaping in urban areas
  • Wildlife habitat for birds and small mammals
