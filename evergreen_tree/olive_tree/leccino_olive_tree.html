Leccino Olive Tree
The Leccino Olive Tree (Olea europaea 'Leccino') is an Italian cultivar known for its high-quality olive oil and adaptability to various climates and soils.

Leccino Olive trees grow 6–10 meters tall with a rounded, dense crown. They thrive in well-drained soils and full sunlight. Leaves are narrow, evergreen, and silvery-green, and the tree produces medium-sized, oval green to black olives prized for oil extraction.

Characteristics of Leccino Olive Trees

  • Evergreen, narrow, silvery-green leaves
  • Rounded, dense crown
  • Produces medium-sized green to black olives
  • Slow-growing, long-lived, highly productive tree
  • Thrives in well-drained soils and full sun
  • Originates from Italy, ideal for olive oil production

Common Uses

  • High-quality olive oil production
  • Table olives in Mediterranean cuisine
  • Ornamental planting in gardens and groves
  • Wildlife habitat for birds and insects
  • Traditional Italian and Mediterranean landscaping
