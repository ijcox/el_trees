Japanese Holly Tree
The Japanese Holly Tree (Ilex crenata) is an evergreen shrub or small tree native to Japan, China, Korea, and Taiwan, valued for its small, rounded leaves and dense growth habit.

Japanese Holly trees grow 1–4 meters tall with a dense, rounded crown. They thrive in well-drained soils and full to partial sunlight. Leaves are small, glossy, and rounded, and the tree produces inconspicuous flowers followed by black berries.

Characteristics of Japanese Holly Trees

  • Evergreen, small, glossy, rounded leaves
  • Dense, compact, rounded crown
  • Produces small inconspicuous flowers and black berries
  • Slow-growing, long-lived, and shade-tolerant
  • Thrives in well-drained soils and full to partial sun
  • Native to Japan, China, Korea, and Taiwan

Common Uses

  • Ornamental planting in gardens, parks, and streets
  • Hedges and privacy screens
  • Bonsai and formal garden plantings
  • Wildlife habitat for birds and insects
  • Container planting and landscape accents
