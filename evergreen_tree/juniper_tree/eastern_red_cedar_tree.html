Eastern Red Cedar Tree
The Eastern Red Cedar Tree (Juniperus virginiana) is an evergreen conifer native to eastern North America. It is known for its aromatic wood, dense foliage, and adaptability to a wide range of soils.

Eastern Red Cedar trees typically grow 10–20 meters tall with a narrow, pyramidal crown. They are highly drought-tolerant, resistant to wind, and commonly found in old fields, pastures, and forest edges. The berries are bluish and provide food for birds and wildlife.

Characteristics of Eastern Red Cedar Trees

  • Evergreen conifer with dense, scale-like foliage
  • Narrow, pyramidal growth habit
  • Produces small, blue, berry-like cones
  • Drought- and wind-tolerant
  • Native to eastern North America

Common Uses

  • Ornamental planting and windbreaks
  • Timber for furniture, fence posts, and cedar chests
  • Wildlife habitat for birds and small mammals
  • Screening and privacy plantings
  • Land reclamation and erosion control
