Rocky Mountain Juniper Tree
The Rocky Mountain Juniper Tree (Juniperus scopulorum) is an evergreen conifer native to western North America. It is known for its dense foliage, adaptability to high elevations, and small blue cones.

Rocky Mountain Juniper trees typically grow 5–15 meters tall and form a narrow, pyramidal crown. They thrive in rocky, dry soils and are highly tolerant of cold, wind, and drought conditions. The bluish cones provide food for birds and other wildlife.

Characteristics of Rocky Mountain Juniper Trees

  • Evergreen conifer with dense, scale-like foliage
  • Narrow, pyramidal growth habit
  • Produces small, bluish berry-like cones
  • Highly tolerant of cold, wind, and drought
  • Native to western North America

Common Uses

  • Ornamental planting and windbreaks
  • Wildlife habitat in high-elevation areas
  • Erosion control on rocky slopes
  • Timber and fence posts locally
  • Hedgerows and natural landscaping
