Savina Juniper Tree
The Savina Juniper Tree (Juniperus sabina) is an evergreen conifer native to central and southern Europe and parts of Asia. It is a low-growing shrub or small tree valued for its aromatic foliage and ornamental use in gardens and rockeries.

Savina Juniper trees typically grow 1–4 meters tall, forming dense, spreading shrubs or compact small trees. They thrive in well-drained soils, tolerate drought, and are resistant to cold. The foliage is scale-like and aromatic, and the plant produces small, dark-colored cones.

Characteristics of Savina Juniper Trees

  • Evergreen conifer with aromatic, scale-like foliage
  • Low-growing shrub or small tree habit
  • Produces small, dark-colored cones
  • Drought- and cold-tolerant
  • Native to central and southern Europe and parts of Asia

Common Uses

  • Ornamental planting in gardens, rockeries, and borders
  • Erosion control on slopes and hillsides
  • Low hedges or groundcover in landscaping
  • Wildlife habitat for birds and small mammals
  • Traditional medicinal uses in some regions
