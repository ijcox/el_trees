Cyprus Cedar Tree
The Cyprus Cedar (Cedrus brevifolia) is a smaller cedar species native to the Troodos Mountains of Cyprus. It is valued for its compact form, dark green needles, and adaptability as an ornamental tree.

Cyprus Cedars are medium-sized evergreen trees, reaching 10–25 meters tall, with a pyramidal crown. Their needle-like leaves are dark green and grow in dense clusters, and they produce small, barrel-shaped cones. These trees thrive in well-drained soils and full sunlight, and they are drought-tolerant.

Characteristics of Cyprus Cedar Trees

  • Evergreen conifers with dark green needle clusters
  • Medium height with pyramidal crown
  • Produce barrel-shaped cones
  • Native to the Troodos Mountains of Cyprus
  • Compact and long-lived, ideal for ornamental planting

Common Uses

  • Ornamental planting in parks, gardens, and landscapes
  • Timber for furniture and local construction
  • Resin for traditional uses
  • Windbreaks and erosion control
  • Wildlife habitat in native and non-native regions
