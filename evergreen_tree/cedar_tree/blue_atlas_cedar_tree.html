Blue Atlas Cedar Tree
The Blue Atlas Cedar (Cedrus atlantica 'Glauca') is a striking evergreen native to the Atlas Mountains of Morocco. It is prized for its silvery-blue needles, broad canopy, and ornamental beauty.

Blue Atlas Cedars are large trees with a wide, spreading crown and silvery-blue needle clusters. They produce barrel-shaped cones and prefer well-drained soils with full sunlight. These trees are drought-tolerant and thrive in temperate climates.

Characteristics of Blue Atlas Cedar Trees

  • Evergreen conifers with silvery-blue needles
  • Wide, spreading canopy with sculptural form
  • Produce barrel-shaped cones
  • Native to the Atlas Mountains of Morocco
  • Drought-tolerant and long-lived

Common Uses

  • Ornamental planting in parks and large gardens
  • Timber for furniture and construction
  • Windbreaks and landscape architecture
  • Resin and aromatic oils for traditional uses
  • Ecological stabilization in dry or mountainous areas
