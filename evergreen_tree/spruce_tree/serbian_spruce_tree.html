Serbian Spruce Tree
The Serbian Spruce (Picea omorika) is a medium-sized evergreen native to the Balkan Peninsula. It is valued for its narrow, elegant form and use in ornamental planting.

Serbian Spruces typically grow 15–25 meters tall with a slender, conical crown. They have dark green needles with a silvery underside and produce small, pendulous cones. This species prefers well-drained soils and is commonly planted in parks, gardens, and as a decorative tree in urban landscapes.

Characteristics of Serbian Spruce Trees

  • Evergreen conifers with dark green needles, silvery beneath (1.5–2.5 cm long)
  • Bark is thin and gray-brown
  • Produce slender, pendulous cones 4–7 cm long
  • Adapted to well-drained soils and temperate climates
  • Lifespan typically 150–250 years

Common Uses

  • Ornamental planting in parks, gardens, and urban areas
  • Windbreaks and hedges
  • Timber for small-scale construction and furniture
  • Wildlife habitat for birds and small mammals
  • Christmas tree cultivation
