Monterey Cypress Tree
The Monterey Cypress Tree (Hesperocyparis macrocarpa) is an evergreen conifer native to the central coast of California. It is prized for its dramatic, windswept appearance and dense, dark green foliage.

Monterey Cypress trees typically grow 10–20 meters tall in cultivation, but can reach over 30 meters in ideal conditions. They are highly salt- and wind-tolerant, making them ideal for coastal landscapes. The tree develops a broad crown with irregular branching that often appears sculpted by ocean winds.

Characteristics of Monterey Cypress Trees

  • Evergreen conifer with dense, dark green foliage
  • Broad, irregular crown often shaped by coastal winds
  • Highly salt- and wind-tolerant
  • Produces large woody cones
  • Native to the central coast of California

Common Uses

  • Ornamental planting in coastal gardens and parks
  • Windbreaks and erosion control in seaside areas
  • Specimen trees for dramatic landscape effect
  • Timber for outdoor use, though limited due to rarity
  • Wildlife habitat in coastal ecosystems
