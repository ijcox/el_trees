Chinese Cypress Tree
The Chinese Cypress Tree (Cupressus chinensis) is an evergreen conifer native to China. It is valued for its dense foliage, longevity, and historical significance in traditional Chinese gardens.

Chinese Cypress trees can grow 20–30 meters tall and develop a broad, conical crown. They thrive in well-drained soils with full sun and are highly tolerant of drought once established. This species is often used in temples, parks, and classical garden landscapes.

Characteristics of Chinese Cypress Trees

  • Evergreen conifer with dark green, scale-like foliage
  • Broad, conical growth habit
  • Long-lived tree, often several hundred years old
  • Drought-tolerant once established
  • Native to China and widely planted in East Asia

Common Uses

  • Ornamental planting in parks and gardens
  • Temple and classical garden plantings
  • Windbreaks and hedges in suitable climates
  • Timber and traditional uses in construction
  • Specimen tree in large landscapes
