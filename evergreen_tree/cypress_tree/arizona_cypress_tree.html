Arizona Cypress Tree
The Arizona Cypress Tree (Cupressus arizonica) is an evergreen conifer native to the southwestern United States and northern Mexico. It is highly drought-tolerant and valued for its striking blue-green foliage.

Arizona Cypress trees typically grow 12–20 meters tall with a pyramidal shape. They thrive in arid and semi-arid climates, often on rocky slopes or dry soils. The foliage is scale-like and aromatic, making it popular in landscaping and erosion control projects.

Characteristics of Arizona Cypress Trees

  • Evergreen conifer with blue-green aromatic foliage
  • Pyramidal growth habit
  • Highly drought-tolerant and hardy in dry climates
  • Produces small woody cones
  • Native to the southwestern US and northern Mexico

Common Uses

  • Ornamental planting in arid and desert landscapes
  • Erosion control on dry slopes
  • Windbreaks in hot, dry regions
  • Specimen trees for gardens and parks
  • Timber and fence posts in local areas
