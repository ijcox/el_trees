Mediterranean Cypress Tree
The Mediterranean Cypress Tree (Cupressus sempervirens) is a tall, narrow evergreen native to the Mediterranean region. It is one of the most iconic trees in Mediterranean landscapes and historical gardens.

Mediterranean Cypress trees typically grow 15–25 meters tall and are known for their columnar, upright shape. They thrive in well-drained soils and full sun, and are highly drought-tolerant. This species is often planted as a formal border, in cemeteries, and along walkways in Mediterranean-style gardens.

Characteristics of Mediterranean Cypress Trees

  • Evergreen conifer with dark green foliage
  • Tall, narrow, and columnar growth habit
  • Highly drought-tolerant
  • Scale-like leaves densely packed along branches
  • Native to the Mediterranean region

Common Uses

  • Ornamental planting in formal gardens and parks
  • Tree-lined walkways and driveways
  • Cemeteries and memorial landscapes
  • Windbreaks in Mediterranean climates
  • Specimen trees in large landscapes
