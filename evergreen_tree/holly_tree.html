Holly Tree
Holly trees are evergreen or deciduous trees and shrubs of the genus Ilex. They are native to temperate and subtropical regions worldwide and are valued for their glossy, spiny leaves and bright berries.

Holly trees thrive in well-drained soils with full sun to partial shade. They are commonly used for ornamental planting, hedges, wildlife habitat, and festive decorations.

Characteristics of Holly Trees

  • Evergreen or deciduous trees with glossy, spiny leaves
  • Produce red, orange, or black berries in fall and winter
  • Moderate growth rate and adaptable to a variety of soils
  • Provide ornamental appeal and seasonal decorations
  • Support wildlife such as birds and small mammals

Common Uses

  • Ornamental trees and hedges in gardens, parks, and streets
  • Festive decorations using leaves and berries
  • Wildlife habitat for birds, insects, and small mammals
  • Wood for small woodworking and crafts
  • Erosion control and landscaping in temperate regions
