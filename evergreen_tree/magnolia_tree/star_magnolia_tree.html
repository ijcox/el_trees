Star Magnolia Tree
The Star Magnolia Tree (Magnolia stellata) is native to Japan and is popular worldwide as an ornamental flowering tree. It is known for its early spring star-shaped white or pink flowers and compact size.

Star Magnolia trees grow 2–5 meters tall with a rounded, compact crown. They thrive in well-drained soils and full to partial sunlight. Leaves are small, simple, and ovate, and the flowers appear before the leaves in early spring.

Characteristics of Star Magnolia Trees

  • Small, ovate leaves
  • Rounded, compact crown
  • Star-shaped white or pink flowers in early spring
  • Slow to medium growth rate
  • Thrives in well-drained soils and full to partial sun
  • Native to Japan

Common Uses

  • Ornamental flowering tree for gardens and landscaping
  • Specimen tree in yards and parks
  • Early spring color and fragrance
  • Wildlife habitat for pollinators
  • Container planting for patios and terraces
