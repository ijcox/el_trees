Ornamental Tree
Ornamental trees are cultivated primarily for decorative purposes in landscapes, gardens, and urban areas. They may be valued for their flowers, foliage, shape, fragrance, or seasonal color changes.

These trees are selected and grown to enhance aesthetics, provide shade, or create focal points in outdoor spaces. Ornamental trees can be deciduous or evergreen and are often smaller in size than timber or fruit trees.

Characteristics of Ornamental Trees

  • Primarily grown for aesthetic appeal
  • Can be deciduous or evergreen
  • Often smaller and more manageable than large timber trees
  • May produce colorful flowers, foliage, or bark
  • Adapted to various climates depending on species

Common Uses

  • Landscape design and garden decoration
  • Urban streetscapes and parks
  • Providing shade and windbreaks
  • Enhancing property value and visual appeal
  • Wildlife habitat and nectar sources for pollinators
