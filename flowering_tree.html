Flowering Tree
Flowering trees are trees that produce visible flowers as part of their reproductive cycle. They are valued for their aesthetic appeal, seasonal blooms, fragrance, and sometimes for attracting pollinators.

Flowering trees include both deciduous and evergreen species. Many flowering trees are cultivated in gardens, parks, and urban areas to enhance visual appeal and provide seasonal interest.

Characteristics of Flowering Trees

  • Produce visible and often colorful flowers
  • Can be deciduous or evergreen
  • Flowers may attract pollinators such as bees and birds
  • Often used for ornamental purposes in landscaping
  • May produce fruits or seeds following flowering

Common Uses

  • Garden and landscape decoration
  • Street and park beautification
  • Providing nectar and habitat for pollinators
  • Seasonal interest through blooms and fragrance
  • Occasionally for fruits or seeds in some species
