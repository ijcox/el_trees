Downy Serviceberry Tree
The Downy Serviceberry is a small ornamental tree known for its early spring white blossoms, edible berries, and excellent fall color.

Downy serviceberries typically grow 4–10 meters tall and are native to North America. They are one of the first trees to flower in spring, producing delicate white blossoms before leaf-out. In summer, they develop sweet purple berries that are popular with birds and wildlife.
