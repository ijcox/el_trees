Western Serviceberry Tree
The Western Serviceberry is a drought-tolerant ornamental tree known for its spring blossoms, edible berries, and adaptability to dry climates.

Western serviceberries grow 3–9 meters tall and are commonly found in western North America. They produce white flowers in early spring followed by dark purple berries that are edible and wildlife-friendly. They are well adapted to dry soils and rocky environments.
