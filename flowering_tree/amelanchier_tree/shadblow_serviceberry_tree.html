Shadblow Serviceberry Tree
The Shadblow Serviceberry is an early-flowering ornamental tree known for its delicate blossoms and wildlife-friendly berries.

Shadblow serviceberries grow 3–8 meters tall and are often found in woodland edges and natural landscapes. They produce clusters of white flowers very early in spring, sometimes before the leaves emerge. Their berries ripen into dark purple fruits that attract birds and wildlife.
