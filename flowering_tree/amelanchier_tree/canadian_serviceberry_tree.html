Canadian Serviceberry Tree
The Canadian Serviceberry is a hardy ornamental tree valued for its early spring flowers, edible berries, and cold climate tolerance.

Canadian serviceberries grow 4–10 meters tall and are well adapted to cold northern climates. They produce clusters of white blossoms in early spring, followed by sweet edible berries in summer. Their foliage turns brilliant orange and red in autumn.
