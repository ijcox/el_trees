Mezquita Jacaranda Tree
The Mezquita Jacaranda is a distinctive ornamental variety known for its vibrant blooms and adaptability to warm, dry climates.

Mezquita jacarandas grow 8–12 meters tall with a moderately spreading canopy. They are valued for their resilience in hot environments and their seasonal display of purple-blue flowers. Their structure makes them suitable for both ornamental and shade purposes.
