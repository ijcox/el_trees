Blue Jacaranda Tree
The Blue Jacaranda is a widely recognized ornamental tree known for its vivid blue-violet flowers and airy, fern-like leaves.

Blue jacarandas grow 8–15 meters tall with a broad, spreading canopy. They are famous for their vibrant blooms that appear in late spring to early summer. When in full bloom, they create striking landscapes filled with blue and purple hues.

Characteristics of Blue Jacaranda Trees

  • Deciduous ornamental tree
  • Blue-violet trumpet-shaped flowers
  • Fern-like foliage
  • Wide spreading canopy
  • Fast-growing in warm climates
  • Highly decorative flowering tree

Common Uses

  • Street and avenue planting
  • Ornamental landscaping
  • Parks and gardens
  • Shade tree in warm climates
  • Seasonal floral displays
