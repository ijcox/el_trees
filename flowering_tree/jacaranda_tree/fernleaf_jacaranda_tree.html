Fernleaf Jacaranda Tree
The Fernleaf Jacaranda is a graceful ornamental tree known for its finely divided, fern-like foliage and vibrant purple-blue blooms.

Fernleaf jacarandas grow 8–15 meters tall with a wide, airy canopy. Their delicate leaves give the tree a soft, textured appearance even when not in bloom. During flowering season, they produce clusters of trumpet-shaped blossoms that create a striking visual display.

Characteristics of Fernleaf Jacaranda Trees

  • Deciduous ornamental tree
  • Finely divided fern-like leaves
  • Purple-blue trumpet-shaped flowers
  • Wide airy canopy
  • Fast-growing in warm climates
  • Highly ornamental appearance

Common Uses

  • Ornamental landscaping
  • Street and avenue planting
  • Parks and gardens
  • Shade tree in warm climates
  • Decorative focal tree
