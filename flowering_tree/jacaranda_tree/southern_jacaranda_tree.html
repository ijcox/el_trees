Southern Jacaranda Tree
The Southern Jacaranda is a warm-climate ornamental tree known for its vibrant purple blooms and broad, shade-giving canopy.

Southern jacarandas grow 8–15 meters tall with a wide, spreading form. They thrive in subtropical and warm temperate regions, where they produce spectacular seasonal flower displays. Their blossoms often blanket the ground, creating a colorful landscape effect.

Characteristics of Southern Jacaranda Trees

  • Wide spreading canopy
  • Warm climate adapted
  • Fast-growing species
  • Highly ornamental flowering tree
