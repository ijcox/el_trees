Brazilian Jacaranda Tree
The Brazilian Jacaranda is a tropical ornamental tree known for its stunning purple-blue flower displays and delicate fern-like foliage.

Brazilian jacarandas grow 8–15 meters tall with a wide, spreading canopy. They are famous for their breathtaking seasonal blooms, which can cover entire streets in purple blossoms. They thrive in warm climates and are commonly planted in parks and along avenues.

Characteristics of Brazilian Jacaranda Trees

  • Deciduous or semi-evergreen tropical tree
  • Purple-blue trumpet-shaped flowers
  • Fern-like delicate foliage
  • Wide spreading canopy
  • Fast-growing in warm climates
  • Highly ornamental flowering tree

Common Uses

  • Street and avenue planting
  • Ornamental landscaping
  • Parks and public gardens
  • Shade tree in warm climates
  • Seasonal flower displays
