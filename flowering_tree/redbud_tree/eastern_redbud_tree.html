Eastern Redbud Tree
The Eastern Redbud (Cercis canadensis) is a small ornamental tree known for its striking pink-purple spring blossoms that appear directly on its branches and trunk.

Eastern redbuds grow 6–10 meters tall with a spreading, rounded canopy. They are native to eastern North America and are often one of the first trees to bloom in early spring. Their heart-shaped leaves emerge after flowering, adding seasonal interest throughout the year.
