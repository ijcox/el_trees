Chinese Redbud Tree
The Chinese Redbud (Cercis chinensis) is an ornamental flowering tree known for its dense clusters of pink-purple blooms and compact form.

Chinese redbuds grow 3–6 meters tall with a rounded, spreading canopy. They are widely used in ornamental gardens and are appreciated for their heavy spring flowering display. Their flowers often appear in dense clusters along branches and even on older trunks.
