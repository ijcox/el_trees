Red Horse Chestnut Tree
The Red Horse Chestnut is a hybrid ornamental tree known for its striking pink-red flower spikes and dense rounded canopy.

Red horse chestnuts grow 15–25 meters tall and are often slightly smaller than the common species. They are prized for their vibrant spring flowers, which range from deep pink to red shades. They are widely planted in parks and ornamental landscapes.
