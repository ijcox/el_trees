Common Horse Chestnut Tree
The Common Horse Chestnut is a large deciduous tree known for its bold white flower spikes and glossy conkers (seeds).

Common horse chestnut trees grow 20–35 meters tall with a broad, rounded canopy. They are widely planted in parks and large gardens for their dramatic spring blossoms and dense summer shade. In autumn, they produce spiky seed cases containing shiny brown seeds known as conkers.
