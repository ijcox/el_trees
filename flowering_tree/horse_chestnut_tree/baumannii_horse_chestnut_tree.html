Baumannii Horse Chestnut Tree
The Baumannii Horse Chestnut is a sterile ornamental variety known for its showy double white flower spikes and lack of seed production.

Baumannii horse chestnuts typically grow 15–25 meters tall with a dense, rounded crown. They are widely planted in cities because they do not produce messy conkers. Their double flowers are more abundant and longer-lasting than the common species.
