Fort McNair Horse Chestnut Tree
The Fort McNair Horse Chestnut is a historic ornamental variety known for its strong growth and classic white flower spikes.

Fort McNair horse chestnuts grow 15–25 meters tall with a broad, rounded canopy. They are often associated with historic plantings and institutional landscapes. Like other horse chestnuts, they produce striking spring flower displays.
