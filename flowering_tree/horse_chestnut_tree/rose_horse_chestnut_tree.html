Rose Horse Chestnut Tree
The Rose Horse Chestnut is an ornamental hybrid known for its soft pink flower spikes and attractive rounded canopy.

Rose horse chestnuts grow 15–25 meters tall and are valued for their delicate pink spring blossoms. They are commonly planted in parks and landscaped areas for seasonal color and shade. Their flowers are less intense than red varieties, giving a softer visual effect.
