Jane Magnolia Tree
The Jane Magnolia Tree (Magnolia 'Jane') is a hybrid ornamental magnolia known for its compact form, fragrant white flowers, and suitability for small gardens.

Jane Magnolia trees grow 3–5 meters tall with a rounded, compact crown. They thrive in well-drained soils and full to partial sunlight. Leaves are simple and glossy, and flowers are large, white, and fragrant, appearing in spring.

Characteristics of Jane Magnolia Trees

  • Compact, rounded crown
  • Simple, glossy leaves
  • Large, fragrant white flowers in spring
  • Slow to medium growth rate
  • Thrives in well-drained soils and full to partial sun
  • Suitable for small gardens and ornamental use

Common Uses

  • Ornamental planting in small yards and gardens
  • Early spring floral display and fragrance
  • Specimen tree for landscaping and patios
  • Wildlife habitat for pollinators
  • Container planting for decorative appeal
