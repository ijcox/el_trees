Golden Shower Hybrid Tree
The Golden Shower Hybrid Tree is a cultivated cross variety developed for enhanced flowering, resilience, and ornamental value.

Golden shower hybrids are bred from traditional golden shower species to improve growth performance and flower density. They typically produce abundant yellow cascading blooms and are suited for modern landscaping and urban environments. These hybrids are often more adaptable to varied soil and climate conditions.
