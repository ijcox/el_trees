Western Golden Shower Tree
The Western Golden Shower Tree is an ornamental tropical tree known for its bright yellow cascading flowers and adaptability to cultivated landscapes.

Western golden shower trees grow 10–18 meters tall with a rounded canopy. They are widely used in landscaped gardens and urban planting schemes for their dramatic floral display. They thrive in warm climates and are valued for their ornamental beauty.
