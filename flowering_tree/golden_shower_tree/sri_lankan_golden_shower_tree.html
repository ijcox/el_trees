Sri Lankan Golden Shower Tree
The Sri Lankan Golden Shower Tree is a tropical flowering tree admired for its long, drooping clusters of bright yellow blossoms.

Sri Lankan golden shower trees grow 10–20 meters tall with a rounded canopy. They thrive in warm, humid climates and produce spectacular flowering displays during the dry season. Their cascading blooms make them a standout ornamental tree.
