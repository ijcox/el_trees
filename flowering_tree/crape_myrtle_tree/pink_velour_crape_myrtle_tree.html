Pink Velour Crape Myrtle Tree
The Pink Velour Crape Myrtle is a popular ornamental variety known for its deep pink blooms and striking dark burgundy foliage.

Pink Velour crape myrtles grow 4–6 meters tall with a rounded, spreading canopy. They are highly valued for their rich leaf color and vibrant summer flowers. They provide strong contrast in landscapes due to their dark foliage and bright blooms.
