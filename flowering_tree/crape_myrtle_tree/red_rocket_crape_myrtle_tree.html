Red Rocket Crape Myrtle Tree
The Red Rocket Crape Myrtle is a fast-growing ornamental tree known for its intense red blooms and upright growth habit.

Red Rocket crape myrtles grow 6–8 meters tall with a narrow, upright form. They are especially valued for their striking red flower clusters and strong summer performance in hot climates. Their tall, column-like shape makes them ideal for lining streets and driveways.
