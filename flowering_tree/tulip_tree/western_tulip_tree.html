Western Tulip Tree
The Western Tulip Tree is a regional cultivar known for its strong growth and distinctive tulip-shaped flowers.

Western tulip trees grow 20–30 meters tall with a straight, column-like trunk and broad canopy. They are valued for their adaptability and are often used in large-scale landscaping and reforestation projects. Their flowers appear in late spring and provide important nectar sources.

Characteristics of Western Tulip Trees

  • Deciduous hardwood tree
  • Tulip-shaped yellow-green flowers
  • Tall upright growth habit
  • Fast-growing species
  • Strong timber potential
  • Wide canopy structure

Common Uses

  • Reforestation projects
  • Large landscape planting
  • Shade trees
  • Timber production
  • Ecological restoration
