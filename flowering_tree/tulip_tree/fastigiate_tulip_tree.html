Fastigiate Tulip Tree
The Fastigiate Tulip Tree is a narrow, upright cultivar prized for its columnar form and ornamental tulip-shaped flowers.

Fastigiate tulip trees grow 20–30 meters tall but remain much narrower than standard forms. They are ideal for urban environments where vertical growth is preferred over spreading canopies. They retain the species’ signature yellow-green flowers and golden fall color.
