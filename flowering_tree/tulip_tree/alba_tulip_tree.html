Alba Tulip Tree
The Alba Tulip Tree is a rare ornamental variety known for its lighter flowers and elegant, refined appearance.

Alba tulip trees grow 20–30 meters tall with a strong upright trunk and broad canopy. They are distinguished by paler yellow-green blooms compared to other tulip tree varieties. These trees are valued for ornamental landscaping and park plantings.
