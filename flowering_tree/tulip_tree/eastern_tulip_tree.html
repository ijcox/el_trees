Eastern Tulip Tree
The Eastern Tulip Tree is a large, fast-growing deciduous tree known for its tulip-shaped flowers and tall, straight trunk.

Eastern tulip trees grow 20–30 meters tall and are among the tallest native hardwoods in North America. They produce greenish-yellow flowers with orange markings in late spring. Their leaves turn a golden yellow in autumn, making them a striking landscape tree.
