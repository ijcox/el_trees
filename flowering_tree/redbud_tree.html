Redbud Tree
Redbud trees (Cercis species) are small to medium-sized deciduous trees native to North America, Europe, and Asia. They are celebrated for their vibrant pink or purple flowers that bloom in early spring before the leaves appear.

Redbud trees thrive in temperate climates with well-drained soil and full to partial sunlight. They are popular in gardens and landscapes as ornamental trees for their early spring floral display and heart-shaped leaves.

Characteristics of Redbud Trees

  • Deciduous trees with heart-shaped leaves
  • Small to medium height, typically 20–30 feet
  • Flowers emerge in early spring in clusters along branches
  • Leaves turn yellow in fall
  • Moderate growth rate with rounded or spreading canopy

Common Uses

  • Ornamental landscaping for gardens, parks, and streets
  • Early spring floral display for aesthetic appeal
  • Attracts pollinators such as bees
  • Shade tree for small spaces
  • Occasional use in small woodworking or craft projects
