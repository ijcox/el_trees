Plumeria Rubra Tree
Plumeria rubra is the botanical name for the Frangipani tree, known for its highly fragrant, tropical flowers in many colors.

Plumeria rubra is a tropical flowering plant species native to Central America, Mexico, and the Caribbean. It is widely cultivated around the world for its fragrant, colorful blooms that range from white and yellow to pink and red. It is commonly associated with tropical gardens and cultural symbolism.
