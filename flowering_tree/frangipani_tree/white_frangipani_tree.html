White Frangipani Tree
The White Frangipani is a tropical ornamental tree known for its fragrant white flowers and lush, sculptural branching form.

White frangipani trees are widely grown in warm climates for their highly fragrant blooms and attractive, spreading canopy. They are often associated with tropical gardens, temples, and coastal landscapes. Their flowers are commonly used in leis and decorative arrangements.

Characteristics of Frangipani Trees

  • Deciduous tropical ornamental tree
  • Highly fragrant flowers
  • Thick, succulent branches
  • Warm climate species
  • Long flowering season
  • Popular cultural and decorative plant

Common Uses

  • Tropical landscaping
  • Temple and cultural gardens
  • Coastal ornamental planting
  • Flower garlands and leis
  • Courtyard feature trees
