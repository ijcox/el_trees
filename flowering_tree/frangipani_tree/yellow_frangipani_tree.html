Yellow Frangipani Tree
The Yellow Frangipani is a tropical ornamental tree prized for its bright yellow, fragrant flowers and lush foliage.

Yellow frangipani trees thrive in warm tropical climates and are often used in ornamental landscaping. Their vibrant yellow blossoms are highly fragrant and bloom repeatedly through warm seasons. They are popular in gardens, resorts, and coastal plantings.
