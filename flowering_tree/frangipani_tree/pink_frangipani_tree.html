Pink Frangipani Tree
The Pink Frangipani is a tropical ornamental tree valued for its soft pink fragrant blossoms and lush canopy.

Pink frangipani trees are widely grown in warm climates and are admired for their delicate, pastel-colored flowers. They bloom throughout warm seasons and are often used in decorative and ceremonial landscaping. Their fragrance is sweet and highly recognizable.
