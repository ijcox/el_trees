Red Frangipani Tree
The Red Frangipani is a vibrant tropical ornamental tree known for its deep red fragrant blossoms and lush branching structure.

Red frangipani trees are highly decorative and are commonly found in tropical and subtropical gardens. They produce striking deep red flowers that are both fragrant and long-lasting. These trees are often used as focal points in landscaped areas.
