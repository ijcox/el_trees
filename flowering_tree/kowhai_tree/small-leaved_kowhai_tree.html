Small-leaved Kowhai Tree
The Small-leaved Kowhai is a compact native tree known for its fine foliage and abundant yellow spring flowers.

Small-leaved kowhai trees are naturally smaller and more delicate in appearance than other kowhai species. They produce dense clusters of bright yellow flowers that are highly attractive to birds. Their fine leaves give the tree a lighter, more airy structure.
