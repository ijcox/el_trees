Coastal Kowhai Tree
The Coastal Kowhai is a hardy native variety adapted to wind, salt exposure, and coastal environments.

Coastal kowhai trees are specially adapted to survive in harsh seaside conditions, including salt spray and strong winds. They still produce the iconic golden-yellow flowers that attract native birds such as tūī and bellbirds. This variety is commonly planted in coastal restoration and landscaping projects.

Characteristics of Coastal Kowhai Trees

  • Salt and wind tolerant native tree
  • Bright yellow spring flowers
  • Bird-attracting nectar source
  • Hardy coastal adaptation
  • Medium-sized growth habit
  • Resilient evergreen structure

Common Uses

  • Coastal restoration projects
  • Windbreak planting
  • Native landscaping
  • Wildlife habitat support
  • Beach and shoreline gardens
