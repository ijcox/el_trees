Large-leaved Kowhai Tree
The Large-leaved Kowhai is a robust native tree known for its bigger foliage and striking yellow floral displays.

Large-leaved kowhai trees are more vigorous than other varieties and can grow into substantial shade trees. They produce abundant clusters of golden-yellow flowers that attract birds and insects. Their larger leaves give them a denser, more shaded canopy.

Characteristics of Large-leaved Kowhai Trees

  • Medium to large native tree
  • Broad, large foliage
  • Bright yellow spring flowers
  • Dense canopy structure
  • Bird-attracting nectar source
  • Fast-growing in good conditions

Common Uses

  • Shade tree planting
  • Native landscaping
  • Wildlife gardens
  • Park and reserve planting
  • Erosion control and restoration
