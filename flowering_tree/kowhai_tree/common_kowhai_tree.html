Common Kowhai Tree
The Common Kowhai is a small to medium-sized tree known for its bright yellow, nectar-rich flowers that attract birds and pollinators.

Kowhai trees are native ornamental trees valued for their striking golden-yellow blooms that appear in spring. They are highly attractive to nectar-feeding birds and are widely planted in gardens and natural landscapes. The Common Kowhai is one of the most widespread and recognizable varieties.

Characteristics of Kowhai Trees

  • Deciduous or semi-deciduous ornamental tree
  • Bright yellow tubular flowers
  • Bird-attracting nectar source
  • Small to medium growth size
  • Hardy native species
  • Seasonal spring flowering

Common Uses

  • Ornamental garden planting
  • Wildlife-friendly landscapes
  • Native restoration projects
  • Street and park planting
  • Pollinator gardens
