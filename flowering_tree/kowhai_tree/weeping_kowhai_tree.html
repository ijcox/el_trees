Weeping Kowhai Tree
The Weeping Kowhai is a graceful ornamental variety known for its cascading branches and bright yellow flowers.

Weeping kowhai trees have drooping branch structures that give them a flowing, elegant appearance. They produce vivid yellow blossoms in spring, which hang along the cascading stems. This variety is especially popular in ornamental gardens for its dramatic shape.

Characteristics of Weeping Kowhai Trees

  • Deciduous ornamental tree
  • Cascading “weeping” branches
  • Bright yellow tubular flowers
  • Graceful arching form
  • Bird-attracting nectar source
  • Seasonal spring bloom

Common Uses

  • Ornamental garden planting
  • Feature landscape trees
  • Courtyard and focal planting
  • Wildlife gardens
  • Decorative park trees
