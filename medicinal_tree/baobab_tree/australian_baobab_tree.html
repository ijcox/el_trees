Australian Baobab Tree
The Australian Baobab is a unique baobab species native to northern Australia, known for its bottle-shaped trunk.

The Australian baobab, also called the boab tree, is native to the Kimberley region and parts of northern Australia. It stores water in its swollen trunk to survive long dry seasons and harsh climates. It is an important cultural and ecological species in Aboriginal traditions.
