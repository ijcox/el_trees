Arjuna Tree
The Arjuna Tree (Terminalia arjuna) is a large deciduous tree native to the Indian subcontinent. It is highly valued in traditional medicine, particularly for cardiovascular health, and is often found along riverbanks and moist areas.

Arjuna trees thrive in full sun with well-drained soils and access to moderate moisture. They are commonly planted in landscaping, along water bodies, and cultivated for medicinal bark extraction.

Characteristics of Arjuna Trees

  • Deciduous tree, typically 60–80 feet tall
  • Leaves are simple, oblong, and arranged alternately
  • Flowers are small, yellowish-white, appearing in clusters
  • Bark is rough, grayish, and used in traditional medicine
  • Fruit is a dry, winged drupe
  • Moderate growth rate; prefers riverine or moist soil conditions

Common Uses

  • Traditional medicine: bark extract used for heart health and circulatory support
  • Ornamental planting along rivers and gardens
  • Soil conservation and erosion control along riverbanks
  • Timber occasionally used for light construction and fuelwood
  • Ecological benefits: provides habitat for birds and insects
