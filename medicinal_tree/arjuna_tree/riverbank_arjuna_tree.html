Riverbank Arjuna Tree
The Riverbank Arjuna is a water-loving variety of Arjuna tree commonly found growing along rivers and wetlands.

Riverbank Arjuna trees thrive in moist soils near rivers, streams, and wetlands. They play an important ecological role in stabilizing riverbanks and preventing soil erosion. Like other Arjuna varieties, they are valued in traditional medicine.

Characteristics of Riverbank Arjuna Trees

  • Moisture-loving evergreen tree
  • River and wetland habitat species
  • Strong root system for erosion control
  • Medicinal bark properties
  • Large shade canopy
  • Long-lived tropical tree

Common Uses

  • Riverbank stabilization
  • Floodplain restoration
  • Ayurvedic medicine
  • Shade tree planting
  • Wetland conservation
