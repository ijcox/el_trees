Common Arjuna Tree
The Common Arjuna is a widely found variety of Arjuna tree known for its medicinal bark and adaptability.

Common Arjuna trees are one of the most frequently encountered forms of the Arjuna species. They grow well in tropical and subtropical regions, especially near rivers and moist soils. Like other Arjuna trees, they are valued for their bark in traditional medicine systems.
