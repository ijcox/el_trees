White Arjuna Tree
The White Arjuna is a variety of Arjuna tree known for its lighter bark and strong medicinal properties.

White Arjuna trees are a variant of the traditional Arjuna species, often distinguished by their paler bark. Like other Arjuna trees, they are widely used in Ayurvedic medicine, especially for cardiovascular health. They thrive in tropical and riverbank environments.

Characteristics of White Arjuna Trees

  • Evergreen medicinal tree
  • Lighter colored bark
  • Riverbank and tropical habitat
  • Strong heart-supporting medicinal use
  • Long-lived shade tree
  • Hardy growth in wet soils

Common Uses

  • Heart health formulations
  • Riverbank stabilization
