Indian Arjuna Tree
The Indian Arjuna is a medicinal tree known for its bark, which is widely used in traditional herbal medicine.

The Arjuna tree is a large evergreen species commonly found along riverbanks and in tropical regions of India. It is highly valued in Ayurveda for its bark, which is traditionally used for supporting heart health and overall wellness. It is also an important shade and ecological tree in natural landscapes.
