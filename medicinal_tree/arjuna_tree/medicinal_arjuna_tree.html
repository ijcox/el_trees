Medicinal Arjuna Tree
The Medicinal Arjuna is a highly valued variety of Arjuna tree widely used in traditional herbal medicine systems.

Medicinal Arjuna trees are especially cultivated for their bark, which is one of the most important ingredients in Ayurvedic formulations. They are known for supporting heart health and improving overall circulation. These trees are commonly grown in tropical regions for medicinal harvesting.

Characteristics of Medicinal Arjuna Trees

  • Evergreen tropical tree
  • High medicinal bark value
  • Used in Ayurvedic treatments
  • Riverbank and forest species
  • Large canopy shade tree
  • Long-lived and hardy

Common Uses

  • Pharmaceutical research
