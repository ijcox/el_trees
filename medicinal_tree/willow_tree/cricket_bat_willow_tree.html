Cricket Bat Willow Tree
The Cricket Bat Willow Tree (Salix alba var. caerulea) is a cultivated variety of white willow known for producing high-quality wood used in cricket bats. It is prized for its light weight, strength, and shock resistance.

Cricket Bat Willow trees grow 20–30 meters tall with a tall, open crown. They thrive in moist, well-drained soils, often near rivers and floodplains. The leaves are narrow and green, and the tree produces catkins in spring.

Characteristics of Cricket Bat Willow Trees

  • Narrow green leaves
  • Tall, open crown
  • Produces catkins in spring
  • Lightweight, strong wood with shock resistance
  • Fast-growing and short to medium lifespan
  • Derived from White Willow species

Common Uses

  • Production of cricket bats
  • Timber for lightweight wood products
  • Ornamental planting near water
  • Soil stabilization along waterways
  • Habitat for wildlife
