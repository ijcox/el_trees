Sugar Gum Eucalyptus Tree
The Sugar Gum Eucalyptus Tree (Eucalyptus cladocalyx) is a tall, fast-growing evergreen native to South Australia, valued for timber, honey production, and ornamental planting.

Sugar Gum Eucalyptus trees grow 20–45 meters tall with a straight trunk and dense, rounded crown. They thrive in well-drained soils and full sunlight. Leaves are narrow, evergreen, and aromatic, while the tree produces clusters of white flowers that attract pollinators.

  • Thrives in well-drained soils and full sun
  • Native to South Australia

Common Uses

  • Timber for construction and furniture
  • Honey production for apiculture
  • Ornamental planting in gardens and parks
  • Windbreaks and reforestation projects
  • Wildlife habitat for birds and insects
