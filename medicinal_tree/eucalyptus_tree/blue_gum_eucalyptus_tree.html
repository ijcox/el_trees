Blue Gum Eucalyptus Tree
The Blue Gum Eucalyptus Tree (Eucalyptus globulus) is a fast-growing, tall evergreen tree native to southeastern Australia, widely cultivated for timber, essential oils, and ornamental purposes.

Blue Gum Eucalyptus trees grow 30–55 meters tall with a straight trunk and a dense, rounded crown. They thrive in well-drained soils and full sunlight. Leaves are long, narrow, and aromatic with a bluish tint, and the tree produces white to cream-colored flowers.

  • Straight trunk with dense, rounded crown
  • Produces white to cream-colored flowers
  • Fast-growing, long-lived, and tall tree
  • Thrives in well-drained soils and full sun
  • Native to southeastern Australia

Common Uses

  • Timber for construction and furniture
  • Essential oil production
  • Ornamental planting in large gardens and parks
  • Windbreaks and reforestation projects
  • Wildlife habitat for birds and insects
