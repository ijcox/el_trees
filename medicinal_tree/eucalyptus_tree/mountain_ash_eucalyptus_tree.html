Mountain Ash Eucalyptus Tree
The Mountain Ash Eucalyptus Tree (Eucalyptus regnans) is the tallest flowering tree in the world, native to southeastern Australia, and valued for its towering height, timber, and ecological significance.

Mountain Ash Eucalyptus trees grow 60–100 meters tall with a straight trunk and sparse crown. They thrive in well-drained, fertile soils and full sunlight. Leaves are long, narrow, and evergreen, and the tree produces clusters of white flowers.

Characteristics of Mountain Ash Eucalyptus Trees

  • Evergreen, long, narrow leaves
  • Straight trunk with sparse crown
  • Produces clusters of white flowers
  • Tallest flowering tree, long-lived, and fast-growing
  • Thrives in well-drained, fertile soils and full sun
  • Native to southeastern Australia

Common Uses

  • Timber for construction, furniture, and paper production
  • Ornamental and reforestation planting
  • Wildlife habitat for birds, mammals, and insects
  • Windbreaks and ecological restoration projects
  • Scenic and recreational forestry areas
