Princeton Sentry Ginkgo Tree
The Princeton Sentry Ginkgo is a narrow, columnar cultivar known for its upright form and bright golden fall color.

Princeton Sentry ginkgo trees grow 15–20 meters tall but remain narrow in width. They are ideal for tight urban spaces where vertical structure is desired. Like other ginkgos, they are highly tolerant of pollution and poor soils.
