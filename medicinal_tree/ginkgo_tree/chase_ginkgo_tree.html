Chase Ginkgo Tree
The Chase Ginkgo is a cultivated variety known for its strong growth, classic fan-shaped leaves, and brilliant golden fall color.

Chase ginkgo trees grow 15–25 meters tall with a broad, upright canopy. They are hardy, long-lived, and tolerant of urban pollution and compact soils. In autumn, their foliage turns a vivid golden yellow before shedding.
