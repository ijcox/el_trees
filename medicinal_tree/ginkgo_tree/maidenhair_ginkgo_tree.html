Maidenhair Ginkgo Tree
The Maidenhair Ginkgo is a living fossil tree known for its fan-shaped leaves and brilliant golden fall color.

Ginkgo trees grow 15–30 meters tall and are among the oldest surviving tree species on Earth. They are extremely hardy, tolerant of pollution, and widely planted in cities. In autumn, their leaves turn a striking golden yellow before dropping almost all at once.
