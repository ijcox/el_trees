Eucalyptus Tree
Eucalyptus trees are fast-growing evergreen trees and shrubs of the genus Eucalyptus. Native to Australia, they are now cultivated worldwide for timber, oil production, and ornamental use.

Eucalyptus trees thrive in well-drained soils with full sun and are highly adaptable to different climates, though they prefer temperate to subtropical conditions. They are valued for their durable wood, aromatic leaves, and ecological benefits.

Characteristics of Eucalyptus Trees

  • Evergreen trees with long, aromatic leaves
  • Fast-growing, often reaching 100+ feet tall
  • Adaptable to various soils but prefer well-drained conditions
  • Produce essential oils with medicinal and commercial value
  • Provide shade, timber, and wildlife habitat

Common Uses

  • Timber for construction, furniture, and paper products
  • Essential oil extraction for medicinal and commercial use
  • Ornamental planting in gardens, parks, and streetscapes
  • Shade trees and windbreaks in suitable climates
  • Wildlife habitat and ecological restoration in native regions
