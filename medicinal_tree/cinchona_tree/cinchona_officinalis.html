Officinalis Cinchona Tree
Cinchona officinalis is a medicinal tree best known as a natural source of quinine, historically used to treat malaria.

Cinchona trees are evergreen tropical plants native to South America, valued for their bark which contains quinine. They have played a major role in medicine and global history due to their anti-malarial properties. The officinalis species is one of the classic medicinal varieties.
