Neem Tree
The Neem Tree (Azadirachta indica) is a fast-growing, evergreen tree native to the Indian subcontinent. It is highly valued for its medicinal properties, multipurpose uses, and ability to thrive in arid conditions.

Neem trees prefer full sunlight and well-drained soils. They are widely planted in tropical and subtropical regions for shade, medicinal applications, and environmental benefits such as erosion control and air purification.

Characteristics of Neem Trees

  • Evergreen tree, typically 30–50 feet tall
  • Leaves are pinnate, fragrant, and dark green
  • Flowers are small, white, and fragrant, appearing in clusters
  • Fruit is a small, green drupe turning yellow when ripe
  • Moderate to fast growth rate, drought-tolerant and long-lived

Common Uses

  • Medicinal: leaves, bark, seeds, and oil used in traditional medicine
  • Pesticide and insect repellent: neem oil and extracts
  • Timber and fuelwood in rural areas
  • Ornamental planting and shade trees in streets and gardens
  • Soil stabilization, erosion control, and environmental benefits
  • Personal care products: soaps, toothpaste, and creams
