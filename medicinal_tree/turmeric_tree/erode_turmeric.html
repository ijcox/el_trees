Erode Turmeric
Erode Turmeric is a premium turmeric variety from India known for its rich color, strong aroma, and high quality.

Erode turmeric is cultivated in the Erode region of Tamil Nadu, India, often referred to as the "Turmeric City" due to its large production. This variety is prized for its vibrant yellow-orange color and consistent quality.

It plays a significant role in both domestic consumption and export markets, being widely used in culinary and medicinal applications.

Characteristics of Erode Turmeric

  • Bright yellow to orange color
  • Strong aroma
  • Moderate to high curcumin content
  • Grown in fertile, well-drained soils
  • Highly regarded for consistent quality
  • Important agricultural product in Tamil Nadu

Common Uses

  • Cooking and spice production
  • Ayurvedic and herbal medicine
  • Food coloring
  • Export trade
  • Natural dyes
