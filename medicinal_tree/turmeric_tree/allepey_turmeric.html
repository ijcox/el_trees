Allepey Turmeric
Allepey Turmeric is a high-curcumin variety of turmeric known for its deep orange color and strong medicinal properties.

Allepey turmeric originates from the Alleppey region of Kerala, India, and is considered one of the highest quality turmeric varieties. It is especially valued for its high curcumin content, which gives it potent anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties.

This variety is widely used in both culinary and medicinal applications, particularly in traditional Ayurvedic practices.

Characteristics of Allepey Turmeric

  • Deep orange color with high curcumin content
  • Strong aroma and slightly bitter taste
  • Rhizomatous perennial plant
  • Native to tropical climates
  • Highly valued for medicinal potency
  • Widely cultivated in southern India

Common Uses

  • Ayurvedic medicine and herbal remedies
  • Cooking and spice blends
  • Natural anti-inflammatory treatments
  • Food coloring and preservation
  • Health supplements
