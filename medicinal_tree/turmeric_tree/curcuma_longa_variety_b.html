Curcuma Longa Variety B
Curcuma Longa Variety B is a cultivated strain of turmeric valued for its agricultural consistency and adaptability.

Curcuma longa is the scientific name for turmeric, and Variety B represents one of the cultivated strains developed for specific growing conditions and yield characteristics. This variety is typically selected for reliability, disease resistance, and stable production.

It is commonly used in both commercial farming and research due to its predictable growth and balanced curcumin content.
