Madras Turmeric
Madras Turmeric is a widely cultivated turmeric variety known for its bright yellow color and balanced flavor.

Madras turmeric is commonly grown in Tamil Nadu, India, and is one of the most widely exported turmeric varieties. It has a moderate curcumin content compared to Allepey turmeric but is highly valued for its color and versatility.

This variety is frequently used in cooking, spice blends, and commercial food production due to its appealing appearance and mild taste.

Characteristics of Madras Turmeric

  • Bright yellow color
  • Moderate curcumin content
  • Mild, earthy flavor
  • Grown in warm tropical climates
  • Popular for culinary use
  • Widely exported variety

Common Uses

  • Cooking and spice blends
  • Food coloring
  • Processed food production
  • Traditional medicine
  • Dyeing and natural pigments
