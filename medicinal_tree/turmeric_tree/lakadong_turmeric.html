Lakadong Turmeric
Lakadong Turmeric is a premium turmeric variety renowned for its exceptionally high curcumin content and potent health benefits.

Lakadong turmeric is grown in the Jaintia Hills of Meghalaya, India, and is considered one of the finest turmeric varieties in the world. It is especially valued for its very high curcumin levels, often significantly higher than most other varieties.

Due to its strength and purity, Lakadong turmeric is highly sought after for medicinal, nutritional, and export purposes.

  • Premium quality variety
