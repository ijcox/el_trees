Neem Variety B
Neem Variety B is a cultivated strain selected for resilience and improved environmental tolerance.

Neem Variety B is another selected form of neem developed for adaptability across a wide range of soil and climate conditions. It is valued in planting programs that require hardy, low-maintenance trees with ecological benefits. Like other neem types, it supports biodiversity and soil health.
