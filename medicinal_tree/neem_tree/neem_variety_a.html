Neem Variety A
Neem Variety A is a cultivated neem strain selected for improved growth consistency and adaptability.

Neem Variety A is a selected form of neem developed for uniform growth and reliable performance in plantation environments. It retains the medicinal and ecological benefits of traditional neem while being optimized for cultivation. It is commonly used in experimental forestry and agricultural systems.
