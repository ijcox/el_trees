Melaleuca Cajuputi Tea Tree
Melaleuca cajuputi is a Tea Tree species known for its strong aromatic oil used in traditional medicine and industry.

Melaleuca cajuputi is a tall, fast-growing tree native to Southeast Asia and northern Australia. It produces cajuput oil, which has antiseptic and medicinal properties similar to tea tree oil. It commonly grows in swampy and coastal environments.

Characteristics of Melaleuca Cajuputi

  • Evergreen aromatic tree
  • Produces cajuput essential oil
  • Grows in wetlands and swamps
  • Fast-growing species
  • White or cream flower spikes
  • Adapted to tropical climates

Common Uses

  • Essential oil production
  • Traditional medicine
  • Natural antiseptics
  • Wetland forestry
  • Aromatherapy products
