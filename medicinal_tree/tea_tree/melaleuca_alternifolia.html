Melaleuca Alternifolia Tea Tree
The Melaleuca alternifolia, commonly known as the Tea Tree, is famous for its essential oil with natural antiseptic properties.

Tea trees are a group of Australian native plants in the Melaleuca genus. They are best known for producing tea tree oil, widely used in skincare and natural medicine. Melaleuca alternifolia is the most commercially important species for oil production.

Characteristics of Tea Trees

  • Evergreen Australian native shrub/tree
  • Aromatic leaves rich in essential oils
  • Adapted to wet and coastal environments
  • Fast-growing species
  • Medicinal and antiseptic properties
  • Important commercial oil crop

Common Uses

  • Tea tree oil production
  • Natural antiseptics
  • Skincare and cosmetics
  • Aromatherapy
  • Wetland restoration planting
