Melaleuca Dissitiflora Tea Tree
Melaleuca dissitiflora is a hardy Tea Tree species known for its strong essential oil content and drought tolerance.

Melaleuca dissitiflora is native to arid regions of Australia and is well adapted to harsh, dry environments. It produces essential oils similar to other tea trees and is valued for both medicinal and ecological uses. It is often studied for its resilience in low-water conditions.
