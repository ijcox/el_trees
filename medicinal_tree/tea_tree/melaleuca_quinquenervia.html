Melaleuca Quinquenervia Tea Tree
Melaleuca quinquenervia is a robust Tea Tree species known for its adaptability and broad ecological range.

Melaleuca quinquenervia is a fast-growing evergreen tree native to Australia and nearby regions. It is known for its paper-like bark and ability to thrive in wet, swampy environments. It has also become naturalized in some parts of the world due to its aggressive growth.

Characteristics of Melaleuca Quinquenervia

  • Evergreen swamp-adapted tree
  • Paper-like flaky bark
  • Fast-growing species
  • White fluffy flower spikes
  • Highly adaptable to wetlands
  • Can become invasive in some regions

Common Uses

  • Wetland planting and stabilization
  • Essential oil production
  • Ornamental landscaping
  • Ecological restoration
  • Traditional medicinal use
