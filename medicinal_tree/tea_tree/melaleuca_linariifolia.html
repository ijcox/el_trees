Melaleuca Linariifolia Tea Tree
Melaleuca linariifolia is a Tea Tree species valued for its fragrant foliage and essential oil production.

Melaleuca linariifolia is a small to medium evergreen tree native to Australia. It produces aromatic oils similar to other tea trees and is often used in ornamental planting as well as essential oil extraction. It is known for its attractive white flowers and adaptability.
