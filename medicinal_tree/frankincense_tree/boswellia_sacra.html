Boswellia Sacra
Boswellia sacra is one of the primary sources of frankincense resin, prized for its aromatic and spiritual significance.

Boswellia sacra is native to the Arabian Peninsula, particularly Oman and Yemen. It grows in harsh, arid environments and produces a fragrant resin known as frankincense when its bark is tapped.

This species has been used for thousands of years in religious ceremonies, perfumes, and traditional medicine.

Characteristics of Boswellia Sacra

  • Small, drought-resistant tree
  • Grows in rocky, arid climates
  • Produces high-quality frankincense resin
  • Papery peeling bark
  • Compound leaves with small leaflets
  • Long-lived and slow-growing

Common Uses

  • Incense and religious rituals
  • Essential oil production
  • Traditional medicine
  • Perfumery
  • Aromatherapy
