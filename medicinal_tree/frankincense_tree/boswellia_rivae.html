Boswellia Rivae
Boswellia rivae is a lesser-known frankincense species that produces aromatic resin used in traditional and regional applications.

Boswellia rivae is native to parts of East Africa, particularly Ethiopia and Somalia. It grows in dry, rocky landscapes similar to other Boswellia species and produces a fragrant resin used locally.

While not as commercially prominent as some other species, it remains important in regional trade and traditional uses.

Characteristics of Boswellia Rivae

  • Small to medium-sized tree
  • Adapted to arid, rocky environments
  • Produces aromatic resin
  • Less commercially harvested
  • Drought-resistant species
  • Similar morphology to other Boswellia trees

Common Uses

  • Traditional medicine
  • Regional trade
