Boswellia Frereana
Boswellia frereana is a premium frankincense species known for producing high-grade resin often called "Maydi".

Boswellia frereana is native to northern Somalia and grows in mountainous, arid regions. Unlike some other Boswellia species, its resin is often considered among the highest quality for chewing and fragrance.

It has been used traditionally for incense, chewing resin, and medicinal purposes, and is especially valued in Middle Eastern markets.

Characteristics of Boswellia Frereana

  • Grows in dry mountainous regions
  • Produces premium "Maydi" resin
  • Distinct aromatic profile
  • Small, hardy tree
  • Peeling bark and compound leaves
  • Adapted to harsh environments

Common Uses

  • High-quality incense
  • Chewing resin
  • Traditional medicine
  • Perfumery
  • Aromatherapy
