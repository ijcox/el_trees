Boswellia Carteri
Boswellia carteri is a well-known frankincense-producing tree valued for its aromatic resin and use in traditional practices.

Boswellia carteri is native to regions of Somalia and the Arabian Peninsula. It is often considered closely related to Boswellia sacra and is sometimes treated as a similar or overlapping species in classification.

The tree produces high-quality frankincense resin that has been widely traded and used throughout history in religious, medicinal, and cultural applications.

Characteristics of Boswellia Carteri

  • Small to medium-sized tree
  • Thrives in dry, rocky environments
  • Produces fragrant resin
  • Peeling, papery bark
  • Compound leaves
  • Adapted to harsh climates

Common Uses

  • Incense production
  • Essential oils
  • Traditional medicine
  • Perfumery
  • Spiritual and ceremonial uses
