Red Delicious Apple Tree
The Red Delicious Apple Tree (Malus domestica 'Red Delicious') is one of the most recognizable apple varieties, celebrated for its deep red skin, distinctive heart-like shape, and mild, sweet flavor. Discovered in Iowa in the 1870s, it became America's most widely grown apple for much of the 20th century.

Red Delicious Apple Trees are vigorous, productive trees that produce medium to large fruit with characteristic five-pointed crown at the base. The skin is deep crimson to dark red, sometimes with subtle striping, while the flesh is creamy white, crisp, and mildly sweet with a delicate floral aroma. The variety is prized for its striking appearance, long storage life, and adaptability to a wide range of growing conditions. Red Delicious apples are best enjoyed fresh, as their texture softens when cooked.

Characteristics of Red Delicious Apple Trees

  • Deep red to dark crimson skin with distinctive heart-like shape and five-pointed crown
  • Creamy white, crisp flesh with mild, sweet flavor and delicate floral notes
  • Mid-season ripening (typically September–October)
  • Vigorous, upright growth habit with heavy annual production
  • Excellent storage life; maintains quality for several months under refrigeration
  • Requires cross-pollination with varieties such as Fuji, Gala, or Golden Delicious

Common Uses

  • Fresh eating (classic lunchbox and snack apple)
  • Fruit arrangements and decorative displays (striking red color holds well)
  • Apple salads and cheese boards
  • Juice and cider blends (adds sweetness and color)
  • Dried apple snacks
  • Home orchards and commercial production
