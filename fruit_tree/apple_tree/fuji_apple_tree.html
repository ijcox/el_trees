Fuji Apple Tree
The Fuji Apple Tree (Malus domestica 'Fuji') is a popular apple cultivar known for its exceptional sweetness, crisp texture, and long storage life. Developed in Japan in the late 1930s, it is now one of the most widely grown apple varieties worldwide.

Fuji Apple Trees typically grow 10–20 feet tall depending on rootstock and produce medium to large fruit with a red-pink blush over a yellow-green background. They are prized for their dense, juicy flesh and balanced sweet-tart flavor. Fuji apples ripen late in the season and are excellent for fresh eating, salads, and cider.

Characteristics of Fuji Apple Trees

  • Medium to large fruit with reddish-pink stripes over a yellow-green base
  • Exceptionally sweet, crisp, and juicy flesh with excellent storage capability
  • Late-season ripening (October–November in most temperate zones)
  • Requires cross-pollination with other apple varieties (e.g., Gala, Golden Delicious)
  • Moderate vigor; produces fruit on spurs and shoots

Common Uses

  • Fresh eating (dessert apple)
  • Apple cider and juice blends
  • Fruit salads and cheese pairings
  • Dried apple snacks
  • Home orchards and u-pick operations
