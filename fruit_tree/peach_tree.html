Peach Tree
Peach trees (Prunus persica) are deciduous fruit trees native to China and widely cultivated throughout temperate regions. They are valued for their sweet, juicy fruit and their beautiful pink spring blossoms.

Peach trees grow best in warm temperate climates with well-drained soil and full sunlight. They are commonly grown in orchards and home gardens for fruit production and ornamental value.

Characteristics of Peach Trees

  • Deciduous trees with rounded crowns and spreading branches
  • Produce fragrant pink blossoms in early spring
  • Fruit ripens in mid to late summer depending on the variety
  • Typically grow 10–25 feet tall with regular pruning
  • Require winter chill hours for proper flowering and fruiting

Common Uses

  • Fresh fruit consumption
  • Production of pies, desserts, preserves, and juices
  • Commercial orchard farming
  • Ornamental planting due to attractive spring blossoms
  • Wildlife food source for birds and animals
