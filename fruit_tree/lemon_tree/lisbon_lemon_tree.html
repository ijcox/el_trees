Lisbon Lemon Tree
The Lisbon Lemon Tree (Citrus × limon 'Lisbon') is a classic, commercially important lemon variety known for its smooth skin, high juice content, and vigorous, cold-hardy growth. Originating in Portugal, Lisbon lemons are often considered superior to Eureka for their smoother skin, higher acidity, and better cold tolerance.

Lisbon Lemon Trees are vigorous, upright, medium-sized evergreen trees with dense, dark green foliage that provides natural protection for the fruit. They produce medium to large, oval to elliptical fruit with smooth, thin, bright yellow skin. The flesh is pale yellow, highly acidic, and exceptionally juicy with a sharp, tangy lemon flavor and contains few to no seeds. Lisbon lemons are everbearing, producing fruit throughout the year with peak harvest in winter and spring. They are more cold-tolerant than Eureka lemons, making them a better choice for regions with occasional frost. The trees are self-fertile and are valued for their vigorous growth, consistent production, and resistance to wind and pests.

Characteristics of Lisbon Lemon Trees

  • Medium to large, oval to elliptical fruit with smooth, thin bright yellow skin
  • Pale yellow, highly acidic, juicy flesh with sharp, tangy lemon flavor
  • Few to no seeds
  • Everbearing; peak harvest in winter and spring
  • Vigorous, upright growth with dense, protective foliage
  • Self-fertile; more cold-tolerant than Eureka

Common Uses

  • Fresh lemon juice and lemonade
  • Cooking, baking, and sauces
  • Zest for flavoring and garnishes
  • Preserved lemons
  • Commercial production and home orchards
