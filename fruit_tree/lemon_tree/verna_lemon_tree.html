Verna Lemon Tree
The Verna Lemon Tree (Citrus × limon 'Verna') is a premium Spanish lemon variety prized for its high juice content, intense acidity, exceptional cold hardiness, and late-season harvest. Originating in Spain, Verna is one of the most important commercial lemon varieties in Europe, known for its ability to hold well on the tree and produce fruit well into summer.

Verna Lemon Trees are vigorous, upright, medium-sized evergreen trees with dense, dark green foliage that provides good fruit protection. They produce medium to large, oval to elliptical fruit with smooth, moderately thick, bright yellow skin. The flesh is pale yellow, exceptionally juicy, and intensely acidic with a sharp, clean lemon flavor and contains few seeds. Verna lemons are distinguished by their extended harvest season, with fruit typically ripening from late winter through summer, often holding on the tree longer than other varieties. They are among the most cold-hardy lemon varieties, tolerating lower temperatures than Eureka or Lisbon. The trees are self-fertile and valued for their consistent, heavy production and superior juice quality.

Characteristics of Verna Lemon Trees

  • Medium to large, oval to elliptical fruit with smooth, bright yellow skin
  • Pale yellow, exceptionally juicy flesh with intense, sharp acidity
  • Few seeds
  • Late-season ripening (late winter through summer)
  • Vigorous, upright growth with dense foliage
  • Self-fertile; exceptionally cold-hardy for a lemon

Common Uses

  • Fresh lemon juice (high juice content and intense acidity)
  • Lemonade and beverages
  • Commercial juice production
  • Cooking, baking, and sauces
  • Preserved lemons and marinades
  • Home orchards and Spanish specialty markets
