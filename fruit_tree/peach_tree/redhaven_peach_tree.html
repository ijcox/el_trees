Redhaven Peach Tree
The Redhaven Peach Tree (Prunus persica 'Redhaven') is one of the most popular and reliable peach varieties, prized for its exceptional flavor, attractive red-blushed skin, and cold hardiness. Developed by Michigan State University in 1940, Redhaven became the standard for early-season freestone peaches and remains a top choice for home orchards.

Redhaven Peach Trees are moderately vigorous, productive trees that bear medium-sized fruit with smooth, golden-yellow skin heavily blushed with red. The flesh is yellow, firm, and exceptionally flavorful with a perfect balance of sweetness and acidity. As a freestone variety, the pit separates cleanly from the ripe flesh. Redhaven peaches ripen in early to mid-summer, making them one of the first freestone peaches of the season. The variety is known for its cold hardiness, reliable production, and resistance to bacterial spot, making it a dependable choice for northern and transitional growing regions.

Characteristics of Redhaven Peach Trees

  • Medium-sized fruit with golden-yellow skin heavily blushed with red
  • Yellow, firm flesh with exceptional sweet-tart balance and rich peach flavor
  • Freestone pit separates cleanly from the flesh when ripe
  • Early-to-mid season ripening (typically July)
  • Cold hardy and reliable producer with resistance to bacterial spot
  • Self-fertile; does not require a pollinator

Common Uses

  • Fresh eating (excellent flavor balance)
  • Peach pies, cobblers, and desserts
  • Canning and preserving
  • Freezing for year-round use
  • Home orchards (particularly in northern climates)
  • Commercial production
