Yellow Peach Tree
The Yellow Peach Tree (Prunus persica var. flavicarpa) produces the classic, tangy-sweet peaches that have become synonymous with summer fruit across North America and Europe. Yellow-fleshed peaches are prized for their vibrant color, robust flavor, and exceptional versatility in both fresh and cooked applications.

Yellow Peach Trees are vigorous, productive trees that bear medium to large fruit with golden-yellow skin blushed with varying degrees of red. The flesh is yellow, firm, and juicy with a characteristic sweet-tart flavor profile that provides the classic peach taste recognized worldwide. Yellow peaches are typically divided into two categories: freestone varieties, where the pit separates easily from the flesh, and clingstone varieties, where the flesh adheres to the pit. Yellow peaches ripen from early to late summer depending on the specific cultivar. They are valued for their superior cooking qualities, as their firm texture and balanced acidity hold up well in baking, canning, and preserving. Most yellow peach varieties are self-fertile and reliable producers.

Characteristics of Yellow Peach Trees

  • Golden-yellow skin with red blush; color deepens as fruit ripens
  • Yellow, firm, juicy flesh with classic sweet-tart peach flavor
  • Available in both freestone and clingstone varieties
  • Ripening varies by cultivar (early, mid, or late summer)
  • Vigorous growth habit with consistent annual production
  • Self-fertile; does not require a pollinator

Common Uses

  • Fresh eating (classic peach flavor)
  • Peach pies, cobblers, and baked desserts
  • Canning and preserving (freestone varieties ideal)
  • Peach preserves, jams, and butters
  • Freezing for year-round use
  • Commercial production and home orchards
