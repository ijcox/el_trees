Donut Peach Tree
The Donut Peach Tree (Prunus persica 'Donut'), also known as Saturn or Saucer Peach, produces a distinctive flat, disc-shaped fruit with an exceptionally sweet, almond-like flavor and creamy texture. This ancient Chinese heirloom variety has gained popularity worldwide for its unique appearance, low acidity, and easy-to-eat size.

Donut Peach Trees are moderately vigorous, spreading trees that bear medium-sized, flattened fruit with a distinctive saucer or donut shape. The skin is pale yellow with a red blush, while the flesh is white, exceptionally sweet, and low in acid with a subtle almond-like flavor and a freestone pit that removes effortlessly. Unlike traditional peaches, Donut peaches have a thinner skin and a smaller pit relative to their size, offering more edible flesh per fruit. They ripen in mid-to-late summer and are prized for their delicate, melt-in-the-mouth texture and mild, candy-like sweetness.

Characteristics of Donut Peach Trees

  • Distinctive flat, saucer-shaped fruit with pale yellow skin blushed with red
  • White, low-acid flesh with exceptionally sweet, almond-like flavor
  • Freestone pit removes easily; small pit relative to fruit size
  • Mid-to-late season ripening (typically July–August)
  • Moderate vigor with spreading growth habit
  • Self-fertile; does not require a pollinator

Common Uses

  • Fresh eating (ideal for children due to sweet, low-acid flavor)
  • Fruit platters and snacking (unique shape adds visual interest)
  • Grilled peaches (flat shape prevents rolling)
  • Desserts and fruit salads
  • Home orchards and specialty markets
  • Gourmet gift baskets
