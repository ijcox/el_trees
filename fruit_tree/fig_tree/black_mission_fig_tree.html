Black Mission Fig Tree
The Black Mission Fig Tree (Ficus carica 'Black Mission') is a classic fig variety known for its deep purple-black fruit and rich, sweet flavor.

Black Mission Fig trees grow 3–10 meters tall with broad, lobed leaves and a spreading canopy. They thrive in warm, dry, and Mediterranean-style climates with full sun. The fruit has dark purple skin and deep red flesh, prized for fresh eating and drying.

Characteristics of Black Mission Fig Trees

  • Deciduous fruit tree with lobed leaves
  • Produces dark purple-black figs
  • Rich, sweet red interior flesh
  • Thrives in warm, dry climates
  • High-quality dessert fig variety
  • Popular historic cultivar

Common Uses

  • Fresh eating and gourmet desserts
  • Dried fig production
  • Jams, preserves, and baking
  • Ornamental garden tree
  • Traditional Mediterranean cuisine
