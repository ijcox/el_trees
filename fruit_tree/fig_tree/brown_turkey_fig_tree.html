Brown Turkey Fig Tree
The Brown Turkey Fig Tree (Ficus carica 'Brown Turkey') is a hardy and widely grown fig variety known for its reliable fruit production and mild, sweet flavor.

Brown Turkey Fig trees grow 3–10 meters tall with broad, lobed leaves and a spreading canopy. They thrive in warm temperate and subtropical climates with full sun and well-drained soil. The fruit is medium to large, with brownish-purple skin and sweet pink flesh.
