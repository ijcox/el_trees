Lemon Tree
Lemon trees (Citrus limon) are evergreen citrus trees native to Asia and widely cultivated in warm climates around the world. They are known for their bright yellow, acidic fruit and fragrant blossoms.

Lemon trees thrive in warm, sunny climates with well-drained soil. They are commonly grown in orchards, gardens, and even containers for their fruit and ornamental value.

Characteristics of Lemon Trees

  • Evergreen trees with glossy green leaves
  • Produce fragrant white blossoms with purple tinges
  • Fruit develops throughout the year in warm climates
  • Typically grow 10–20 feet tall
  • Often have thorny branches

Common Uses

  • Fruit used in cooking, beverages, and desserts
  • Production of lemon juice, lemonade, and flavorings
  • Essential oils extracted from lemon peel
  • Ornamental planting due to fragrant flowers
  • Cleaning products and natural remedies
