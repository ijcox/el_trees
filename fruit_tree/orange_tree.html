Orange Tree
Orange trees (Citrus sinensis) are evergreen fruit trees native to Southeast Asia and widely cultivated in tropical and subtropical regions. They are best known for producing sweet oranges, one of the most widely consumed fruits in the world.

Orange trees grow best in warm climates with plenty of sunlight and well-drained soil. They are commonly cultivated in orchards and gardens for their fruit, fragrant blossoms, and ornamental appeal.

Characteristics of Orange Trees

  • Evergreen trees with glossy green leaves
  • Produce fragrant white blossoms in spring
  • Fruit ripens in late fall through winter depending on variety
  • Typically grow 20–30 feet tall
  • Thrive in warm climates with mild winters

Common Uses

  • Fresh fruit consumption
  • Production of juice, marmalade, and desserts
  • Commercial orchard farming
  • Ornamental planting due to fragrant blossoms and bright fruit
  • Essential oils extracted from orange peel
