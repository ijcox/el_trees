Plum Tree
Plum trees (Prunus species) are deciduous fruit trees widely cultivated across temperate regions of the world. They are valued for their sweet or tart fruit and their attractive spring blossoms.

Plum trees thrive in temperate climates with well-drained soils and full sunlight. They are commonly grown in orchards, gardens, and landscapes for fruit production and ornamental beauty.

Characteristics of Plum Trees

  • Deciduous trees with spreading or upright growth habits
  • Produce white or pale pink blossoms in early spring
  • Fruit develops in mid to late summer depending on variety
  • Typically grow 15–30 feet tall
  • Adapt well to temperate climates with moderate rainfall

Common Uses

  • Fresh fruit consumption
  • Production of jams, jellies, desserts, and dried prunes
  • Commercial orchard farming
  • Ornamental planting due to spring blossoms
  • Wildlife food source for birds and animals
