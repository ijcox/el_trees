Sweet Lime Tree
The Sweet Lime Tree (Citrus limetta) is a citrus tree known for its mild, low-acid fruit, commonly consumed fresh or as juice in many warm regions.

Sweet Lime trees grow 3–6 meters tall with dense foliage and relatively few thorns. They thrive in warm climates with well-drained soil and full sunlight. The fruit is less acidic than other limes, making it popular for fresh eating and beverages.

Characteristics of Sweet Lime Trees

  • Medium-sized tree with dense foliage
  • Produces mild, low-acid fruit
  • Fewer thorns than other lime varieties
  • Thrives in warm, sunny climates
  • Light green to yellow fruit
  • Popular in subtropical and tropical regions

Common Uses

  • Fresh consumption and juice production
  • Beverages and refreshing drinks
  • Ornamental planting in gardens
  • Flavoring for mild dishes and desserts
  • Nutritional fruit source in warm climates
