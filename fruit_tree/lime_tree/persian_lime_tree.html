Persian Lime Tree
The Persian Lime Tree (Citrus latifolia) is a popular citrus tree known for its larger, seedless fruit and milder flavor compared to Key limes.

Persian Lime trees grow 4–6 meters tall with dense foliage and fewer thorns than other lime varieties. They thrive in warm climates with full sunlight and well-drained soil. The fruit is widely used in cooking, beverages, and commercial lime production due to its size and ease of use.

Characteristics of Persian Lime Trees

  • Medium-sized tree with dense foliage
  • Produces large, seedless fruit
  • Less thorny than other lime varieties
  • Thrives in warm, sunny climates
  • Mild, less acidic flavor
  • High-yield and widely cultivated

Common Uses

  • Culinary uses in drinks, cooking, and garnishes
  • Commercial lime production
  • Flavoring for sauces and marinades
  • Ornamental planting in gardens
  • Food and beverage industry applications
