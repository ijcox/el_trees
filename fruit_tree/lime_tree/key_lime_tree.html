Key Lime Tree
The Key Lime Tree (Citrus aurantiifolia) is a small tropical citrus tree known for its aromatic, tart fruit, widely used in cooking and beverages.

Key Lime trees grow 2–5 meters tall with thorny branches and dense foliage. They thrive in warm, sunny climates with well-drained soil. The fruit is smaller and more aromatic than other limes, commonly used in desserts, drinks, and culinary dishes.

Characteristics of Key Lime Trees

  • Small tree with thorny branches
  • Thrives in warm tropical climates
  • Produces small, highly aromatic fruit
  • Dense green foliage
  • Fast-growing and productive
  • Sensitive to cold temperatures

Common Uses

  • Culinary uses in desserts, drinks, and cooking
  • Flavoring for pies, sauces, and marinades
  • Ornamental planting in gardens
  • Fruit production in tropical regions
  • Essential oils and fragrance use
