Finger Lime Tree
The Finger Lime Tree (Citrus australasica) is a unique citrus tree native to Australia, known for its elongated fruit filled with pearl-like vesicles often called "citrus caviar."

Finger Lime trees grow 2–7 meters tall with slender branches and small leaves. They thrive in subtropical and tropical climates with well-drained soil. The fruit is prized for its texture and flavor, often used in gourmet dishes and modern cuisine.

Characteristics of Finger Lime Trees

  • Small to medium tree with slender branches
  • Produces elongated fruit with bead-like pulp
  • Thrives in subtropical and tropical climates
  • Distinctive texture known as "citrus caviar"
  • Native to Australia
  • Relatively hardy and adaptable

Common Uses

  • Gourmet culinary use in seafood and desserts
  • Flavoring for drinks and sauces
  • Ornamental planting in gardens
  • Specialty fruit production
  • High-end restaurant and culinary applications
