Valencia Orange Tree
The Valencia Orange Tree (Citrus sinensis 'Valencia') is the world's premier juicing orange, prized for its abundant juice, balanced sweet-tart flavor, and exceptional late-season ripening. Developed in California in the mid-19th century, Valencia oranges became the foundation of Florida's citrus industry and remain the standard for orange juice production worldwide.

Valencia Orange Trees are vigorous, medium to large evergreen trees with dense, dark green foliage and fragrant spring blossoms. They produce medium-sized, round to slightly oval fruit with thin, smooth, bright orange skin that may develop a green blush when mature in warm climates. The flesh is exceptionally juicy, containing small seeds, with a perfectly balanced sweet-tart flavor that defines fresh orange juice. Valencia oranges ripen late in the season, typically from March through July, and are valued for their ability to hold well on the tree, allowing for an extended harvest period. Unlike Navel oranges, Valencia juice does not develop bitterness, making them the preferred choice for juicing. The trees are self-fertile and thrive in subtropical and Mediterranean climates.

Characteristics of Valencia Orange Trees

  • Medium-sized, round to oval fruit with thin, smooth bright orange skin
  • Exceptionally juicy flesh with balanced sweet-tart flavor
  • Contains small seeds
  • Late season ripening (typically March–July)
  • Holds well on the tree; extended harvest period
  • Self-fertile; vigorous grower in subtropical climates

Common Uses

  • Fresh orange juice (the standard for commercial and home juicing)
  • Fresh eating (though less sweet than Navel)
  • Fruit salads and marinades
  • Zest and flavoring
  • Commercial juice production and home orchards
