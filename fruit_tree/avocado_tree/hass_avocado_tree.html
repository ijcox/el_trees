Hass Avocado Tree
The Hass Avocado Tree (Persea americana 'Hass') is the most widely cultivated avocado variety in the world, known for its creamy texture and rich flavor.

Hass avocado trees grow 5–12 meters tall with dense evergreen foliage. They thrive in subtropical and Mediterranean-like climates with well-drained soil and moderate humidity. The fruit has a pebbled dark skin and buttery flesh, making it the dominant commercial avocado variety globally.

Characteristics of Hass Avocado Trees

  • Evergreen fruit tree with dense canopy
  • Produces dark-skinned, creamy avocados
  • Thrives in warm subtropical climates
  • High commercial yield variety
  • Medium to large tree size
  • Requires well-drained soil

Common Uses

  • Fresh eating and guacamole
  • Salads, sandwiches, and spreads
  • Commercial avocado production
  • Cooking oils and food processing
  • Ornamental orchard planting
