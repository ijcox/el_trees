Zutano Avocado Tree
The Zutano Avocado Tree (Persea americana 'Zutano') is a cold-tolerant avocado variety known for its thin skin and heavy productivity.

Zutano avocado trees grow 6–12 meters tall with vigorous evergreen growth. They thrive in cooler avocado-growing regions and are often used as pollinators for other varieties. The fruit has thin, glossy green skin and a lighter flavor with lower oil content.

Characteristics of Zutano Avocado Trees

  • Fast-growing evergreen fruit tree
  • Cold-tolerant compared to many varieties
  • Produces thin-skinned green fruit
  • Lower oil content, mild flavor
  • Heavy annual yields
  • Often used as a pollinizer

Common Uses

  • Fresh eating and cooking
  • Commercial avocado orchards
  • Pollination support for other varieties
  • Salads and light dishes
  • Home garden production
