Pinkerton Avocado Tree
The Pinkerton Avocado Tree (Persea americana 'Pinkerton') is a high-yield avocado variety known for its elongated fruit and rich, creamy texture.

Pinkerton avocado trees grow 5–10 meters tall with dense evergreen foliage. They thrive in mild subtropical climates and produce fruit with a small seed and high flesh-to-seed ratio. The skin is green and slightly pebbled, and the flavor is rich and buttery.

Characteristics of Pinkerton Avocado Trees

  • Evergreen fruit tree with compact structure
  • Produces elongated, high-flesh fruit
  • Rich, creamy flavor profile
  • High yield variety
  • Small seed, large edible portion
  • Thrives in mild climates

Common Uses

  • Fresh eating and guacamole
  • Salads and gourmet dishes
  • Commercial orchard production
  • Home garden cultivation
  • High-value fruit markets
