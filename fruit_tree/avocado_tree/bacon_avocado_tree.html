Bacon Avocado Tree
The Bacon Avocado Tree (Persea americana 'Bacon') is a cold-hardy avocado variety known for its mild flavor and early-season fruit production.

Bacon avocado trees grow 5–10 meters tall with dense evergreen foliage. They thrive in cooler subtropical climates compared to other avocado varieties. The fruit has smooth green skin and a lighter, milder taste than Hass or Fuerte.

Characteristics of Bacon Avocado Trees

  • Evergreen fruit tree with moderate size
  • Cold-hardy compared to other avocado varieties
  • Produces smooth green-skinned fruit
  • Mild, light flavor profile
  • Early-season fruit production
  • Adaptable to cooler climates

Common Uses

  • Fresh eating and salads
  • Guacamole and spreads
  • Home orchard cultivation
  • Early harvest commercial production
  • Ornamental planting
