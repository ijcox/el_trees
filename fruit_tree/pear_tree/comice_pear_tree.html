Comice Pear Tree
The Comice Pear Tree (Pyrus communis 'Comice') is widely regarded as the finest dessert pear, celebrated for its exceptionally sweet, juicy flesh and buttery, melt-in-the-mouth texture. Originating in France in the mid-19th century, Comice is often called the "Christmas pear" for its popularity in holiday gift boxes and gourmet fruit baskets.

Comice Pear Trees are moderately vigorous, spreading trees that produce short, rounded fruit with a distinctive squat shape and a greenish-yellow skin often blushed with red. The flesh is pure white, exceptionally fine-grained, and remarkably juicy with an intensely sweet, floral flavor and a silky, buttery texture that is considered the benchmark for pear quality. Comice pears are delicate and bruise easily, requiring careful handling. They are best enjoyed fresh, as their refined texture makes them less suitable for cooking. The variety is also a favorite of pear enthusiasts for home orchards and specialty markets.

Characteristics of Comice Pear Trees

  • Squat, rounded fruit with greenish-yellow skin often blushed with red
  • Exceptionally sweet, fine-grained white flesh with buttery, melt-in-the-mouth texture
  • Mid-to-late season ripening (typically September–October)
  • Moderate vigor with spreading growth habit
  • Delicate skin and flesh; bruises easily, requiring careful handling
  • Requires cross-pollination with varieties such as Bartlett, Anjou, or Bosc

Common Uses

  • Fresh eating (widely considered the finest dessert pear)
  • Gourmet fruit baskets and holiday gifts
  • Cheese boards (pairs exceptionally well with soft-ripened cheeses like Brie and Camembert)
  • Elegant fruit platters and tablescapes
  • Wine and cheese pairings
  • Specialty home orchards and premium markets
