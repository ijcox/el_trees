Greengage Plum Tree
The Greengage Plum Tree (Prunus domestica subsp. italica var. claudiana) produces exquisite small, green-skinned plums renowned for their intense sweetness, honeyed flavor, and aristocratic reputation. Originating in France, where they are known as Reine Claude, greengages are considered by many to be the finest dessert plum, prized for their delicate flavor and elegant appearance.

Greengage Plum Trees are moderately vigorous, spreading trees that bear small to medium, round fruit with distinctive green to golden-green skin, sometimes blushed with pink or red when fully ripe. The flesh is greenish-yellow, exceptionally sweet, and aromatic with a complex honey-like flavor that is unmatched among plums. Greengages are clingstone varieties, with flesh that adheres to the small pit. Greengages ripen in mid-to-late summer and are best eaten fresh at peak ripeness, when their sugar content is highest. They are more delicate than other plums, with thinner skin and softer flesh, requiring careful handling. The trees are partially self-fertile but produce better yields with a pollinator, and they prefer warm, sunny locations with well-drained soil.

Characteristics of Greengage Plum Trees

  • Small to medium, round fruit with distinctive green to golden-green skin
  • Greenish-yellow flesh with exceptional sweetness and honeyed, aromatic flavor
  • Clingstone; flesh adheres to the small pit
  • Mid-to-late season ripening (typically August)
  • Delicate skin and flesh; requires careful handling
  • Partially self-fertile; yields improve with cross-pollination

Common Uses

  • Fresh eating (considered the finest dessert plum)
  • Elegant fruit platters and cheese boards
  • Greengage preserves and jams
  • Baked desserts (tarts, clafoutis, and galettes)
  • French cuisine and specialty markets
  • Home orchards and heritage collections
