Mirabelle Plum Tree
The Mirabelle Plum Tree (Prunus domestica subsp. syriaca) produces small, golden-yellow plums celebrated for their intense sweetness, honeyed flavor, and legendary use in French cuisine and eau-de-vie. Native to Lorraine, France, Mirabelle plums are protected by Appellation d'Origine Contrôlée (AOC) status and are considered one of the finest plum varieties for preserves and spirits.

Mirabelle Plum Trees are small to medium, hardy trees that bear abundant clusters of small, round to slightly oval fruit with smooth, golden-yellow skin often dotted with tiny red specks. The flesh is golden, exceptionally sweet, and aromatic with a honey-like flavor and subtle almond undertones. Mirabelles are clingstone, with flesh that adheres to the small pit. Mirabelle plums ripen in mid-to-late summer and are prized for their high sugar content, which makes them ideal for sweet preserves and the famous Mirabelle eau-de-vie, a clear fruit brandy. They are self-fertile and productive, thriving in temperate climates with well-drained soil. The trees are also valued for their ornamental spring blossoms and compact size.

Characteristics of Mirabelle Plum Trees

  • Small, round to slightly oval fruit with smooth, golden-yellow skin
  • Golden, exceptionally sweet flesh with honeyed flavor and almond undertones
  • Clingstone; flesh adheres to the small pit
  • Mid-to-late season ripening (typically August)
  • Compact, hardy trees; self-fertile and productive
  • High sugar content; protected AOC status in France

Common Uses

  • Mirabelle jam and preserves (celebrated in French cuisine)
  • Mirabelle eau-de-vie (traditional fruit brandy)
  • Fresh eating (sweet and flavorful)
  • Baked desserts (tarts, clafoutis, and pastries)
  • Dried Mirabelles
  • Home orchards and French specialty production
