Alphonso Mango Tree
The Alphonso Mango Tree (Mangifera indica 'Alphonso') is a premium tropical fruit tree originating from India, celebrated for its rich, sweet flavor and vibrant golden-yellow fruit.

Alphonso Mango trees grow up to 10–12 meters tall with a spreading canopy. They thrive in tropical and subtropical climates with well-drained soil and full sunlight. The tree produces fragrant, juicy mangoes that are highly prized worldwide for fresh consumption and desserts.

Characteristics of Alphonso Mango Trees

  • Medium to large-sized tree with spreading canopy
  • Produces golden-yellow, sweet, aromatic fruit
  • Requires warm, tropical climate
  • Moderate growth rate
  • Native to India
  • Highly prized for flavor and pulp quality

Common Uses

  • Fresh consumption and desserts
  • Juices and smoothies
  • Pickles and preserves
  • Flavoring in confectionery and ice cream
  • Ornamental planting in tropical gardens
