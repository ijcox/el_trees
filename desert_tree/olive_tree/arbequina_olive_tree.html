Arbequina Olive Tree
The Arbequina Olive Tree (Olea europaea 'Arbequina') is a small, compact cultivar originating from Spain, highly valued for its high-quality oil and adaptability to intensive cultivation.

Arbequina Olive trees grow 3–5 meters tall with a compact, rounded crown. They thrive in well-drained soils and full sunlight. Leaves are small, narrow, and silvery-green, and the tree produces small, oval, brown-purple olives known for their delicate flavor.

Characteristics of Arbequina Olive Trees

  • Evergreen, small, narrow, silvery-green leaves
  • Compact, rounded crown
  • Produces small, oval, brown-purple olives
  • Slow-growing, long-lived, highly productive cultivar
  • Thrives in well-drained soils and full sun
  • Originates from Spain, ideal for intensive planting

Common Uses

  • High-quality olive oil production
  • Table olives in Mediterranean cuisine
  • Ornamental planting in small gardens and patios
  • Wildlife habitat for birds and insects
  • Container planting and commercial groves
