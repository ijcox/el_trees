Prosopis Velutina
Prosopis velutina, known as velvet mesquite, is a native desert tree valued for shade and ecological support.

Prosopis velutina is native to the southwestern United States and northern Mexico. It is especially common in desert riparian zones and plays an important role in stabilizing soils and supporting wildlife.

Its name comes from the soft, velvety texture of its young leaves.

Characteristics of Prosopis Velutina

  • Medium-sized desert tree
  • Velvety young leaves
  • Deep taproot system
  • Produces edible seed pods
  • Drought-tolerant and hardy
  • Important for wildlife habitat

Common Uses

  • Shade tree in arid regions
  • Wildlife forage
  • Firewood
  • Soil stabilization
  • Restoration of desert ecosystems
