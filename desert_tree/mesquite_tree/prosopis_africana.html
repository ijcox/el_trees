Prosopis Africana
Prosopis africana is an important African mesquite species valued for timber, fuelwood, and ecological restoration.

Prosopis africana is native to the savannas and dry regions of sub-Saharan Africa. Unlike some introduced mesquite species, it is indigenous and plays an important role in local ecosystems and traditional economies.

It is valued for its dense hardwood and is widely used in carpentry, fuel production, and soil improvement.

Characteristics of Prosopis Africana

  • Medium-sized savanna tree
  • Dense, durable hardwood
  • Drought and heat tolerant
  • Deep root system
  • Nitrogen-fixing species
  • Native to African drylands

Common Uses

  • Timber and woodworking
  • Firewood and charcoal
  • Traditional medicine
  • Soil fertility improvement
  • Rural construction materials
