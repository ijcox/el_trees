Prosopis Chilensis
Prosopis chilensis is a hardy mesquite species valued for shade, fuelwood, and dryland restoration.

Prosopis chilensis is native to South America, particularly Chile and surrounding regions. It has been widely introduced to arid and semi-arid regions around the world due to its drought tolerance and usefulness in degraded landscapes.

It is commonly used for erosion control and as a multipurpose tree in dry climates.

Characteristics of Prosopis Chilensis

  • Medium-sized thorny tree
  • Highly drought-tolerant
  • Deep rooting system
  • Produces edible pods
  • Adapted to arid environments
  • Fast establishment in poor soils

Common Uses

  • Fuelwood and charcoal
  • Windbreaks
  • Soil erosion control
  • Livestock fodder
  • Land rehabilitation
