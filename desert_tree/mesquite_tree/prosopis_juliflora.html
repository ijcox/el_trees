Prosopis Juliflora
Prosopis juliflora is a fast-growing mesquite species widely used for fuelwood and land restoration in arid regions.

Prosopis juliflora is native to Central and South America but has been widely introduced to Africa, Asia, and Australia. It thrives in harsh, dry environments and is known for its rapid growth and resilience.

While valuable for fuel and erosion control, it is also considered invasive in some regions due to its aggressive spread.

Characteristics of Prosopis Juliflora

  • Fast-growing thorny tree
  • Highly drought-tolerant
  • Produces long seed pods
  • Invasive in some ecosystems
  • Deep root system
  • Fixes nitrogen in soil

Common Uses

  • Firewood and charcoal production
  • Land rehabilitation
  • Windbreaks
  • Animal fodder
  • Soil stabilization
