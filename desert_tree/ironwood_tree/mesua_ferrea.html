Mesua Ferrea
Mesua ferrea, commonly known as the Indian ironwood or Ceylon ironwood, is a tropical evergreen tree valued for its extremely hard timber and ornamental flowers.

Mesua ferrea is native to South and Southeast Asia, where it is commonly found in tropical forests. It is known for its fragrant white flowers and exceptionally dense, durable wood.

The tree is culturally significant in several regions and is often planted in temples and sacred spaces.

Characteristics of Mesua Ferrea

  • Extremely dense hardwood
  • Evergreen tropical tree
  • Fragrant white flowers
  • Slow-growing and long-lived
  • Native to South and Southeast Asia
  • Highly valued ornamental species

Common Uses

  • High-quality timber
  • Ornamental landscaping
  • Religious and temple planting
  • Traditional medicine
  • Shade tree in tropical regions
