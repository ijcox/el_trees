Eusideroxylon Zwageri
Eusideroxylon zwageri, commonly known as Bornean ironwood, is one of the hardest and most durable tropical hardwood trees in the world.

Eusideroxylon zwageri is native to Southeast Asia, particularly Borneo and surrounding regions. It produces extremely dense timber that is highly resistant to decay, insects, and weathering.

Because of its strength and longevity, it has been heavily exploited and is now considered a conservation concern in many areas.

Characteristics of Eusideroxylon Zwageri

  • One of the hardest tropical hardwoods
  • Extremely slow-growing tree
  • Native to Borneo and Southeast Asia
  • Highly rot and insect resistant
  • Long lifespan
  • Important rainforest species

Common Uses

  • Heavy-duty construction timber
  • Bridges and marine structures
  • Flooring and outdoor structures
  • Traditional woodworking
  • High-durability applications
