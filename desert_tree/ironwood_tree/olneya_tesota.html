Olneya Tesota
Olneya tesota, commonly known as desert ironwood, is a long-lived tree native to the Sonoran Desert.

Olneya tesota is a slow-growing desert tree found primarily in the Sonoran Desert of the southwestern United States and northwestern Mexico. It is known for its extremely dense, heavy wood and its ability to thrive in harsh, arid conditions.

The tree plays an important ecological role by providing shade and shelter for desert wildlife and helping stabilize desert soils.

Characteristics of Olneya Tesota

  • Extremely dense hardwood
  • Native to Sonoran Desert
  • Very slow-growing and long-lived
  • Drought and heat tolerant
  • Small compound leaves
  • Important desert keystone species

Common Uses

  • Firewood (high energy density)
  • Carvings and artisan woodwork
  • Desert habitat restoration
  • Wildlife shelter
  • Ecological conservation
