Krugiodendron Ferosum
Krugiodendron ferreum, commonly known as black ironwood, is an extremely dense hardwood tree native to the Caribbean region.

Krugiodendron ferreum is native to the Caribbean, including southern Florida, the Bahamas, and parts of Central America. It is known for producing one of the heaviest and hardest woods in the region.

Due to its density and slow growth, it is highly valued but naturally uncommon in mature form.
