Carpinus Caroliniana
Carpinus caroliniana, commonly known as American hornbeam, is a small hardwood tree native to North America.

Carpinus caroliniana is native to eastern North America and is commonly found in moist woodlands and along stream banks. Despite its relatively small size, it produces extremely hard, dense wood that has earned it the nickname “ironwood.”

It plays an important ecological role in forest understories and provides food and shelter for wildlife.

Characteristics of Carpinus Caroliniana

  • Very dense hardwood
  • Native to eastern North America
  • Prefers moist, shaded environments
  • Slow-growing understory tree
  • Smooth gray bark with muscle-like texture
  • Highly shade tolerant

Common Uses

  • Ornamental landscaping
  • Wildlife habitat
  • Soil stabilization near waterways
  • Shade tree in forests and parks
  • Ecological restoration
