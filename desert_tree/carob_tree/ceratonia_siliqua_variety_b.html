Ceratonia Siliqua Variety B
A hardy carob cultivar adapted to hotter, drier Mediterranean and semi-arid regions.

Ceratonia siliqua Variety B is selected for increased heat tolerance and survival in poorer soils. It tends to produce slightly smaller pods but excels in resilience and long-term survival in harsh climates.

The species as a whole is widely valued for its ability to thrive where many other fruit trees fail.

Characteristics of Ceratonia Siliqua Variety B

  • High heat tolerance
  • Very drought resistant
  • Smaller but abundant pods
  • Adapted to poor soils
  • Evergreen Mediterranean tree
  • Long-lived cultivar

Common Uses

  • Carob production in arid regions
  • Land reclamation
  • Soil erosion control
  • Livestock fodder
  • Ornamental dry-land planting
