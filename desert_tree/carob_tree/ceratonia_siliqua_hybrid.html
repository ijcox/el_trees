Ceratonia Siliqua Hybrid
A selectively bred hybrid form of carob combining traits for yield, resilience, and adaptability.

Ceratonia siliqua Hybrid is developed by combining different carob cultivars to improve productivity and environmental tolerance. It is often used in experimental agriculture and modern orchard systems.

These hybrids aim to balance pod quality with faster establishment and improved stress resistance.
