Ceratonia Siliqua Variety A
A cultivated form of Ceratonia siliqua known for balanced pod production and strong drought tolerance.

Ceratonia siliqua is a long-lived evergreen tree native to the Mediterranean region. Variety A is typically selected for stable yield, consistent pod size, and resilience in semi-arid climates.

It is widely cultivated for its edible pods, which are naturally sweet and often used as a cocoa substitute.
