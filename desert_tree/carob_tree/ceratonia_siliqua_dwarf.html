Ceratonia Siliqua Dwarf
A compact ornamental form of carob adapted for small gardens and container planting.

Ceratonia siliqua Dwarf is a cultivated form selected for its reduced size and slower growth habit. It retains the drought resistance and evergreen nature of standard carob trees but remains much smaller in overall structure.

This makes it ideal for urban landscapes and limited-space environments.
