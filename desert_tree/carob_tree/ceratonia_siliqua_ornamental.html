Ceratonia Siliqua Ornamental
An ornamental form of carob valued for its aesthetic shape, evergreen foliage, and landscape design use.

Ceratonia siliqua Ornamental is cultivated primarily for visual appeal rather than agricultural production. It retains the hardiness and drought resistance of standard carob trees but is shaped and selected for landscape beauty.

It is commonly used in parks, streetscapes, and decorative garden settings where low maintenance is desired.
