Acacia Cyanophylla Hybrid
A selectively bred hybrid combining fast growth and improved environmental tolerance in Acacia cyanophylla forms.

Acacia cyanophylla Hybrid is developed to combine desirable traits from multiple variants, including improved growth rate, drought resistance, and canopy structure balance. It is commonly used in experimental forestry and land rehabilitation programs.

This hybrid is designed for adaptability across a wide range of degraded or dry landscapes.
