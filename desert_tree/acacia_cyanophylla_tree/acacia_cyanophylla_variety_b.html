Acacia Cyanophylla Variety B
A drought-adapted Acacia form optimized for survival in extremely arid and nutrient-poor soils.

Acacia cyanophylla Variety B is a resilient selection that prioritizes survival under harsh environmental conditions. It typically has a more open canopy and reduced leaf density compared to Variety A.

It is frequently used in desert reclamation and ecological restoration projects.
