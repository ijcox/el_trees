Acacia Cyanophylla Dwarf
A compact ornamental form of Acacia cyanophylla suited for small landscapes and controlled environments.

Acacia cyanophylla Dwarf is a reduced-size cultivar that maintains the species’ drought tolerance while growing in a compact, manageable form. It is often used in urban landscaping where space is limited.

This variety retains the same ecological benefits as larger forms, including soil stabilization and low water requirements.
