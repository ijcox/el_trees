Pachycereus Pringlei
Pachycereus pringlei, known as the Mexican giant cardon, is one of the largest cactus species in the world.

Pachycereus pringlei is native to the Sonoran Desert in Mexico and can reach towering heights, making it one of the tallest cacti on Earth. It forms massive columnar trunks that branch with age and can dominate desert landscapes.

This cactus plays an important ecological role by providing shelter and food sources for desert wildlife.
