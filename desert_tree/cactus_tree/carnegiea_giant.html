Carnegiea Giant
Carnegiea gigantea, commonly known as the saguaro, is a massive cactus species native to the Sonoran Desert.

The Carnegiea giant cactus is one of the most iconic desert plants in North America. It is known for its towering columnar structure and branching arms that develop with age.

These cacti are extremely slow-growing and can live for over 150 years, playing a key role in desert ecosystems.
