Echinopsis Pachanoi
Echinopsis pachanoi, commonly known as San Pedro cactus, is a fast-growing columnar cactus native to the Andes Mountains.

Echinopsis pachanoi is native to South America, especially Peru and Ecuador, where it grows in high-altitude environments. It is known for its tall, ribbed green stems and rapid growth compared to many other cacti species.

This cactus has been historically significant in Andean cultures and is widely cultivated as an ornamental plant.
