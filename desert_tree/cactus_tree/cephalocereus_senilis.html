Cephalocereus Senilis
Cephalocereus senilis, commonly known as the old man cactus, is a columnar cactus covered in long white hairs.

Cephalocereus senilis is native to eastern Mexico and is easily recognized by its dense covering of white, hair-like spines that help protect it from sun and cold temperatures.

It is a slow-growing cactus that can live for many decades in arid environments, storing water in its thick stem.
