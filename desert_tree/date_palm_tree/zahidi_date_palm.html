Zahidi Date Palm
Zahidi is a semi-dry date variety known for its golden color, firm texture, and mildly sweet flavor.

Zahidi Date Palm originates from Iraq and is widely grown across the Middle East and parts of the Americas. It is valued for its durability, long shelf life, and lower sugar content compared to softer date varieties.

Because of its firmness and stability, Zahidi is often used in baking and food processing rather than as a soft fresh snack.

Characteristics of Zahidi Date Palm

  • Firm, semi-dry texture
  • Golden yellow to light brown color
  • Mild sweetness
  • High fiber content
  • Long storage life
  • Resistant to handling damage

Common Uses

  • Baking and cooking
  • Food processing industry
  • Snack mixes
  • Export markets
  • Low-sugar dietary options
