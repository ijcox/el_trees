Khalas Date Palm
Khalas is a premium date variety known for its balanced sweetness, soft texture, and rich caramel flavor.

Khalas Date Palm is widely cultivated in the Arabian Peninsula, particularly in Saudi Arabia and the United Arab Emirates. It is highly valued for its consistent quality and well-rounded flavor profile that is less intensely sweet than Medjool but more complex than drier varieties.

It is commonly enjoyed with coffee or as a traditional hospitality fruit in Middle Eastern culture.

Characteristics of Khalas Date Palm

  • Medium-sized, soft fruit
  • Balanced caramel-like sweetness
  • Amber to dark brown color
  • Smooth, slightly sticky texture
  • Popular in Gulf countries
  • Excellent storage stability

Common Uses

  • Traditional hospitality serving
  • Snack food
  • Pairing with coffee or tea
  • Export and commercial trade
  • Culinary desserts
