Barhi Date Palm
Barhi is a soft, sweet date variety known for its ability to be eaten at multiple ripening stages.

Barhi Date Palm originates from the Middle East and is especially popular in Iraq. It is unique among date cultivars because it can be consumed fresh, semi-ripe, or fully dried while still maintaining excellent flavor.

Its soft texture and honey-like sweetness make it highly desirable in fresh fruit markets.

Characteristics of Barhi Date Palm

  • Soft, creamy texture when ripe
  • Golden yellow at fresh stage
  • Extremely sweet flavor profile
  • Can be eaten at multiple ripeness stages
  • Delicate fruit skin
  • Thrives in hot climates

Common Uses

  • Fresh fruit consumption
  • Gourmet produce markets
  • Natural sweetener
  • Desserts and snacks
  • Traditional Middle Eastern cuisine
