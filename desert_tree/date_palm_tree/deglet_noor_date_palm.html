Deglet Noor Date Palm
Deglet Noor is a semi-dry date variety known for its light color, firm texture, and delicate honey-like flavor.

Deglet Noor dates originated in North Africa, particularly Algeria and Tunisia, and are now widely cultivated in the United States and the Middle East. They are often called the “queen of dates” due to their balanced sweetness and versatile culinary use.

These dates are commonly used in baking and cooking because they hold their shape well compared to softer varieties.

Characteristics of Deglet Noor Dates

  • Semi-dry texture
  • Light amber color
  • Subtle honey-like sweetness
  • Firm flesh ideal for cooking
  • Long shelf life
  • Grown in arid climates
