Joshua Tree Hybrid
Joshua Tree Hybrid is a cultivated form combining traits from multiple Yucca brevifolia lineages for improved resilience.

Joshua Tree Hybrid refers to selectively propagated forms of Yucca brevifolia designed to enhance drought tolerance, structural stability, and adaptability across varied desert environments. These hybrids are typically used in conservation, restoration, and controlled landscaping projects.

They maintain the iconic silhouette of the Joshua tree while improving survivability in changing climate conditions.
