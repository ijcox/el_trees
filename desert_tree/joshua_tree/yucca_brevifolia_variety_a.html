Yucca Brevifolia Variety A
A regional variation of the Joshua tree adapted to specific Mojave Desert microclimates.

Yucca brevifolia Variety A is a form of the Joshua tree found in select regions of the Mojave Desert. It exhibits subtle differences in branching structure, growth rate, and leaf density compared to other varieties.

These variations are believed to result from localized environmental pressures such as soil composition, elevation, and rainfall patterns.
