Yucca Brevifolia Variety B
A distinct Joshua tree variation adapted to lower desert basins and harsher arid conditions.

Yucca brevifolia Variety B is found in drier, lower elevation regions of the Mojave Desert. It tends to have more spread-out branching and a slightly more irregular growth form compared to Variety A.

This variety shows strong resilience to heat stress and prolonged drought conditions, making it well-suited to extreme desert environments.

Characteristics of Variety B

  • Looser, more open branching structure
  • Adapted to low elevation desert basins
  • Extreme drought tolerance
  • Irregular growth patterns
  • Highly heat resistant
  • Slow-growing desert species
