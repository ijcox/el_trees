Mojave Joshua Tree
Mojave Joshua Tree is a regional form of Yucca brevifolia found primarily in the Mojave Desert ecosystem.

Mojave Joshua Tree represents populations of Yucca brevifolia that are native to the Mojave Desert, where they form iconic desert woodlands. These trees are closely tied to the ecology of the region and support a wide range of desert wildlife.

Their distribution is strongly influenced by elevation, rainfall patterns, and soil composition within the Mojave region.
