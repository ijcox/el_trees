Cultivated Brazil Nut Tree
The Cultivated Brazil Nut Tree is a managed form of Bertholletia excelsa grown in controlled or semi-managed systems for improved nut harvest efficiency.

Cultivated Brazil nut trees are typically grown in managed agroforestry systems within tropical regions. While still large rainforest trees, they are supported by human-managed harvesting practices to improve yield and accessibility. They remain dependent on specific pollinators and ecological conditions.

Characteristics of Cultivated Brazil Nut Trees

  • Managed tropical forest tree
  • Produces large woody seed pods
  • Partially domesticated harvesting system
  • Requires specialized pollinators
  • Used in agroforestry systems
  • Improved harvest efficiency vs wild stands

Common Uses

  • Agroforestry systems
  • Export nut industry
