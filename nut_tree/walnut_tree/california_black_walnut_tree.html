California Black Walnut Tree
The California Black Walnut Tree (Juglans hindsii) is a medium to large deciduous tree native to California, USA. It is valued for its durable wood, edible nuts, and use as a rootstock for English Walnut cultivation.

California Black Walnut trees grow 15–25 meters tall with a rounded to spreading crown. They thrive in full sun and well-drained soils. The tree produces edible nuts enclosed in thick husks and has pinnately compound leaves. Its wood is prized for furniture, cabinetry, and veneer, and it is widely used in hybrid walnut orchards.

Characteristics of California Black Walnut Trees

  • Deciduous tree with pinnately compound leaves
  • Produces edible nuts in thick husks
  • Rounded to spreading crown
  • Moderate growth rate, long-lived
  • Native to California, USA

Common Uses

  • Timber for furniture, cabinetry, and veneer
  • Edible nuts for culinary use
  • Ornamental planting in large gardens and parks
  • Rootstock for English Walnut orchards
  • Wildlife habitat and food source for birds and mammals
