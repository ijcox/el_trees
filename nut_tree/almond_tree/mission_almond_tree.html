Mission Almond Tree
The Mission Almond Tree (Prunus dulcis 'Mission') is one of the oldest almond varieties, known for its dark shells and strong, distinctive flavor.

Mission almond trees grow 4–10 meters tall with spreading deciduous canopies. They thrive in Mediterranean climates with hot summers and mild winters. The nuts are typically small with hard, dark shells and are often used in traditional and processed almond products.

Characteristics of Mission Almond Trees

  • Deciduous nut-bearing tree

  • Strong, traditional flavor profile
  • Thrives in dry Mediterranean climates
  • Old heritage variety
  • Used in processing and roasting
