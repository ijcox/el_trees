Butte Almond Tree
The Butte Almond Tree (Prunus dulcis 'Butte') is a late-blooming almond variety valued for its hard shell and suitability in processing industries.

Butte almond trees grow 4–10 meters tall with broad deciduous canopies. They thrive in Mediterranean climates and are known for later flowering, which helps reduce frost damage. The nuts have hard shells and are commonly used in processed almond products.

Characteristics of Butte Almond Trees

  • Deciduous nut-bearing tree
  • Late blooming (frost-resistant)
  • Hard-shelled almond variety
  • Thrives in warm dry climates
  • Used in industrial almond processing
  • Reliable orchard performer

Common Uses

  • Almond processing industry
  • Baking and confectionery
  • Nut oil production
  • Commercial orchards
  • Food manufacturing
