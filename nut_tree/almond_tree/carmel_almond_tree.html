Carmel Almond Tree
The Carmel Almond Tree (Prunus dulcis 'Carmel') is a widely cultivated almond variety known for its high yield and strong compatibility as a pollinizer.

Carmel almond trees grow 4–10 meters tall with broad deciduous canopies. They thrive in Mediterranean climates with hot, dry summers and mild winters. The nuts are high quality, commonly used in commercial almond production and as a reliable pollinating partner for other varieties.

Characteristics of Carmel Almond Trees

  • Deciduous nut-bearing tree
  • High-yield commercial variety
  • Good pollinizer for other almonds
  • Thrives in dry Mediterranean climates
  • Produces medium-sized sweet kernels
  • Reliable annual production

Common Uses

  • Commercial almond production
  • Baking and confectionery
  • Almond milk and flour
  • Nut processing industry
  • Orchard pollination support
