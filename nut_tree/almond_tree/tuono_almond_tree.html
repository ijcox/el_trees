Tuono Almond Tree
The Tuono Almond Tree (Prunus dulcis 'Tuono') is an Italian almond variety valued for its high-quality kernels and use as a pollinizer in orchards.

Tuono almond trees grow 4–10 meters tall with spreading deciduous canopies. They thrive in Mediterranean climates with warm, dry summers and mild winters. The nuts are medium-sized with a rich flavor and are often used in confectionery and traditional almond products.
