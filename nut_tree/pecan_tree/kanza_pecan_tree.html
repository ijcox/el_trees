Kanza Pecan Tree
The Kanza Pecan Tree (Carya illinoinensis 'Kanza') is a highly cold-hardy, disease-resistant pecan variety known for excellent nut quality and northern adaptability.

Kanza pecan trees grow 15–25 meters tall with compact, spreading canopies. They thrive in cooler temperate regions where many pecan varieties struggle. The nuts are high quality, well-filled, and especially valued for consistent production in northern orchards.

Characteristics of Kanza Pecan Trees

  • Deciduous nut-bearing tree
  • Very cold-hardy pecan variety
  • High disease resistance
  • Excellent nut quality
  • Adapted for northern climates
  • Reliable orchard production

Common Uses

  • Commercial pecan production
  • Pecan pies and desserts
  • Cold-region orchards
  • Nut processing industry
  • Wildlife food source
