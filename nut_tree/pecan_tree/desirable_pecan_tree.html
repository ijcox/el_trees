Desirable Pecan Tree
The Desirable Pecan Tree (Carya illinoinensis 'Desirable') is a high-quality pecan variety known for its excellent nut flavor and strong commercial value.

Desirable pecan trees grow 20–35 meters tall with wide spreading canopies. They thrive in warm climates with deep soils and consistent moisture. The nuts are medium to large, easy to shell, and highly prized for desserts and processing.
