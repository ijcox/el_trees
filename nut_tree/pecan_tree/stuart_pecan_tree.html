Stuart Pecan Tree
The Stuart Pecan Tree (Carya illinoinensis 'Stuart') is a classic, long-established pecan variety known for its large nuts and reliable production.

Stuart pecan trees grow 20–40 meters tall with broad, spreading deciduous canopies. They thrive in warm temperate climates with deep, fertile soils and long growing seasons. The nuts are large, thick-shelled, and commonly used in both commercial and traditional pecan production.

Characteristics of Stuart Pecan Trees

  • Large deciduous nut-bearing tree
  • Produces big, thick-shelled pecans
  • Reliable but later-maturing variety
  • Thrives in warm climates
  • Long-lived orchard tree
  • Strong historical cultivation variety

Common Uses

  • Pecan pies and baking
  • Nut harvesting and processing
  • Commercial orchards
  • Confectionery production
  • Wildlife food source
