Cheyenne Pecan Tree
The Cheyenne Pecan Tree (Carya illinoinensis 'Cheyenne') is a high-yielding, disease-resistant pecan variety known for early production and good nut quality.

Cheyenne pecan trees grow 18–30 meters tall with broad spreading canopies. They thrive in warm climates and are valued for their early bearing and consistent yields. The nuts are medium-sized, well-filled, and commonly used in commercial orchards.
