Walnut Tree
Walnut trees are large deciduous trees of the genus Juglans, prized for their high-quality timber and nutritious nuts. They are native to temperate regions of the Northern Hemisphere.

Walnuts thrive in well-drained soils with full sun exposure. They are valued for their strong, dark wood used in fine furniture and cabinetry, as well as for edible walnuts consumed worldwide.

Characteristics of Walnut Trees

  • Deciduous trees with pinnately compound leaves
  • Produce edible nuts enclosed in hard shells
  • Grow straight, tall trunks suitable for timber
  • Adapted to temperate climates and well-drained soils
  • Provide shade, wildlife habitat, and nut production

Common Uses

  • Timber for furniture, cabinetry, and woodworking
  • Nut production for culinary use and oil extraction
  • Ornamental planting and shade trees
  • Wildlife food and habitat
  • Soil stabilization and ecological support in landscapes
