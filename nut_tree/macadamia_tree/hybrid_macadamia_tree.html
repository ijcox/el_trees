Hybrid Macadamia Tree
The Hybrid Macadamia Tree is a cultivated cross between macadamia species designed to improve yield, nut quality, and climate adaptability.

Hybrid macadamia trees typically grow 10–15 meters tall with dense evergreen canopies. They are developed by crossing species such as Macadamia integrifolia and Macadamia tetraphylla. These hybrids aim to combine smooth shells, better cold tolerance, and higher productivity.
