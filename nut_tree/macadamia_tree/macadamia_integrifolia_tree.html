Macadamia Integrifolia Tree
The Macadamia Integrifolia Tree (Macadamia integrifolia) is a tropical evergreen nut tree known for producing smooth-shelled, high-quality macadamia nuts.

Macadamia integrifolia trees grow 10–15 meters tall with dense evergreen foliage. They thrive in warm subtropical climates with well-drained volcanic or sandy soils. This species is one of the primary commercial sources of macadamia nuts worldwide.

Characteristics of Macadamia Integrifolia Trees

  • Evergreen tropical nut tree
  • Produces smooth-shelled macadamia nuts
  • Thrives in subtropical climates
  • High-value commercial crop
  • Slow to mature but long-lived
  • Dense canopy structure

Common Uses

  • Premium nut production
  • Confectionery and baking
  • Snack food industry
  • Macadamia oil production
  • Commercial orchards
