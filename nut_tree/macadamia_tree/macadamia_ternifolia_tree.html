Macadamia Ternifolia Tree
The Macadamia Ternifolia Tree (Macadamia ternifolia) is a lesser-grown macadamia species known for its smaller nuts and use in breeding programs.

Macadamia ternifolia trees grow 8–12 meters tall with dense evergreen foliage. They are less commonly cultivated commercially due to smaller nut size but are important in genetic diversity and hybrid development. They thrive in warm subtropical climates with well-drained soils.
