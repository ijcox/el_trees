Macadamia Tetraphylla Tree
The Macadamia Tetraphylla Tree (Macadamia tetraphylla) is a macadamia species known for its rough-shelled nuts and cold tolerance compared to other varieties.

Macadamia tetraphylla trees grow 10–18 meters tall with lush evergreen canopies. They tolerate slightly cooler climates than other macadamia species and produce textured, rough-shelled nuts. They are used in both commercial orchards and hybrid breeding programs.
