Turkish Hazelnut Tree
The Turkish Hazelnut Tree (Corylus colurna) is a tall, single-trunk hazelnut species known for its hardiness and use in ornamental and urban planting.

Turkish hazelnut trees grow 10–20 meters tall, forming a strong central trunk unlike most hazelnut species. They thrive in temperate climates and are highly drought and pollution tolerant. The nuts are edible but harder-shelled and less commonly cultivated for large-scale production.

Characteristics of Turkish Hazelnut Trees

  • Single-trunk deciduous tree
  • Very tall for a hazelnut species
  • Hard-shelled edible nuts
  • Highly drought and pollution tolerant
  • Often used in urban landscaping
  • Long-lived ornamental tree

Common Uses

  • Ornamental urban planting
  • Street and park trees
  • Limited nut harvesting
  • Windbreaks and shade trees
  • Environmental resilience planting
