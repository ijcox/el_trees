European Hazelnut Tree
The European Hazelnut Tree (Corylus avellana) is the most widely cultivated hazelnut species, known for its high-yield nuts used in confectionery and chocolate production.

European hazelnut trees grow 3–8 meters tall and can be trained as shrubs or small trees. They thrive in temperate climates with cool winters and mild summers. The nuts are larger and sweeter than American varieties, making them the dominant commercial hazelnut worldwide.

Characteristics of European Hazelnut Trees

  • Deciduous shrub or small tree
  • Produces large, high-quality hazelnuts
  • Widely cultivated commercially
  • Thrives in cool temperate climates
  • High oil content nuts
  • Common orchard crop in Europe

Common Uses

  • Chocolate and confectionery production
  • Nut oils and spreads (e.g., hazelnut paste)
  • Baking and desserts
  • Commercial orchards
  • Food processing industry
