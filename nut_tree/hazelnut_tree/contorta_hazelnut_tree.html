Contorta Hazelnut Tree
The Contorta Hazelnut Tree (Corylus avellana 'Contorta') is an ornamental hazelnut variety known for its dramatically twisted branches and unique landscape appearance.

Contorta hazelnut trees grow 3–5 meters tall and are primarily grown for ornamental value rather than nut production. They thrive in temperate climates and prefer well-drained soil with full to partial sun. Their spiraled branches make them a popular feature in gardens and decorative landscapes.
