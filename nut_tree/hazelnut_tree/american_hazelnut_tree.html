American Hazelnut Tree
The American Hazelnut Tree (Corylus americana) is a hardy native shrub-like tree known for producing small, flavorful hazelnuts and supporting wildlife habitats.

American hazelnut trees typically grow 2–5 meters tall and form dense thickets. They thrive in temperate climates across North America and tolerate a wide range of soil conditions. The nuts are smaller than European varieties but highly nutritious and valued by wildlife and humans alike.

Characteristics of American Hazelnut Trees

  • Multi-stemmed shrub or small tree
  • Produces small edible hazelnuts
  • Native to North America
  • Highly cold hardy
  • Supports wildlife ecosystems
  • Forms dense natural hedges

Common Uses

  • Nut production for food and wildlife
  • Hedgerows and natural fencing
  • Ecological restoration projects
  • Ornamental native planting
  • Wildlife habitat support
