Common Cashew Tree
The Common Cashew Tree (Anacardium occidentale) is a tropical evergreen tree known for producing cashew nuts and the cashew apple fruit.

Cashew trees grow 8–15 meters tall with wide, irregular evergreen canopies. They thrive in hot tropical climates with well-drained sandy soils and high sunlight. The tree produces a kidney-shaped nut and a fleshy cashew apple used in beverages and local foods.

Characteristics of Common Cashew Trees

  • Evergreen tropical fruit tree
  • Produces cashew nuts and cashew apples
  • Thrives in hot, dry tropical climates
  • Fast-growing and drought tolerant
  • Spreading irregular canopy
  • Commercially important nut crop

Common Uses

  • Cashew nut production
  • Snacking and cooking ingredients
  • Cashew apple beverages and juices
  • Commercial agriculture
  • Oil and food processing
