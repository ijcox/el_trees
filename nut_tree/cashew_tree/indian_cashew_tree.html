Indian Cashew Tree
The Indian Cashew Tree (Anacardium occidentale, Indian cultivars) is a widely cultivated tropical variety known for high-quality nuts and strong commercial production.

Indian cashew trees grow 8–12 meters tall with dense evergreen canopies. They thrive in tropical coastal regions with warm temperatures and sandy soils. India is one of the world’s largest producers and processors of cashew nuts.
