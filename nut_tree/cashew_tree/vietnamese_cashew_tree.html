Vietnamese Cashew Tree
The Vietnamese Cashew Tree (Anacardium occidentale, Vietnamese cultivars) is a high-efficiency tropical variety known for export-grade nut production and intensive cultivation systems.

Vietnamese cashew trees grow 8–12 meters tall with compact, productive evergreen canopies. They thrive in tropical climates with distinct dry seasons and are often grown in large-scale plantations. Vietnam is one of the world’s leading exporters of processed cashew nuts.
