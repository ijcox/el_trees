Green Ash Tree
The Green Ash Tree (Fraxinus pennsylvanica) is a fast-growing deciduous tree known for its compound leaves and adaptability to various soils. It is native to eastern and central North America and widely planted for urban landscaping.

Green Ash trees typically grow 15–25 meters tall with a straight trunk and rounded crown. They tolerate wet and dry soils and can thrive in urban environments. Their samaras provide food for birds, and the wood is moderately strong for timber use.

Characteristics of Green Ash Trees

  • Compound leaves with 5–9 leaflets
  • Straight trunk with rounded crown
  • Produces winged seeds called samaras
  • Moderately strong and flexible wood
  • Fast-growing and adaptable
  • Native to eastern and central North America

Common Uses

  • Urban and street planting
  • Shade tree for parks and yards
  • Timber for furniture and tool handles
  • Wildlife habitat and food source
  • Ornamental landscaping
