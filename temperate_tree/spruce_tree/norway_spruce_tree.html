Norway Spruce Tree
The Norway Spruce (Picea abies) is a large, fast-growing evergreen native to Europe. It is widely planted in North America for timber, Christmas trees, and ornamental landscaping.

Norway Spruces can grow 35–55 meters tall with a straight trunk and dense, conical crown. They have needle-like leaves that are dark green and four-sided, and they produce long, pendulous cones. This species is highly valued for timber, pulpwood, and as a decorative tree in parks and gardens.

Characteristics of Norway Spruce Trees

  • Evergreen conifers with needle-like, four-sided leaves (1–2.5 cm long)
  • Bark is thin and scaly, becoming reddish-brown with age
  • Produce long, pendulous cones 9–17 cm in length
  • Fast-growing and tolerant of a range of soils, including loamy and acidic soils
  • Lifespan typically 200–300 years

Common Uses

  • Timber for construction, furniture, and pulpwood
  • Christmas tree plantations
  • Ornamental planting in parks and large landscapes
  • Windbreaks and reforestation projects
  • Wildlife habitat for birds and small mammals
