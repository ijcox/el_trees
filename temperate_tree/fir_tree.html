Fir Tree
Fir trees are evergreen coniferous trees belonging to the genus Abies. They are commonly found in mountainous regions of the Northern Hemisphere and are prized for their symmetrical shape and soft needles.

Fir trees grow tall with upright cones and dense foliage. They are widely used for timber, paper production, Christmas trees, and ornamental landscaping.

Characteristics of Fir Trees

  • Evergreen conifers with needle-like, soft foliage
  • Upright cones that disintegrate at maturity
  • Adapted to temperate and mountainous climates
  • Symmetrical conical shape ideal for ornamentation
  • Provide habitat for wildlife and forest ecosystems

Common Uses

  • Timber for construction and furniture
  • Pulp for paper production
  • Christmas trees and ornamental landscaping
  • Windbreaks and reforestation projects
  • Habitat for birds and small forest animals
