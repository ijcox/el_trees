Goat Willow Tree
The Goat Willow Tree (Salix caprea), also known as Pussy Willow, is a small deciduous tree native to Europe and western Asia. It is well known for its soft, furry catkins that appear in early spring.

Goat Willow trees grow 6–12 meters tall with a rounded, irregular crown. They thrive in moist soils and tolerate a wide range of conditions. The leaves are broad and oval, and the tree produces distinctive fuzzy catkins before the leaves emerge.

Characteristics of Goat Willow Trees

  • Broad, oval leaves
  • Rounded, irregular crown
  • Produces soft, fuzzy catkins in early spring
  • Tolerant of a wide range of soil conditions
  • Small to medium-sized tree
  • Native to Europe and western Asia

Common Uses

  • Ornamental tree for gardens and landscapes
  • Early pollen source for bees and pollinators
  • Decorative branches (pussy willows)
  • Soil stabilization in moist areas
  • Wildlife habitat
