Scarlet Oak Tree
The Scarlet Oak Tree (Quercus coccinea) is a medium to large deciduous oak native to eastern North America. It is best known for its brilliant red foliage in autumn and rounded crown.

Scarlet Oak trees typically grow 18–24 meters tall with a broad, rounded crown. They thrive in well-drained acidic soils and full sun. Acorns provide food for wildlife, and the wood is used for furniture, flooring, and construction.

Characteristics of Scarlet Oak Trees

  • Deciduous tree with deeply lobed green leaves turning bright red in fall
  • Broad, rounded crown
  • Produces acorns that feed birds and mammals
  • Medium growth rate, long-lived
  • Native to eastern North America

Common Uses

  • Ornamental planting for fall color in parks and landscapes
  • Timber for furniture, flooring, and construction
  • Wildlife habitat and acorn food source
  • Shade tree in open areas
  • Forest restoration and erosion control
