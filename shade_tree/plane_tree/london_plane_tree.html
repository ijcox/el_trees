London Plane Tree
The London Plane Tree (Platanus × acerifolia) is a hybrid of the American Sycamore and Oriental Plane, widely planted in urban areas for its pollution tolerance and rapid growth. It is valued for its shade and distinctive mottled bark.

London Plane trees grow 20–30 meters tall with a broad, rounded crown. They tolerate a variety of soils and urban conditions, including pollution and compacted soil. The leaves are large, lobed, and maple-like, and the bark peels in patches to reveal a creamy, smooth trunk.

Characteristics of London Plane Trees

  • Large, lobed, maple-like leaves
  • Broad, rounded crown
  • Mottled bark peeling to reveal smooth, light-colored trunk
  • Fast-growing and long-lived
  • Highly tolerant of urban pollution
  • Hybrid of American Sycamore and Oriental Plane

Common Uses

  • Street and urban planting
  • Shade trees in parks and large gardens
  • Timber for furniture and veneer
  • Ornamental planting for visual appeal
  • Windbreaks and urban canopy improvement
