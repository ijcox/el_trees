Sweetbay Magnolia Tree
The Sweetbay Magnolia Tree (Magnolia virginiana) is native to the southeastern United States. It is an evergreen or semi-evergreen tree valued for its fragrant white flowers and adaptability to wet soils.

Sweetbay Magnolia trees grow 6–15 meters tall with a pyramidal to rounded crown. They thrive in acidic, wet soils, often near swamps or wetlands. Leaves are narrow, glossy, and aromatic, and flowers are creamy-white and highly fragrant.

Characteristics of Sweetbay Magnolia Trees

  • Narrow, glossy, aromatic leaves
  • Pyramidal to rounded crown
  • Fragrant creamy-white flowers in late spring
  • Evergreen or semi-evergreen depending on climate
  • Thrives in wet, acidic soils
  • Native to southeastern United States

Common Uses

  • Ornamental planting in gardens and wetlands
  • Fragrant flowers for landscaping
  • Wildlife habitat and food source for pollinators
  • Shade tree in parks and yards
  • Screening and border planting in moist areas
