Kwanzan Cherry Tree
The Kwanzan Cherry Tree (Prunus serrulata 'Kwanzan') is a small ornamental deciduous tree, widely cultivated for its striking double pink blossoms in spring. It is a popular cultivar of Japanese Cherry Trees.

Kwanzan Cherry trees grow 6–12 meters tall with a rounded crown. They thrive in full sun and well-drained soils. The tree produces abundant double pink blossoms in spring and glossy green leaves that turn yellow-orange in fall. It is primarily used as an ornamental tree in parks, gardens, and streetscapes.

Characteristics of Kwanzan Cherry Trees

  • Deciduous ornamental tree with ovate green leaves
  • Produces abundant double pink blossoms in spring
  • Rounded crown, small to medium-sized tree
  • Moderate growth rate and moderately long-lived
  • Popular cultivar of Japanese Cherry Trees

Common Uses

  • Ornamental planting for spectacular spring flowers
  • Public parks, gardens, and streetscapes
  • Shade tree in small to medium landscapes
  • Wildlife habitat and nectar for pollinators
  • Cultural plantings for Cherry Blossom Festivals
