Crimean Linden Tree
The Crimean Linden is a hardy ornamental tree valued for its drought tolerance, fragrant flowers, and compact form.

Crimean lindens are adaptable trees that perform well in warmer, drier climates compared to other linden species. They produce fragrant yellow flowers in summer that attract bees and other pollinators. Their balanced form makes them suitable for urban and landscape planting.
