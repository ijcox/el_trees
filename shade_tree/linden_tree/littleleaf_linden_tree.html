Littleleaf Linden Tree
The Littleleaf Linden is a compact, highly ornamental shade tree known for its dense foliage and fragrant summer flowers.

Littleleaf lindens are smaller than American lindens, making them ideal for urban streets and tighter landscapes. They produce abundant clusters of fragrant yellow flowers that attract bees and other pollinators. Their small leaves create a dense, uniform canopy.

Characteristics of Littleleaf Linden Trees

  • Deciduous ornamental shade tree
  • Dense compact canopy
  • Fragrant yellow flowers
  • Smaller leaf size
  • Urban tolerant species
  • Excellent street tree
