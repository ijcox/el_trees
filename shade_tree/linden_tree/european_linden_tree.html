European Linden Tree
The European Linden is a classic shade tree widely planted across cities and historic landscapes for its fragrance and symmetrical form.

European lindens are long-lived ornamental trees commonly found in parks, town squares, and formal avenues. They produce fragrant yellow flowers in early summer that are highly attractive to bees. Their rounded canopy provides dense, cooling shade.

  • Pollinator-friendly flowers
