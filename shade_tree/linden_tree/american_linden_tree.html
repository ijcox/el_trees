American Linden Tree
The American Linden is a large, shade-producing deciduous tree known for its fragrant flowers and heart-shaped leaves.

American linden trees are tall, broad-canopied trees commonly planted in parks and streets for their dense shade and attractive form. In early summer, they produce clusters of small, highly fragrant yellow flowers that are important for pollinators. Their heart-shaped leaves turn yellow in autumn.

Characteristics of Linden Trees

  • Deciduous shade tree
  • Fragrant summer flowers
  • Heart-shaped leaves
  • Dense canopy structure
  • Excellent urban tolerance
  • Pollinator-friendly species

Common Uses

  • Street tree planting
  • Urban shade trees
  • Parks and large landscapes
  • Pollinator gardens
  • Avenue plantings
