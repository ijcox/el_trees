Avicennia Marina Variety B
A resilient Grey Mangrove variation adapted to fluctuating tidal and salinity conditions.

Avicennia Marina Variety B is a coastal Grey Mangrove form commonly found in estuaries, mudflats, and sheltered shorelines. It is highly adaptable to changing water levels and extreme salinity conditions.

Like other Grey Mangroves, it plays a critical role in shoreline stabilization and provides essential habitat for marine and bird life.

Characteristics of Grey Mangrove Trees

  • High tolerance to salt and flooding
  • Pneumatophore root system
  • Grows in tidal wetlands and estuaries
  • Evergreen coastal species
  • Stabilizes muddy shorelines
  • Supports wetland ecosystems

Common Uses

  • Coastal protection and restoration
  • Wetland rehabilitation
  • Habitat support for wildlife
  • Shoreline stabilization
  • Environmental conservation efforts
