Grey Mangrove Coastal
A shoreline-adapted form of Grey Mangrove commonly found along tidal coasts and estuarine edges.

The Grey Mangrove Coastal type is specifically adapted to thrive along exposed and semi-protected shorelines. It plays a major role in stabilizing coastal sediments and reducing the impact of waves and storm surges.

This form is often found forming dense stands that help build and protect coastal ecosystems while supporting marine biodiversity.

Characteristics of Grey Mangrove Trees

  • Highly salt-tolerant species
  • Pneumatophore root system
  • Thrives in tidal and coastal wetlands
  • Evergreen foliage
  • Stabilizes muddy and sandy shorelines
  • Supports coastal ecosystems

Common Uses

  • Coastal erosion control
  • Shoreline stabilization
  • Wetland restoration
  • Wildlife habitat support
  • Environmental conservation projects
