Grey Mangrove Hybrid
A crossbred Grey Mangrove form developed for enhanced resilience and adaptability in coastal ecosystems.

The Grey Mangrove Hybrid is a cultivated or naturally occurring cross between Avicennia Marina varieties, selected for improved tolerance to salinity, flooding, and sediment changes. It is commonly used in coastal restoration due to its strong establishment rate and ecological benefits.

This hybrid retains the key features of Grey Mangroves, including pneumatophore root systems that allow survival in oxygen-poor soils.

Characteristics of Grey Mangrove Trees

  • Pneumatophore breathing roots

  • Evergreen coastal species
  • Strong shoreline stabilization ability
  • Supports diverse marine ecosystems
