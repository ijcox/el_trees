Indo-Pacific Grey Mangrove
A widespread Grey Mangrove form native to Indo-Pacific coastal regions, known for extreme environmental tolerance.

The Indo-Pacific Grey Mangrove is a major regional form of Avicennia marina found across tropical and subtropical coastlines. It thrives in harsh intertidal zones where salinity, temperature, and water levels frequently fluctuate.

This mangrove is a key species in coastal ecosystems, helping stabilize shorelines, trap sediments, and support rich biodiversity.

Characteristics of Grey Mangrove Trees

  • Extreme salt and drought tolerance
  • Pneumatophore root system for oxygen intake
  • Thrives in tidal mudflats and estuaries
  • Evergreen coastal species
  • Supports shoreline stability
  • Critical habitat for coastal wildlife

Common Uses

  • Coastal protection and stabilization
  • Wetland restoration projects
  • Marine habitat conservation
  • Storm surge mitigation
  • Environmental rehabilitation efforts
