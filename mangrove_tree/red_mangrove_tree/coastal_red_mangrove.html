Coastal Red Mangrove
A shoreline-dominant form of Red Mangrove that thrives directly along coastlines and protects against erosion.

The Coastal Red Mangrove is specifically adapted to grow along exposed shorelines, where it plays a vital role in stabilizing coastlines and reducing wave impact. Its dense network of prop roots traps sediment and builds up coastal land over time.

This type of Red Mangrove is commonly found in tropical coastal zones, forming natural barriers that protect inland ecosystems from storms and tidal surges.

Characteristics of Red Mangrove Trees

  • Extensive prop roots for shoreline stability
  • Highly tolerant of saltwater exposure
  • Thrives in coastal and tidal environments
  • Evergreen foliage year-round
  • Builds and maintains coastal landforms
  • Acts as a natural barrier against waves

Common Uses

  • Coastal erosion prevention
  • Storm surge protection
  • Habitat for marine and bird species
  • Shoreline restoration projects
  • Environmental conservation efforts
