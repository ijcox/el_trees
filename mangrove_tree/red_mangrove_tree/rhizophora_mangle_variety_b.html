Rhizophora Mangle Variety B
A resilient variation of the Red Mangrove adapted to dynamic coastal conditions and fluctuating tides.

Rhizophora Mangle Variety B is another form of the Red Mangrove tree, known for its adaptability to changing coastal environments. It thrives in areas with varying salinity levels and tidal movements, making it highly versatile within mangrove ecosystems.

Like other Red Mangrove types, it features strong prop roots that anchor it securely in soft sediments while supporting marine biodiversity. This variety is often found in estuaries and protected coastal zones.
