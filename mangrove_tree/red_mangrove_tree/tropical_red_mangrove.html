Tropical Red Mangrove
A warm-climate thriving Red Mangrove type commonly found in tropical regions with high humidity and consistent temperatures.

The Tropical Red Mangrove is widely distributed throughout equatorial and tropical coastal regions. It flourishes in warm climates where tidal waters and saline conditions dominate the environment.

This mangrove type plays a critical role in maintaining coastal ecosystems, supporting marine biodiversity, and protecting shorelines from erosion and storm damage.

Characteristics of Red Mangrove Trees

  • Thrives in warm tropical climates
  • Salt-tolerant and flood-resistant
  • Develops strong prop root systems
  • Evergreen foliage year-round
  • Supports diverse coastal ecosystems
  • Adapts to tidal fluctuations

Common Uses

  • Coastal protection and restoration
  • Marine habitat conservation
  • Storm surge mitigation
  • Shoreline stabilization
  • Environmental sustainability projects
