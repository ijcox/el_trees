Red Mangrove Hybrid
A crossbred form of Red Mangrove combining traits for enhanced resilience and environmental adaptability.

The Red Mangrove Hybrid represents a cultivated or naturally occurring cross between Red Mangrove varieties, designed or evolved to improve growth performance and environmental tolerance. These hybrids often exhibit stronger root systems and increased adaptability to a wider range of coastal conditions.

They maintain the signature prop roots of Red Mangroves while sometimes displaying faster growth rates or improved resistance to environmental stressors such as salinity fluctuations and sediment changes.

Characteristics of Red Mangrove Trees

  • Hybrid vigor with enhanced growth potential
  • Strong and extensive prop root systems
  • Improved tolerance to environmental stress
  • Evergreen coastal species
  • Supports biodiversity in coastal habitats
  • Adaptable to varying tidal conditions

Common Uses

  • Coastal restoration projects
  • Reinforcing shoreline protection
  • Habitat enhancement for marine species
  • Experimental and ecological planting
  • Environmental sustainability initiatives
