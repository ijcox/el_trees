Rhizophora Mangle Variety A
A coastal-adapted form of the Red Mangrove known for its strong prop roots and ability to thrive in tidal environments.

Rhizophora Mangle Variety A is a specialized form of the Red Mangrove tree that grows along tropical and subtropical coastlines. It is particularly recognized for its arching prop roots, which stabilize the tree in muddy, oxygen-poor soils and help protect shorelines from erosion.

This variety plays an important ecological role by providing habitat for marine life, birds, and other coastal organisms. Its salt tolerance and resilience make it a key species in mangrove ecosystems.
