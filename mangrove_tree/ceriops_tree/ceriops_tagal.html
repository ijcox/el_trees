Ceriops Tagal
A salt-tolerant mangrove species commonly found in Indo-Pacific coastal forests.

Ceriops Tagal is a mangrove tree that grows in intertidal zones, often behind the outer mangrove fringe. It is well adapted to saline and waterlogged soils and plays an important role in stabilizing coastal ecosystems.

This species contributes to sediment binding, shoreline protection, and supports biodiversity in mangrove forests.
