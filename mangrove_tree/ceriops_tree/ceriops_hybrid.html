Ceriops Hybrid
A cultivated hybrid mangrove developed for improved resilience and coastal adaptability.

Ceriops Hybrid is a crossbred form of Ceriops species designed to enhance growth performance, salt tolerance, and survival in harsh coastal environments. It is often used in restoration projects where rapid establishment is important.

This hybrid maintains the structural and ecological traits of Ceriops mangroves, contributing to shoreline stabilization and ecosystem recovery.
