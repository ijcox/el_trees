Ceriops Decandra
A mangrove species adapted to saline coastal wetlands and sheltered intertidal zones.

Ceriops Decandra is a mangrove tree found in tropical coastal regions, often growing in the mid to upper intertidal zones. It is highly tolerant of salt stress and waterlogged soils, allowing it to thrive where many other plants cannot survive.

This species contributes to coastal ecosystem stability by helping bind soil, reduce erosion, and support mangrove biodiversity.
