Rhizophora Mangle Tree
A classic red mangrove species known for its iconic prop roots and critical role in coastal ecosystems.

Rhizophora Mangle, commonly known as the Red Mangrove, is one of the most important mangrove species in tropical and subtropical coastal regions. It is easily recognized by its arching prop roots that provide stability in soft, waterlogged soils.

This species plays a vital role in protecting shorelines from erosion, buffering storm surge impacts, and creating nursery habitats for marine life.

Characteristics of Rhizophora Trees

  • Evergreen mangrove species
  • Stabilizes shoreline sediments
  • Supports marine ecosystems
