Rhizophora Mucronata
A robust mangrove species commonly found in tropical and subtropical tidal forests.

Rhizophora Mucronata is a widely distributed mangrove tree known for its strong prop roots and tall growth habit. It thrives in deep muddy soils and is highly adapted to saline, waterlogged coastal environments.

This species plays a key role in shoreline stabilization, sediment trapping, and supporting rich coastal ecosystems.
