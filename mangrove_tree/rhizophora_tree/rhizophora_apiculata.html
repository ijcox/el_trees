Rhizophora Apiculata
A strong-stemmed mangrove species widely distributed across Indo-Pacific coastal regions.

Rhizophora Apiculata is a major mangrove species found in tropical estuaries and tidal forests. It is known for its tall growth form, dense prop roots, and strong ability to stabilize muddy coastal soils.

This species plays an essential role in coastal protection, sediment accumulation, and providing nursery habitats for marine organisms.

Characteristics of Rhizophora Trees

  • Dense prop root system
  • High salt tolerance
  • Thrives in tidal mudflats
  • Evergreen coastal mangrove
  • Strong shoreline stabilization ability
  • Supports marine biodiversity

Common Uses

  • Coastal erosion control
  • Wetland restoration
  • Storm surge protection
  • Habitat support for fish and birds
  • Environmental conservation projects
