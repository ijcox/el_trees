Rhizophora Hybrid
A cultivated hybrid mangrove developed for improved resilience and adaptability in coastal ecosystems.

The Rhizophora Hybrid is a crossbred form of Rhizophora species selected for enhanced growth, salt tolerance, and environmental adaptability. It is commonly used in coastal restoration and reforestation projects where rapid establishment is important.

This hybrid maintains the signature prop root system of Rhizophora trees, helping stabilize shorelines and support marine habitats.

Characteristics of Rhizophora Trees

  • Strong prop root structure
  • High salt and flood tolerance
  • Rapid coastal establishment
  • Evergreen mangrove species
  • Stabilizes shoreline sediments
  • Supports marine biodiversity

Common Uses

  • Coastal restoration projects
  • Erosion control
  • Wetland rehabilitation
  • Storm surge protection
  • Habitat enhancement
