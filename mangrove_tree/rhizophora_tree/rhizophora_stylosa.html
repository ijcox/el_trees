Rhizophora Stylosa
A coastal mangrove species adapted to sandy shorelines and reef-associated environments.

Rhizophora Stylosa is a mangrove tree commonly found in coastal areas of the Indo-Pacific region. It is particularly adapted to sandy substrates and coral-associated shorelines, where it forms dense coastal stands.

This species contributes to shoreline protection, sediment stabilization, and provides habitat for marine and bird life.
