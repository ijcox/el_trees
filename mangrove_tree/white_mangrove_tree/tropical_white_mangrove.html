Tropical White Mangrove
A warm-climate White Mangrove form that thrives in tropical coastal wetlands with high humidity and stable temperatures.

The Tropical White Mangrove is commonly found in equatorial coastal regions where it grows in tidal flats, estuaries, and protected shorelines. It is well adapted to warm environments and fluctuating salinity levels typical of tropical ecosystems.

This species contributes to coastal stability by binding sediments and supporting diverse wetland habitats, playing an important ecological role in tropical shorelines.
