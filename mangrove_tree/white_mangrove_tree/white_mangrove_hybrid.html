White Mangrove Hybrid
A crossbred White Mangrove form combining traits for improved resilience and coastal adaptability.

The White Mangrove Hybrid is a naturally occurring or cultivated cross between White Mangrove varieties, selected for improved tolerance to salinity, flooding, and soil variation. It is commonly used in coastal restoration projects due to its adaptability and strong root development.

This hybrid maintains the characteristic salt-excreting leaves and stabilizing root systems of White Mangroves, helping protect fragile coastal ecosystems.

Characteristics of White Mangrove Trees

  • Moderate salt tolerance with adaptive traits
  • Thrives in coastal and estuarine environments
  • Evergreen foliage with salt-excreting leaves
  • Strong shoreline stabilization ability
  • Supports diverse wetland ecosystems
  • Improved resilience through hybridization

Common Uses

  • Coastal restoration projects
  • Wetland rehabilitation
  • Shoreline erosion control
  • Habitat enhancement
  • Environmental conservation efforts
