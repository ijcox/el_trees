Laguncularia Racemosa Variety A
A salt-tolerant White Mangrove variety commonly found in coastal wetlands and tidal zones.

Laguncularia Racemosa Variety A is a form of the White Mangrove tree that thrives in coastal environments where freshwater and saltwater mix. It is known for its adaptability to high salinity and poor soil conditions, making it a key species in mangrove ecosystems.

This tree often grows alongside other mangrove species, contributing to shoreline stabilization and ecosystem diversity.

Characteristics of White Mangrove Trees

  • Moderate salt tolerance compared to other mangroves
  • Thrives in coastal and estuarine zones
  • Produces specialized salt-excreting leaves
  • Evergreen coastal species
  • Stabilizes sandy and muddy soils
  • Supports coastal biodiversity

Common Uses

  • Coastal restoration and protection
  • Wetland stabilization
  • Wildlife habitat support
  • Erosion control projects
  • Environmental conservation efforts
