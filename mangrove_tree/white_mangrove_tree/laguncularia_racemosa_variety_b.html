Laguncularia Racemosa Variety B
A resilient White Mangrove variation adapted to fluctuating tidal and salinity conditions.

Laguncularia Racemosa Variety B is a coastal White Mangrove form that thrives in dynamic wetland ecosystems. It is particularly well-suited for areas with changing water levels and variable salinity, such as estuaries and lagoons.

Like other White Mangroves, it contributes to shoreline stability and provides important habitat for coastal wildlife.
