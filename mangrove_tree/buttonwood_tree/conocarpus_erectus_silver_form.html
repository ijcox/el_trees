Conocarpus Erectus Silver Form
A silvery-leaved variety of Buttonwood known for its striking appearance and strong coastal tolerance.

Conocarpus Erectus Silver Form is a distinctive variant of the Buttonwood tree characterized by its silvery-green foliage. This adaptation helps reflect intense sunlight and reduces water loss, making it well-suited for hot, coastal environments.

It is commonly used in landscaping for its ornamental value as well as its ability to withstand salt spray, wind, and drought conditions.

Characteristics of Buttonwood Trees

  • Evergreen coastal species
  • High tolerance to salt and wind
  • Silvery foliage for sun reflection
  • Drought-resistant once established
  • Thrives in sandy coastal soils
  • Strong structural branching

Common Uses

  • Coastal landscaping
  • Windbreak planting
  • Erosion control
  • Urban greening projects
  • Ornamental shade tree use
