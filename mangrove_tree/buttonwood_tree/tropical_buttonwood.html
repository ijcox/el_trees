Tropical Buttonwood
A warm-climate Buttonwood variety that thrives in tropical coastal regions with high humidity and salt exposure.

The Tropical Buttonwood is commonly found in coastal tropical zones where it grows in sandy soils, limestone flats, and shoreline edges. It is highly adapted to heat, salt spray, and periodic drought conditions.

This tree plays an important role in coastal ecosystems by stabilizing soil and providing habitat for birds and small wildlife.

Characteristics of Buttonwood Trees

  • Thrives in tropical coastal climates
  • High salt and wind tolerance
  • Evergreen foliage
  • Strong drought resistance
  • Grows in sandy and rocky coastal soils
  • Helps stabilize shorelines

Common Uses

  • Coastal landscaping
  • Windbreaks and erosion control
  • Shoreline stabilization
  • Habitat restoration projects
  • Urban coastal planting
