Conocarpus Erectus Green Form
A dense, green-leaved form of Buttonwood known for its strong coastal resilience and adaptability.

Conocarpus Erectus Green Form is a natural variation of the Buttonwood tree commonly found in coastal and near-coastal environments. It is valued for its dense green foliage, tolerance to salt spray, and ability to thrive in sandy or rocky soils.

This form is widely used in coastal landscaping and environmental stabilization due to its hardiness and wind resistance.

Characteristics of Buttonwood Trees

  • Evergreen coastal tree
  • High salt and wind tolerance
  • Dense branching structure
  • Thrives in sandy and rocky soils
  • Strong erosion resistance
  • Adapted to tropical and subtropical climates

Common Uses

  • Coastal windbreaks
  • Shoreline stabilization
  • Urban and coastal landscaping
  • Erosion control projects
  • Habitat support for wildlife
