Buttonwood Hybrid
A cultivated hybrid form of Buttonwood developed for improved resilience, growth, and coastal adaptability.

The Buttonwood Hybrid is a crossbred form of Conocarpus Erectus varieties selected for enhanced tolerance to salt, wind, and drought conditions. It is commonly used in coastal restoration and urban landscaping due to its strength and adaptability.

This hybrid retains the dense branching and hardy nature of Buttonwood trees while often showing improved growth performance in challenging environments.

Characteristics of Buttonwood Trees

  • Hybrid vigor with improved resilience
  • High tolerance to salt and wind
  • Evergreen coastal species
  • Drought-resistant once established
  • Strong root and branch structure
  • Adapted to tropical and subtropical climates

Common Uses

  • Coastal restoration projects
  • Windbreak planting
  • Erosion control
  • Urban and coastal landscaping
  • Environmental stabilization efforts
