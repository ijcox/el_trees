Black Mangrove Hybrid
A crossbred form of Black Mangrove combining traits for enhanced adaptability and environmental resilience.

The Black Mangrove Hybrid is a naturally occurring or cultivated cross between Black Mangrove varieties, developed to improve survival and growth in challenging coastal environments. These hybrids often display increased tolerance to salinity changes and sediment variation.

They retain the defining pneumatophore root systems of Black Mangroves, allowing them to thrive in oxygen-poor soils while supporting coastal ecosystems.

Characteristics of Black Mangrove Trees

  • Hybrid vigor with improved resilience
  • Pneumatophores for oxygen absorption
  • High tolerance to salinity fluctuations
  • Evergreen coastal species
  • Supports marine and bird life
  • Adaptable to diverse wetland conditions

Common Uses

  • Shoreline stabilization
  • Habitat enhancement
