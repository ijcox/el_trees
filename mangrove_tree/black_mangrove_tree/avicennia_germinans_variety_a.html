Avicennia Germinans Variety A
A hardy form of the Black Mangrove known for its salt-filtering roots and adaptability to coastal wetlands.

Avicennia Germinans Variety A is a variation of the Black Mangrove tree commonly found in coastal and estuarine environments. It is well known for its unique pneumatophores—vertical root structures that protrude from the soil to help the tree breathe in oxygen-poor conditions.

This variety thrives in muddy, saline environments and plays an essential role in maintaining coastal ecosystems by stabilizing sediments and supporting marine life.
