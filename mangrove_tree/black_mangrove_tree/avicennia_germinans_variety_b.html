Avicennia Germinans Variety B
A resilient variation of Black Mangrove adapted to fluctuating salinity and tidal environments.

Avicennia Germinans Variety B is another form of the Black Mangrove that thrives in dynamic coastal ecosystems. It is particularly suited to areas where salinity and water levels change frequently, such as estuaries and tidal flats.

Like other Black Mangroves, it produces pneumatophores that allow it to survive in oxygen-poor soils. This variety contributes significantly to shoreline stability and ecological balance.
