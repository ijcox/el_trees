Tropical Black Mangrove
A warm-climate Black Mangrove variety that thrives in tropical coastal wetlands and saline environments.

The Tropical Black Mangrove is commonly found in equatorial and tropical regions, where high temperatures and saline conditions dominate. It plays a critical role in stabilizing coastal ecosystems and supporting diverse marine life.

This tree uses specialized pneumatophore roots to absorb oxygen in waterlogged soils, making it highly adapted to tidal environments and salt marshes.

Characteristics of Black Mangrove Trees

  • Thrives in warm tropical climates
  • Highly salt-tolerant
  • Produces pneumatophores for respiration
  • Evergreen coastal species
  • Supports marine and bird habitats
  • Adapts to tidal fluctuations

Common Uses

  • Coastal protection and restoration
  • Marine habitat conservation
  • Shoreline stabilization
  • Wetland rehabilitation
  • Environmental sustainability projects
