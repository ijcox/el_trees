Avicennia Marina Variety A
A highly salt-tolerant form of Grey Mangrove adapted to coastal and estuarine environments.

Avicennia Marina Variety A is a form of the Grey Mangrove found in intertidal zones across Indo-Pacific and surrounding coastal regions. It is well known for its ability to survive in extremely saline and oxygen-poor soils.

This variety plays an essential ecological role by stabilizing coastlines, trapping sediment, and supporting diverse wetland ecosystems.

Characteristics of Grey Mangrove Trees

  • Extreme salt tolerance
  • Pneumatophore root system for respiration
  • Thrives in tidal wetlands and mudflats
  • Evergreen coastal species
  • Stabilizes shoreline sediments
  • Supports marine biodiversity

Common Uses

  • Coastal erosion control
  • Wetland restoration
  • Habitat protection for wildlife
  • Storm surge buffering
  • Environmental conservation projects
