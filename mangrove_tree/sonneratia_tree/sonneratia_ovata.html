Sonneratia Ovata
A lesser-known mangrove species adapted to sheltered coastal wetlands and brackish environments.

Sonneratia Ovata is a mangrove tree found in select tropical coastal regions where water movement is calmer and sediment accumulation is high. It is adapted to grow in soft, nutrient-rich mud and contributes to the stability of wetland ecosystems.

This species supports coastal biodiversity by providing shelter and contributing to sediment trapping in mangrove forests.
