Sonneratia Caseolaris
A fast-growing mangrove species known for its edible fruit and ability to thrive in tidal coastal zones.

Sonneratia Caseolaris is a mangrove tree commonly found along river mouths, estuaries, and sheltered coastal waters. It is recognized for its round, buoyant fruit and its strong adaptation to brackish and saline environments.

This species contributes to coastal ecosystem health by stabilizing sediment, reducing erosion, and providing food and habitat for wildlife.
