Sonneratia Acida
A mangrove species known for its sour fruit and strong adaptation to saline coastal environments.

Sonneratia Acida is a coastal mangrove tree found in tropical estuaries and tidal wetlands. It is notable for its acidic edible fruit and its ability to grow in highly saline, waterlogged soils.

This species contributes to shoreline stability and supports coastal ecosystems by trapping sediment and providing habitat for wildlife.
