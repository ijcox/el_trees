Sonneratia Apetala
A hardy mangrove species known for rapid colonization of coastal mudflats and disturbed wetland areas.

Sonneratia Apetala is a mangrove tree that thrives in tropical and subtropical coastal regions, especially in newly formed or disturbed mudflats. It is known for its fast growth and ability to stabilize loose sediments in dynamic tidal environments.

This species plays an important role in early-stage mangrove forest development and coastal ecosystem recovery.
