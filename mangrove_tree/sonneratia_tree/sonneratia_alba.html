Sonneratia Alba
A coastal mangrove species known for its large white flowers and strong tolerance to saline environments.

Sonneratia Alba is a mangrove tree commonly found in tropical coastal regions, especially in estuaries and tidal mudflats. It is well known for its distinctive white, brush-like flowers and its ability to thrive in oxygen-poor, salt-rich soils.

This species plays an important ecological role by stabilizing shorelines, improving sediment structure, and supporting marine biodiversity.
