Hardwood Tree
Hardwood trees are typically broadleaf trees that produce seeds enclosed in fruits or nuts. They are generally slower growing than softwoods and often produce dense, durable wood used in furniture, flooring, and fine woodworking.

Hardwood trees belong primarily to the group of flowering plants known as angiosperms. Many hardwood trees lose their leaves seasonally (deciduous), though some are evergreen in tropical climates.

Characteristics of Hardwood Trees

  • Usually broadleaf trees
  • Produce seeds enclosed in fruits or nuts
  • Generally slower growing than softwood trees
  • Often produce dense, durable wood
  • Many species lose their leaves seasonally

Common Uses

  • Furniture and cabinetry
  • Flooring and interior construction
  • Decorative woodworking and carving
  • Musical instruments
  • Tool handles and specialty wood products
  • High-quality veneers and paneling
