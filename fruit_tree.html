Fruit Tree
Fruit trees are trees that produce edible fruits consumed by humans and wildlife. They are widely cultivated in orchards and gardens around the world for food production and agriculture.

Fruit trees belong to many different botanical families and can grow in tropical, subtropical, and temperate climates. Some fruit trees are deciduous while others remain evergreen throughout the year.

Common Types of Fruit Trees

Apple Tree Pear Tree Cherry Tree Peach Tree Plum Tree Mango Tree Orange Tree Lemon Tree Lime Tree Coconut Tree Fig Tree Avocado Tree

Characteristics of Fruit Trees

  • Produce edible fruits containing seeds
  • Often cultivated in orchards or agricultural systems
  • Require pollination for fruit production
  • Can grow in many climates depending on species
  • Provide food for humans, animals, and ecosystems

Common Uses

  • Fresh fruit consumption
  • Food processing such as juices, jams, and preserves
  • Commercial agriculture and orchards
  • Home gardening and landscaping
  • Habitat and food sources for wildlife
