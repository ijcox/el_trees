Chinese Beech Tree
The Chinese Beech Tree (Fagus engleriana) is a deciduous tree recognized for its smooth gray bark and vibrant green leaves. It is native to central and eastern China and commonly found in temperate forests.

Chinese Beech trees can grow 20–35 meters tall with a broad, spreading canopy. They prefer moist, well-drained soils and thrive in temperate climates. The tree produces small beechnuts that provide food for wildlife, and its foliage offers excellent shade.

Characteristics of Chinese Beech Trees

  • Smooth gray bark that remains intact with age
  • Broad, dense canopy providing ample shade
  • Oval leaves with serrated edges
  • Produces small edible beechnuts
  • Moderate to fast growth rate
  • Native to central and eastern China

Common Uses

  • Ornamental and shade tree in parks and gardens
  • Timber for furniture and construction
  • Wildlife habitat and food source
  • Forest conservation and reforestation
  • Firewood and charcoal
