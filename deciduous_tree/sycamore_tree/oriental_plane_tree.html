Oriental Plane Tree
The Oriental Plane Tree (Platanus orientalis) is a large deciduous tree native to southeastern Europe and western Asia. It is admired for its longevity, broad canopy, and distinctive mottled bark.

Oriental Plane trees grow 20–35 meters tall with a wide, spreading crown. They prefer deep, moist soils and full sunlight. The leaves are large, lobed, and maple-like, while the bark peels in irregular patches exposing a pale, smooth trunk.

Characteristics of Oriental Plane Trees

  • Large, lobed, maple-like leaves
  • Wide, spreading crown
  • Mottled bark peeling to reveal smooth trunk
  • Fast-growing and long-lived
  • Thrives in deep, moist soils
  • Native to southeastern Europe and western Asia

Common Uses

  • Shade tree in parks and large gardens
  • Timber for furniture and veneer
  • Ornamental planting for visual appeal
  • Street and urban planting
  • Windbreaks and erosion control along rivers
