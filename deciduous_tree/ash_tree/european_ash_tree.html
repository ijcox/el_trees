European Ash Tree
The European Ash Tree (Fraxinus excelsior) is a large deciduous tree valued for its tall, straight trunk and hard, flexible wood. It is native to Europe and widely planted in forests, parks, and along streets.

European Ash trees grow 20–40 meters tall with a broad, rounded crown. They prefer well-drained, fertile soils and full sunlight. The tree produces winged seeds called samaras and supports diverse wildlife.

Characteristics of European Ash Trees

  • Compound leaves with 9–13 leaflets
  • Tall, straight trunk with rounded crown
  • Produces winged seeds (samaras)
  • Hard, flexible wood
  • Moderate to fast growth rate
  • Native to Europe

Common Uses

  • Timber for furniture, sports equipment, and flooring
  • Shade tree in parks and streets
  • Ornamental planting in gardens and landscapes
  • Wildlife habitat and food source
  • Firewood and charcoal
