Italian Poplar Tree
The Italian Poplar Tree (Populus nigra 'Italica') is a tall, narrow deciduous tree recognized for its columnar shape. It is native to Europe and widely planted for ornamental and windbreak purposes.

Italian Poplar trees typically grow 20–30 meters tall with a very narrow, columnar crown. They thrive in full sunlight and well-drained soils. The leaves are triangular or lance-shaped with finely serrated edges and bright green in color.

Characteristics of Italian Poplar Trees

  • Columnar, narrow crown
  • Triangular or lance-shaped, serrated leaves
  • Fast-growing and hardy
  • Produces fluffy seeds in spring
  • Tall, slender trunk
  • Native to Europe

Common Uses

  • Ornamental tree for streets and parks
  • Windbreaks and boundary plantings
  • Rapid shade tree in urban and rural landscapes
  • Timber for light construction and pulp
  • Wildlife habitat and food source
